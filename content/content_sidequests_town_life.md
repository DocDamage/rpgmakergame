# SIDEQUEST CHAINS

## TIER 1: ASHVEIL SANCTUARY (Level 1-15)

### Quest Chain: "The Salvager's Debt"

**Quest Giver:** Mira Thorn (Innkeeper, Ashveil Sanctuary)

#### Part 1: "Missing Scavengers"
**Level:** 5  
**Type:** Search & Rescue  
**Objective:** Find 3 missing scavengers in Ashveil outskirts  
**Rewards:** 500 XP, 200 G, Healing Potion ×3

**Journal Entry:**
- **Accepted:** "Mira at the inn is worried about three scavengers who went to check the old supply depot and never came back. The Dominion's presence has everyone on edge."
- **Active:** "I should search the outskirts near the supply depot. Mira mentioned they were looking for medical supplies."
- **Completion:** "Found the scavengers. Two made it back, but one... didn't. At least I got the medicine they found."

**Steps:**
1. Travel to Supply Depot (map marker)
2. Search depot (interaction point)
3. Find tracks leading to cave
4. Defeat Dominion patrol (combat)
5. Rescue survivors
6. Return to Mira

**Reactive Dialogue After Major Clears:**
- After D1: "You cleared the ruins? Maybe the scavengers can go back to work now."
- After D4: "The sky's wrong but at least the ground stopped shaking near the depot."

---

#### Part 2: "The Debt Collectors"
**Level:** 8  
**Prerequisite:** "Missing Scavengers" complete  
**Type:** Combat/Investigation  
**Objective:** Deal with debt collectors threatening Mira  
**Rewards:** 800 XP, 350 G, Ironhide Cloak

**Journal Entry:**
- **Accepted:** "Turns out Mira's husband owed money to the Ironhawk Syndicate before he died. Now they're collecting from her. I should see what they want."
- **Active:** "The Syndicate enforcer is at the east checkpoint. Maybe I can negotiate... or convince them to leave Mira alone."
- **Branch A (Combat):** "The Syndicate won't bother Mira anymore. Dead enforcers make poor collectors."
- **Branch B (Negotiate):** "Paid off the debt with Syndicate work. Mira's safe, but I owe them a favor now."
- **Branch C (Intimidate):** "Convinced the Syndicate that collecting from heroes is bad business. They'll find easier targets."

---

#### Part 3: "Family Heirloom"
**Level:** 12  
**Prerequisite:** "The Debt Collectors" complete  
**Type:** Recovery  
**Objective:** Recover Mira's husband's compass from D1  
**Rewards:** 1200 XP, 500 G, Compass Charm (+1 Navigation)

# QUEST JOURNAL TEXT STANDARDS

## Entry Structure

```
QUEST TITLE
[Difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆] [Type: Main/Side/Character]

---

ACCEPTED:
[2-3 sentences setting up context]

ACTIVE:
[Current objective with clarity]
- Step 1
- Step 2

HINT:
[Optional hint if player seems stuck]
"Try checking the supply depot again. Maybe you missed something."

COMPLETION:
[2-3 sentences wrapping up]

REWARDS:
- XP: [amount]
- Gil/Gold: [amount]
- Items: [list]
```

## Example: Full Journal Entry

```
THE SALVAGER'S DEBT (Part 1: Missing Scavengers)
[Difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆] [Type: Side Quest]

---

ACCEPTED:
Mira Thorn, the innkeeper at Ashveil Sanctuary, is worried sick about three scavengers who went to check the old supply depot west of town. They never came back, and with Dominion patrols increasing, she's fearing the worst.

ACTIVE:
Search the Ashveil outskirts for the missing scavengers. Mira mentioned they were specifically looking for medical supplies at the old depot.

Current steps:
- Travel to the Supply Depot (marked on map)
- Search the depot interior
- Follow any tracks or signs

HINT:
If you can't find tracks at the depot, try circling around back. The scavengers might have fled through the rear exit.

COMPLETION:
Found two survivors hiding in a nearby cave. They were ambushed by Dominion scouts. The third scavenger, Tomas, didn't make it. I recovered the medical supplies they found and returned them to Mira.

REWARDS:
- XP: 500
- Gold: 200
- Items: Healing Potion ×3
- Unlocks: "The Debt Collectors" (Part 2)
```
