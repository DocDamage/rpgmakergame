## FOUNDATIONS

### CODEX: MASS
**Unlock:** Visit D6 or defeat Mass-aligned enemy

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FOUNDATION OF MASS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Gravity. Weight. The anchor of existence.

Mass is not merely the heaviness of objects—it is the
attraction that binds matter, the force that creates
planets from dust and holds stars in their courses.

When the Foundation of Mass destabilized, the Obsidian
Quarry became a demonstration of what happens when
gravity forgets its rules. Rocks float. People fall
upward. The very planet risks tearing itself apart
as Mass fights to reassert dominance.

The Progenitors understood Mass as "the First Binding."
Without it, there is no ground to stand on—literally.

「The mountain does not move. The mountain does not
  break. But the mountain can forget why it stands.」
  —Grit, upon witnessing the Quarry

RELATED ENTRIES:
• The Eight Foundations
• The Obsidian Quarry Incident
• Progenitor Gravity Theory
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### CODEX: MOTION
**Unlock:** Visit D2 or defeat Motion-aligned enemy

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FOUNDATION OF MOTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Movement. Velocity. The eternal dance of position.

Motion is change itself—the difference between what was
and what is. It drives wind, propels waves, and animates
every living thing. Without Motion, time would have no
meaning, for nothing would ever be different.

The Fungal Depths demonstrate Motion's importance through
its absence. There, gravity shifts unpredictably because
Motion has become... confused. Things fall sideways.
Momentum accumulates without cause. The very air resists
movement, then suddenly accelerates it.

Progenitor texts describe Motion as "the Second Binding,"
the force that separates existence from stagnation.

「If Mass is the stage, Motion is the dance. One is
  meaningless without the other.」
  —Nix-7 analysis

RELATED ENTRIES:
• The Eight Foundations
• Fungal Depths Gravity Anomalies
• Progenitor Kinematics
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### CODEX: TIME
**Unlock:** Visit D7 or defeat Time-aligned enemy

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FOUNDATION OF TIME
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Duration. Sequence. The river that flows one way—usually.

Time is the most mysterious Foundation. Unlike Mass or
Motion, it cannot be directly observed, only measured
through their changes. The Progenitors treated Time with
special reverence, building the Frozen Citadel as both
sanctuary and study.

When Time destabilizes, causality itself becomes...
flexible. Effect may precede cause. Memories may form
before experiences. The Citadel's inhabitants exist in
pockets of frozen moments, neither alive nor dead, simply
...paused.

The Progenitors called Time "the Final Binding," the
Framework that gives everything else context.

「Time is not a river. It is a circuit. And someone is
  flipping switches.」
  —Sova's observation at the Citadel

RELATED ENTRIES:
• Elder Mordai
• The Frozen Citadel
• Progenitor Chronomancy
• Time Loop Theory
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## REGIONS

### CODEX: ASHVEIL REGION
**Unlock:** Arrive at Ashveil Sanctuary

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ASHVEIL REGION
The Shattered Frontier
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Once a prosperous mining region, Ashveil has become
synonymous with disaster. The Ruins of Ashveil—ancient
structures predating recorded history—have begun "growing,"
producing organic matter from stone through processes that
violate conventional biology.

The Dominion established Ashveil Sanctuary as a refugee
camp, but resources are scarce and tensions high. Most
residents are either too poor to leave or too stubborn
to abandon their homes.

Notable Locations:
• Ashveil Sanctuary (refugee settlement)
• Ruins of Ashveil (Growth corruption source)
• Supply Depot (abandoned)
• The Fellpath (dangerous route east)

「The ground remembers. The ground grows. The ground
  does not forgive.」
  —Local saying

DANGER LEVEL: HIGH
DOMINION PRESENCE: MODERATE
RECOMMENDED LEVEL: 1-15
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### CODEX: THE CAPITAL CHAIN
**Unlock:** Reach Crown District

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE CAPITAL CHAIN
Old Lumencrest → Crown District → The Spire
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The administrative heart of Dominion territory on Orion.
What began as a modest settlement (Old Lumencrest) expanded
through colonial ambition into a dense urban network,
ultimately culminating in the Crown District—the seat of
Triumvirate power.

The architecture tells the story of conquest: Old Lumencrest
shows practical frontier design, the Grand Boulevards display
Dominion propaganda in marble and bronze, and the Crown
District's brutalist towers literally overshadow everything.

The Spire—the tallest structure—houses the Triumvirate's
private chambers and, allegedly, direct communication with
the Progenitor Engine itself.

Notable Locations:
• Old Lumencrest Outer Wards (residential)
• Grand Boulevard (commercial)
• Archive District (restricted knowledge)
• Crown Hub (government)
• The Spire (Triumvirate seat)

DANGER LEVEL: EXTREME (Dominion High Security)
DOMINION PRESENCE: MAXIMUM
RECOMMENDED LEVEL: 120+
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## FACTIONS

### CODEX: THE DOMINION
**Unlock:** Encounter Dominion forces

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE DOMINION OF ORION
Administrative Authority
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

What began as a colonial administration has become, through
centuries of mission creep and emergency powers, a military
dictatorship controlling all human settlements on Orion.

STRUCTURE:
• Triumvirate (supreme authority)
  — Arbiter: Judicial/Religious authority
  — Hierophant: Military/Strategic authority  
  — Marshal: Law enforcement/Civilian authority
• Prefects (regional governors)
• Auditors (internal security)
• Legions (military forces)

IDEOLOGY:
The Dominion officially promotes "Ordered Development"—
the belief that Orion must be civilized according to
strict guidelines to prevent "recurrence" of some
historical disaster. In practice, this means total
control of resources, information, and movement.

OPPOSITION:
The party represents the most significant organized
resistance to Dominion rule in decades, though various
independent settlements maintain quiet non-compliance.

「The Dominion doesn't want to kill you. They want to
  file you. Bureaucracy is their true weapon.」
  —Twist

KNOWN MEMBERS:
• Marshal Nadia Korr (defected)
• Arbiter Lucien Marr (Tower F50)
• Hierophant Orin Vask (Tower F50)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### CODEX: THE AURORA FOUNDRY
**Unlock:** Enter Archive District

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE AURORA FOUNDRY
Cult of the Engine
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Not all Dominion citizens accept the Triumvirate's version
of truth. The Aurora Foundry—a secret society within the
Archive District—worships the Progenitor Engine as a god
and seeks to accelerate the "Reset" they believe will
create paradise.

BELIEFS:
• The Engine is divine judgment made manifest
• Current reality is corrupted beyond repair
• The Reset will purify Orion and its people
• Those who assist the Reset will be reborn in the new world

METHODS:
The Foundry operates through sabotage, spreading
Foundation destabilization, and infiltration of Dominion
institutions. They are the hidden hand behind several
"accidents" that accelerated the current crisis.

LEADERSHIP:
The Foundry is led by the "Seam Wardens"—mysterious
figures who claim to receive visions from the Engine.
Their true identities are unknown even to most members.

WARNING:
Foundry members are fanatics. They cannot be reasoned
with, only stopped.

「They think they're saving the world by ending it.
  Classic cult logic.」
  —Korr
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## Book: "The Eightfold Truth" (Religious text)
**Location:** Various sanctuaries, shrines

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
EXCERPT: "THE EIGHTFOLD TRUTH"
A Progenitor Catechism
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

...And the Progenitors looked upon the void and saw
that it was without form. And they spoke the Eight
Names, and reality answered.

MASS gave shape to the void.
MOTION gave change to the shape.
HEAT gave energy to the change.
TIDE gave flow to the energy.
GROWTH gave life to the flow.
LIGHT gave sight to the life.
SHADOW gave depth to the sight.
TIME gave meaning to all.

These are the Eight Foundations. They are not gods,
for gods demand worship. They are not laws, for laws
can be broken. They simply ARE—the framework upon
which existence rests.

When the Foundations are properly seated, Orion
flourishes. When they are misaligned, Orion sickens.

The duty of all who live is to maintain the Balance.
The reward for service is existence itself.

[Multiple translations and annotations in margins,
some contradicting the main text]
```
