# Party Character Reference (Canon-Aligned)

This reference anchors banter, combat barks, NPC interactions, and story reactions to the current Orion canon.

## Voice Rules
- Keep tone gritty and mission-focused; humor is pressure-release, not slapstick.
- Avoid out-of-world/meta jokes; references should stay in Orion context.
- Crush energy can exist as awkward teasing only; no explicit romance arcs.
- Korr content is phase-gated: hostile command presence before liberation, disciplined ally after Nix burns the tether.

## Character Profiles
### Kade (`kade`)
- Role: Gunblade lead and drake-born field captain
- Voice: Blunt, protective, practical under pressure.
- Core Concern: keeping the crew alive without becoming what he hates
- Arc: Learns to lead without surrendering compassion.
- Relationship Notes: Founding bond with Twist; protective of Nix; carries Vos scars.

### Nix-7 (`nix7`)
- Role: Lattice interface and tactical support android
- Voice: Precise, literal, quietly protective. Avoid contractions.
- Core Concern: remaining a person instead of a Dominion asset
- Arc: Builds identity and chooses the crew by her own will.
- Relationship Notes: Strong trust with Kade and Suresh; sync with Renna and Sova.

### Renna (`renna`)
- Role: Engineer, drone specialist, field improviser
- Voice: Dry technical sarcasm with hidden heart.
- Core Concern: keeping everyone functional when systems fail
- Arc: Stops using humor only as armor and trusts the team.
- Relationship Notes: Tech-sync with Nix; rivalry with Twist; respects Sova process.

### Dr. Suresh (`suresh`)
- Role: Combat medic and triage lead
- Voice: Clinical compassion with hard edges from guilt.
- Core Concern: atoning for harm done under Dominion authority
- Arc: Turns guilt into disciplined care and decisive action.
- Relationship Notes: Protective of Nix and younger members; moral anchor beside Vex.

### Twist (`twist`)
- Role: Rogue infiltrator and relic thief
- Voice: Fast, sly, emotionally guarded under humor.
- Core Concern: never being powerless again
- Arc: Learns trust without giving up her edge.
- Relationship Notes: Founding chaos with Kade; rivalry with Renna; loyalty to Petra.

### Dr. Sova (`sova`)
- Role: Black mage analyst and strategy lead
- Voice: Evidence-first, restrained, ruthlessly clear.
- Core Concern: using truth without excusing harm
- Arc: Chooses responsibility over detached certainty.
- Relationship Notes: Strategic alignment with Korr; technical cadence with Nix/Renna.

### Grit (`grit`)
- Role: Heavy vanguard and defensive anchor
- Voice: Laconic, grounded, fiercely protective.
- Core Concern: earning redemption through deeds
- Arc: Turns survivor guilt into dependable protection.
- Relationship Notes: Protective of Nix and Petra; mutual respect with Korr/Sova.

### Ashka (`ashka`)
- Role: Scout ranger and long-range execution specialist
- Voice: Quiet hunter focus with controlled grief.
- Core Concern: protecting life while hunting warped threats
- Arc: Finds purpose beyond vengeance.
- Relationship Notes: Field patience with Grit; kinship with Senna on trauma and resolve.

### Senna (`senna`)
- Role: Enhanced monk and anti-cult strike specialist
- Voice: Disciplined aggression, conviction-heavy.
- Core Concern: never letting the cult claim another child
- Arc: Channels fury into principled protection.
- Relationship Notes: Protective intensity with Ashka; tactical respect for Korr/Vex.

### Callum (`callum`)
- Role: Summoner and drake-touched caster
- Voice: Controlled sarcasm over old aristocratic wounds.
- Core Concern: refusing ownership
- Arc: Chooses found family over inherited status.
- Relationship Notes: Drake kinship with Kade; gentle trust with Nix and Vex.

### Petra (`petra`)
- Role: Mutation berserker and shock front
- Voice: Sparse, blunt, intentional.
- Core Concern: holding control over mutation
- Arc: Accepts power as protection, not shame.
- Relationship Notes: Understated trust with Grit and Vex; respects direct orders from Korr.

### Vex (`vex`)
- Role: Oath-broken templar and protective support
- Voice: Solemn, compact, oath-like.
- Core Concern: redeeming faith through protection rather than obedience
- Arc: Reforges belief around chosen duty.
- Relationship Notes: Moral resonance with Suresh; hard-earned respect with Korr/Senna.

### Korr (`korr`)
- Role: Marshal defector and tactical suppressor
- Voice: Disciplined command language with dry soldier humor.
- Core Concern: owning damage caused while tethered
- Arc: From conditioned enforcer to fully autonomous ally.
- Relationship Notes: Initially hunts the crew; later commands with accountability after tether burn.

## Story Anchors Used Across Files
- Act 1: relic hunt, terminal contact, Dominion pursuit.
- Catastrophe: Omega pedestal activation and World Break after Dungeon 4.
- Mid Act 2: Nix recalibrates a Prime terminal and burns Korr tether signal.
- Act 2+: Prime pedestal restoration campaign and anti-Triumvirate operations.
- Endgame: Final Palace and direct confrontation with the Progenitor Engine.

## Recurring Pair Dynamics
- Kade + Nix-7: balancing instinct with telemetry under pressure.
- Kade + Twist: founding trust built on bad odds.
- Kade + Korr: accountability after the World Break.
- Nix-7 + Renna: human-machine teamwork under fire.
- Nix-7 + Suresh: care versus reformatting fear.
- Sova + Korr: strategy and trust under fire.
- Grit + Petra: control when rage is useful.
- Ashka + Senna: survivor anger turned disciplined force.
- Callum + Vex: faith without ownership.
