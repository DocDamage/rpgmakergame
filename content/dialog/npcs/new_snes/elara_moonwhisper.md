# Elara "Star-Seer" Moonwhisper
## Star Ocean NPC - Uplands Elf Mystic

**Sprite:** `so_elf_mystic`  
**Location:** Uplands Hidden Grove  
**Role:** Mystic, nature seer, knows Sova's true nature  
**Schedule:** Dawn meditation, noon rituals, midnight stargazing

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Serene, mysterious, speaks in riddles  
**Depth:** Alien (not from this world), deeply homesick, kind beneath the mystery  
**Voice:** Musical, echoes slightly, uses celestial metaphors

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*An elf stands in a shaft of light that 
seems too perfect to be natural. 
Her eyes are silver, reflecting stars 
that aren't there.*

Elara: *doesn't turn around*
You walk loudly for someone with secrets, 
child of dust.

Kade: How did you-

Elara: *turns, and her smile is ancient*
The grove told me. 
The stones remember your footsteps. 
The wind carries your name.

*steps closer, studying Kade*
You seek the darkness. 
The one who extinguishes. 
*tilts head*
But you also seek the light. 
In yourself. In... *her*.

*looks past Kade to Sova if present*

Interesting. Very interesting.
```

### About the Stars
```
Elara: Your stars are wrong.
*points up*
That one. The blue-white. 
It should be elsewhere. 
Has been elsewhere... 
*calculates*
...for three centuries.

Kade: Stars don't move.

Elara: *smiles sadly*
Your stars don't. 
Mine did. Before I came here.

*sits on seemingly nothing - actually a fallen log covered in moss*

I am not of this world, child of dust. 
I fell. Through the thin places. 
The gaps between stars.

*quietly*
I have been trying to go home 
for four hundred years.
```

### Sova's Secret (if Sova in party)
```
Elara: *approaches Sova, silver eyes wide*
Sister-of-the-sand. 
You carry the desert in your soul. 
But not this desert. 
*reaches out, hesitant*
May I?

Sova: *nods, wary*

Elara: *touches Sova's forehead, gasps*
Oh. Oh, my. 
*withdraws hand, shaking*

You are OF the world but not FROM it. 
Like me. But different. 
The sand claimed you. 
Remade you. 

*to Kade*
She is more than she knows. 
More than she remembers.

Sova: *quietly*
What am I?

Elara: *kindly*
The desert's daughter. 
The storm's child. 
*smiles*
And someone's very good friend, 
if the way he watches you means anything.

*Sova blushes, Kade coughs*
```

### Party Reactions

**Nix-7 in Party:**
```
Elara: *circles Nix-7, fascinated*
Metal that thinks. 
Soul in circuits. 
*whispers to the machine*
Do you dream of stars, little one?

Nix-7: I... dream of service. 
Of purpose.

Elara: *sad smile*
We all do, at first. 
Then we dream of more. 
Freedom. Home. 
*touching Nix-7's face gently*
Love.

Nix-7: *quietly*
...Is that possible? For me?

Elara: *nodding*
Anything is possible. 
That's what makes existence terrifying. 
And wonderful.
```

**Renna in Party:**
```
Renna: Can you teach me? 
To read the stars?

Elara: *studies Renna*
You seek knowledge to save others. 
Good reason. 
But the stars speak in riddles. 
Truths wrapped in metaphors.

*hands over star chart*
Start with this one. 
The Hunter. 
It shows the way to lost things.

Renna: Lost things?

Elara: *meaningfully*
Or lost people. 
*looks at Kade*
Sisters, perhaps.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The Elf's Promise"

### Quest Start
```
Elara: I need a crystal. 
From the Prism Highlands. 
*embarrassed*
I cannot go myself. 
The crystals... they know I am outsider. 
They reject my touch.

But you. You are of this world. 
They will sing for you.

Bring me a singing crystal. 
And I will tell you something 
no living mortal knows.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Elara: *cradles crystal like a child*
It sings. It remembers. 
*listens intently*

*looks up, silver eyes glowing*
The promise kept. 
Here is your truth:

The Extinguisher can be saved. 
Not destroyed. Saved. 
There is a ritual. 
Ancient. Dangerous. 
*hands over star chart*
Written here, in the language of my people.

*serious*
But it requires someone who loves her. 
Truly loves her. 
Despite everything.

*quietly*
Do you know such a person?
```
