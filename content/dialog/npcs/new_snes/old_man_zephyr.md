# Old Man Zephyr
## Breath of Fire Elder - Dustbelt Storyteller

**Sprite:** `bof_villager_male_old`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Town Square - Bench under dead tree  
**Role:** Village storyteller, historian, secret quest giver  
**Schedule:** Always there from dawn to dusk, leaves at sundown

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Frail old man who tells rambling stories  
**Depth:** Last survivor of the first Dustbelt expansion, knows ancient secrets  
**Voice:** Soft, wavering, but becomes strong when telling stories

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*An old man sits on a bench beneath a dead tree. 
His eyes are milky but seem to see more than they should.*

Zephyr: *doesn't look up*
You walk like someone with somewhere to be.
Dangerous habit, that. 
The sand takes those in a hurry first.

Kade: The sand takes everyone eventually, doesn't it?

Zephyr: *small smile*
Smart. But wrong. 
*finally looks up*
Sand takes the unwary. The greedy. 
The ones who don't listen.

I listen. Been listening for eighty years.
Sit. I'll tell you about the oasis.

Kade: What oasis?

Zephyr: *chuckles*
Exactly.
```

### Stories (Random)
```
Zephyr: Before your grandfather's grandfather, 
this was green. Hard to believe, eh?
But I remember. The first expansion. 
The day the sand came.

Zephyr: People think the Extinguisher was a monster. 
Wrong. The Extinguisher was an answer. 
Terrible answer to a terrible question. 
What was the question? 
*shakes head*
Not for me to say. Not yet.

Zephyr: There's an oasis that moves. 
Follows the wind. The Lost Oasis, we called it. 
Find it, and you'll find... 
*trails off*
...well. You'll find something.

Zephyr: I had a daughter once. Beautiful. 
Brave. She went looking for the oasis. 
Never came back. Sometimes, at night, 
I think I hear her singing on the wind.

Zephyr: The sand remembers. Everything.
Every footstep, every death, every promise. 
When you walk on it, you're walking on history. 
Be respectful.
```

### About the Lost Oasis
```
Kade: Tell me more about this oasis.

Zephyr: *eyes sharpen*
Why? Looking for something? 
Or running from something?

Kade: Looking, I think.

Zephyr: *nods slowly*
Good answer. Running gets you dead. 
Looking... looking might get you answers.

The Lost Oasis appears to those who need it. 
Not want - NEED. To the desperate. 
The broken. The ones with nowhere else.

*pulls out ancient map fragment*
Last place I saw it. Twenty years ago. 
North-east, where the sand turns glass. 
But it moves. Always moves.

Bring me proof you've found it, 
and I'll tell you what I know about 
the Extinguisher's weakness.

Yes, old man knows things. 
Old man has seen things.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: You've seen battle, old timer. 
*points to scars on Zephyr's hands*
Those aren't farming injuries.

Zephyr: *chuckles*
Sharp eyes. Yes, I was soldier. 
Before. When there was something to fight FOR.

Korr: There's still something worth fighting for.

Zephyr: *studies Korr*
Maybe. Maybe you can remind me what.
```

**Renna in Party:**
```
Renna: Your stories... some of them sound like 
the texts I studied. Ancient history.

Zephyr: *interested*
You study the old ways? The old words?

Renna: I try to.

Zephyr: Then you know that history is written 
by survivors. And survivors... lie. 
To protect themselves. To protect others. 
To hide shameful things.

*quietly*
I know the true history. 
The shameful things. 
Some day, I'll tell someone.
```

**Sova in Party:**
```
Zephyr: *studies Sova intently*
You carry sand in your soul, child. 
Not from here. From somewhere... else.

Sova: I came from the south. From the expansion.

Zephyr: No. 
*firm for once*
You came FROM the sand. 
Different thing entirely.

*softens*
But you don't know that yet. 
You'll learn. The sand teaches, 
even when we don't want to learn.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The Lost Oasis"

### Quest Start
```
Zephyr: Find the oasis. Bring me proof.
A flower from its water. Nothing less.
And I'll tell you secrets worth more than gold.
```

### During Quest
```
Zephyr: *when approached*
Still looking? Good. 
Most give up after the first day.
The sand tests patience.

Remember: it finds the needy, not the greedy. 
Go with an open heart, not grasping hands.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Zephyr: *takes the flower with trembling hands*
Real. It's real. 
*tears in eyes*
She's still there. My daughter. 
Protecting it. Being protected by it.

*composes himself*
A deal's a deal. You want to know 
how to hurt the Extinguisher?

*leans close*
It was human once. Remember that. 
Humans have weaknesses. Memories. 
Regrets. Love. Find what it loved, 
and you'll find how to end it.

*hands over ancient locket*
This was hers. Before she was... changed. 
Might help. Might not.

Either way, thank you. 
For giving an old man hope.
```

**Reward:** "Lady's Locket" (key item), Story revelation, EXP
