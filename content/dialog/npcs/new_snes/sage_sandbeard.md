# Sage Sandbeard
## Dragon Quest 3 NPC - Dustbelt Hermit

**Sprite:** `dq3_sage_bearded`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Hidden Cave (oasis edge)  
**Role:** Hermit, ancient wisdom keeper, former advisor to the king  
**Schedule:** Always there, meditates at dawn and dusk

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Classic wise old hermit, cryptic answers  
**Depth:** Knows he's dying, desperate to pass on knowledge  
**Voice:** Raspy but resonant, speaks slowly with weight

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*An ancient man sits by an underground pool. 
His beard is white as sand, reaching to the floor. 
He doesn't open his eyes as you enter.*

Sandbeard: Took you long enough.

Kade: You were expecting me?

Sandbeard: *opens one eye, milky with cataracts*
Expected someone. 
Hoped it would be you. 
*closes eye*
Sit. The water's cool. 
The world's hot. 
Makes for good contrast.

*waits for Kade to sit*

You're the child of the lost queen. 
The one they thought dead. 

*small smile*
I knew your mother. 
Advised her, once. 
Before I became... this.
```

### Ancient Wisdom
```
Sandbeard: You want to know how to win. 
Everyone does. 
But winning isn't the question.

Kade: Then what is?

Sandbeard: *opens both eyes*
What are you willing to lose?

*stands with surprising grace, 
walks to cave paintings*

Three heroes have faced the Extinguisher. 
Three have won. 
*points to paintings*
First lost her love. 
Second lost his kingdom. 
Third... 
*trails off*

Third became the next Extinguisher.

*turns to Kade*
What will YOU lose, child?
```

### About the King (Kade's father)
```
Sandbeard: Your father was... complicated. 
Good man. Weak king. 
Loved your mother truly. 
Couldn't save her.

*quietly*
None of us could.

The Extinguisher doesn't kill queens 
because she hates them. 
She kills them because she WAS one. 
*taps staff*
Jealousy from beyond reason.

*hands over signet ring*
Take this. 
Proof of who you are. 
When you reach the palace... 
some doors only open for blood.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: You were military advisor.

Sandbeard: *pleased*
Sharp eyes, soldier. 
Yes. Three wars. 
Zero victories worth the cost.

*studies Korr*
You carry that weight too. 
Battles that haunt sleep. 

*offers tea*
Drink. Listen. 
The dead forgive. 
Eventually.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Ancient Wisdom"

### Quest Start
```
Sandbeard: Three questions I have for you. 
Answer truly, and I give you what I know. 
Answer falsely, and leave empty.

First: Why do you fight?

[Choice: For justice / For revenge / To protect / I don't know]

*accepts any answer*

Second: What do you fear?

[Choice: Failure / Death / Becoming the enemy / Losing those I love]

*nods at any answer*

Third: Will you sacrifice yourself 
to save the world?

[Choice: Yes / No / I don't know]

*smiles at any answer*

Good. You answered truly. 
All answers are correct, 
if they are honest.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Sandbeard: *hands over ancient tome*
The Chronicle of Endings. 
Every time the world nearly died. 
How it was saved. 
The costs paid.

*serious*
Read it. Learn it. 
Then do something new.

*quietly*
The world doesn't need another dead hero. 
It needs a living one.

*coughs, blood on hand*
Hurry, child. 
I would see you succeed... 
before I go.
```

**Reward:** "Chronicle of Endings" (lore book), Royal Signet Ring (key item)
