# Oni "Flame-Head" Rage
## Bakumatsu Kourinden NPC - Ember Demon Mercenary

**Sprite:** `bko_oni_demon`  
**Location:** Ember - Mercenary Camp  
**Role:** Demon mercenary, actually honorable, fights for causes not coin  
**Schedule:** Trains fighters all day, tells stories at night

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Terrifying demon, roars a lot, seems bloodthirsty  
**Depth:** Secretly protective, fights for the oppressed, misses his home realm  
**Voice:** Booming, actually quite articulate, uses human idioms wrong

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*A massive red demon with flaming hair 
is arm-wrestling three men at once. 
He wins effortlessly.*

Oni: HA! 
*notices Kade*
YOU! 
*points giant finger*
Look strong! 
Want to arm-wrestle?

Kade: I...

Oni: No? SMART! 
*laughs, shaking the ground*
I am ONI! 
Demon of the Burning Mountain! 
Mercenary of Fortune! 
...Actually, mercenary of JUSTICE, 
but fortune pays better.

*proudly*
I fight for the little humans! 
The ones big humans step on!

*quietly*
Also, I am very good at stepping back.
```

### About Being a Demon
```
Oni: You look at me funny. 
*not unkindly*
Scared? Wise. 
Curious? Also wise.

*shrugs massive shoulders*
I am demon, yes. 
From below. Fire and darkness. 
*snorts flame*
But I got... what is word... 
BORED! Yes! Bored of evil.

*earnestly*
Evil is BORING. 
Same thing every day. 
Scream, burn, conquer. 
No VARIETY!

*grins, showing fangs*
But helping? Saving? 
Every day is NEW! 
New people! New problems! 
New ways to hit bad guys!

*happy sigh*
I love hitting bad guys.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: *hand on weapon, wary*
Demon.

Oni: *sad*
Always the same. 
*holds up hands*
I am friendly! 
See? No fire! 
...Okay, some fire, 
but FRIENDLY fire!

Korr: *doesn't relax*

Oni: *sighs*
I will prove. 
*calls over*
HEY! SMALL HUMAN! 
The one I saved from bandits yesterday!

*small child runs over, hugs Oni's leg*

See? GOOD with children! 
EVIL demons not good with children!

Korr: *slightly lowers guard*
...Point.
```

```

*embarrassed*

Demon contract. 
Very binding. Very serious.

To help human village. 
But I need... what is word... 
WITNESS! Yes! 
Someone to see contract is fair.

You look... what is word... 
HONEST! Yes! 
Help me?
```

### Quest Complete
```
Oni: *beaming*
CONTRACT SEALED! 
*shakes Kade's hand, nearly dislocating shoulder*

Village is safe! 
Bandits will NOT return! 
*flexes*
I explained VERY clearly.

*hands over demon coin*
Payment! 
Demon token! 
Burn it, I come help. 
Once. 
*serious*
For IMPORTANT things only. 
Not for... what is word... 
PRANKS! Yes.
```
