# Zyx-7 "The Curious"
## Star Ocean Alien Merchant - Dustbelt Strange Goods

**Sprite:** `so_alien_fuzzy` (small furry alien with large eyes)  
**Location:** Dustbelt - Tent on outskirts (strange glowing tent)  
**Role:** Alien merchant, sells exotic items, information broker  
**Schedule:** Random hours, sometimes "closed for dimensional calibration"

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Cheerful, curious, talks in slightly wrong idioms  
**Depth:** Homesick, worried about being stranded, desperate to go home  
**Voice:** High, musical, uses "human-isms" incorrectly

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*The tent glows with soft blue light. 
Inside, a small furry creature with six eyes 
bounces excitedly.*

Zyx-7: CUSTOMER! HELLO CUSTOMER! 
*scampers in circle*
I am Zyx-Seven of the Collective Curious, 
Traders of the Nine Systems, 
Currently Stranded and Open for Business!

Kade: ...You're an alien.

Zyx-7: *all six eyes widen*
YES! You know the word! 
Most humans say "demon" or "monster" 
or "kill it with fire."

*deflates slightly*
That last one was unpleasant.

But you! You seem NICE. 
Intelligent! Probably not a xenophobe!
Would you like to buy something exotic? 
Or sell something? Or just TALK? 
I miss talking. My translator learns 
from conversation!

[Choice: What do you sell? / How did you get here? / Are you dangerous?]

What do you sell:
Zyx-7: *produces items from seemingly nowhere*
Many things! Some from my ship, 
some from... elsewhere. 
*whispers*
Between places. The thin spots.

How did you get here:
Zyx-7: CRASH! 
*makes explosion gestures*
Ship said "landing error." 
I said "what is sand?" 
Ship said "imminent destruction." 
I said "OH NO."

Then: here! Stranded! 
For... *counts on many fingers*
...many of your "months."

Are you dangerous:
Zyx-7: *offended*
I am MERCHANT! 
Merchants are DANGEROUS only to WALLETS! 
*small laugh*
That is joke. I learn jokes.

I am small. Soft. Furry. 
My people are COLLECTORS, not WARRIORS. 
We find things. Trade things. 
Learn things. Very safe!
```

### Shop Menu
```
Zyx-7: What do you seek, friend-human?
I have things from FAR away. 
Some from THIS world but WRONG time. 
Some from places that don't EXIST anymore.

*items glow with strange light*

Careful with the blue one. 
It hums. Humming things are often trouble.

[SHOP MENU - Sells:
- Alien artifacts (+random stats)
- "Time-wrong" items (anachronistic gear)
- Information (rumors, locations)
- Strange consumables (effects unknown until used)]
```

### Random Conversations
```
Zyx-7: Your world is LOUD. 
So many SOUNDS. My world is quiet. 
We speak in colors mostly.

Zyx-7: I tried to eat a "cactus." 
*shudders*
Do not eat cactus. Very sharp. 
Very wet inside. Very regret.

Zyx-7: Your "sand" is fascinating! 
So many tiny rocks! 
On my world, sand is BIGGER. 
We have boulder beaches. Very relaxing. 
Very dangerous.

Zyx-7: I miss my bond-group. 
*small chirp*
They are probably worried. 
Or maybe they think I am DEAD. 
*thoughtful pause*
I hope they didn't divide my things yet.

Zyx-7: Do you think I am CUTE? 
Many humans say "cute" and try to touch. 
I am NOT pet! I am MERCHANT! 
...But I do like the ear scratches. 
*whispers*
Don't tell anyone.
```

### About Going Home
```
Kade: Any luck fixing your ship?

Zyx-7: *all six eyes dim*
Ship is... broken. Very broken. 
Need parts. Need power. 
Need someone who understands 
TERTIARY spatial compression.

Your world doesn't have that yet. 
Will have it in... *calculates*
...about four thousand years? 
Give or take?

*small chirp that sounds sad*
I am very stuck.

But! Being stuck means I can TRADE! 
And LEARN! And EAT interesting things! 
So it is not ALL bad.

*quietly*
But I do miss home. 
The color-singing. The boulder beaches. 
My bond-group.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Alien Artifacts"

### Quest Start
```
Zyx-7: I need things! 
Not for ME - for my SHIP! 
*produces list*

1. Power crystal (big one, very shiny)
2. Metallic ore (conductive, non-magnetic)
3. Biological sample (diverse, non-sapient)

If you bring these, I give you 
something WONDERFUL from my collection!
```

### During Quest
```
Zyx-7: Any luck with the SHOPPING? 
*does little excited dance*
I am EXCITED to see what you find!

[When items brought individually, 
gives hints about what else is needed]
```

### Quest Complete
```
Zyx-7: *takes items, examines with all six eyes*
YES! PERFECT! 
*scampers around, celebrating*

*produces a small device*
This is TRANSLATION MATRIX! 
Very rare! Very useful!

Put in ear, understand ANY language. 
Even ANIMALS! (Though they mostly say 
"food" and "danger" and "mate.")

Also: 
*produces small orb*
EMERGENCY TELEPORT! 
One use. Takes you to SAFE PLACE. 
Very useful when DYING!

Thank you, friend-human! 
You are BEST customer!
```

**Reward:** 
- "Universal Translator" accessory (understand all languages)
- "Emergency Teleport" item (escape any combat)
- Access to "alien tier" shop items
