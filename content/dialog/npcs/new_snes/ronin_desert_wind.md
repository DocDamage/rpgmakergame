# Ronin Desert-Wind
## Bakumatsu Kourinden NPC - Dustbelt Wandering Warrior

**Sprite:** `bko_samurai_honor`  
**Location:** Dustbelt - Travels, often at dojo edge  
**Role:** Dishonored samurai, seeking redemption through combat  
**Schedule:** Trains at dawn, wanders during day, meditates at dusk

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Silent, stern, disciplined to a fault  
**Depth:** Traumatized by failure, desperate to die honorably  
**Voice:** Sparse, measured, speaks in haiku when emotional

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*The warrior stands at the edge of town, 
staring at the horizon. 
He doesn't turn as you approach.*

Ronin: You carry a blade.
*finally turns*
Do you know how to use it?

Kade: Well enough.

Ronin: *narrows eyes*
"Well enough" gets farmers killed. 
"Well enough" gets villages burned.

*unsheathes slightly*
Show me.

[If Korr in party:]
*Ronin notices Korr's stance*
You. Soldier. 
You know this dance.

Korr: I've seen it.

Ronin: *nods*
Then you know: 
Blades demand truth. 
Lies shatter like poor steel.

*to Kade*
When you are ready for truth, 
find me.
```

### Sparring Offer
```
Ronin: I need to test a theory.

Kade: What theory?

Ronin: That I am still worthy.
*quietly*
That I can still protect.

*offers wooden sword*
Spar with me. 
Not to win. 
To learn.

*if accepted*
*combat tutorial with sword mechanics*

*after*
Good. You listen. 
Most don't. 
Most die.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: You were imperial guard.

Ronin: *stiffens*
...Yes.

Korr: I know the signs. 
The stance. The scars.

Ronin: *meets Korr's eyes*
You were something similar.

Korr: Once.

Ronin: *quietly*
Did you fail too?

Korr: Every day. 
But I keep trying.

Ronin: *almost smiles*
...Perhaps that is the answer.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The Master's Sword"

### Quest Start
```
Ronin: My master's blade. 
Lost when I... fell.

*hands over broken hilt*
Bandits took it. 
Broke it. 
Sold the pieces.

Find them. 
Three pieces. 
Restore it.

Then I can face him. 
Tell him... 
*tortured*
Tell him I failed, 
but I tried to be worthy.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Ronin: *holds reforged blade, weeping*
It is done.

*turns to Kade*
You have given me peace. 
Or the chance at it.

*offers sheath*
Take this. 
My master's teaching. 
Written for his successor.

*quietly*
I was never worthy to read it. 
Perhaps you are.

*walks toward the desert*
Time to face my shame.
Wish me... 
*pauses*
...wish me honorable death.
```

**Reward:** "Master's Teaching" (sword technique manual), Ronin's blessing
