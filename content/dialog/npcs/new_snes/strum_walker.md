# Strum "The Sand" Walker
## Tales of Phantasia NPC - Dustbelt Traveling Bard

**Sprite:** `top_bard_playing`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Saloon (evenings)  
**Role:** Bard, song-collector, knows lost history  
**Schedule:** Travels between towns, performs 6pm-midnight

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Charming, flirtatious, always performing  
**Depth:** Searching for a lost song that can change fate  
**Voice:** Musical even in speech, uses lyrical metaphors

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting (Saloon)
```
*The bard finishes a song to scattered applause. 
He spots Kade and grins.*

Strum: There! The hero enters! 
*stands on table*
Tall, dark, and looking for trouble! 

*gestures dramatically*
Come, friend! Sit! 
Let me play you the song of your people! 
*strums, makes discordant sound*
...Which I apparently don't know yet. 

*sits down, uninvited*
Strum Walker, at your service. 
Collector of songs, teller of tales, 
and - if the lady insists - 
stealer of hearts.

*winks at any female party member*

What brings you to the ass-end of nowhere, 
friend? Fortune? Glory? 
*leans in*
Lost causes?
```

### The Lost Song
```
Strum: You ever heard of the Song of Unmaking?

Kade: Can't say I have.

Strum: *serious for once*
Good. Means you're still sane.

*strums nervously*
It's a myth. Maybe. 
A song so powerful it can unmake reality. 
Rewrite history. Change... anything.

*quietly*
I think it's real. 
And I think it's the only way to stop 
what's coming.

*back to cheerful*
But hey! Enough doom! 
*plays upbeat tune*
Know any requests?
```

### Party Reactions

**Twist in Party:**
```
Strum: *spots Twist pocketing someone's wallet*
*whistles low*
Quick fingers. 
Ever think about music?

Twist: Music?

Strum: Same principle. 
Timing. Rhythm. 
Knowing exactly when to strike.

*demonstrates a complex chord*
See? Theft and music. 
Same skill, different application.

Twist: *actually interested*
...Teach me?

Strum: *grins*
First round's on you, and we have a deal.
```

**Renna in Party:**
```
Strum: *plays something haunting*
*notices Renna's reaction*
You know this song.

Renna: My mother... used to sing it.

Strum: *softly*
"The Mother's Lullaby." 
Old as the mountains. 
*stops playing*

You haven't heard it since.

Renna: *shakes head*

Strum: *resumes, gentler*
Then let me play it for you. 
Properly. 
*meets her eyes*
For her. And for you.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The Lost Song"

### Quest Start
```
Strum: I need a lyre string. 
Not just any - one from the singing reeds 
of the Mire. 

They only grow in one spot. 
Dangerous spot. 

*hands over map*
Bring me a string, 
and I'll teach you a song 
that opens locked doors. 
Physical and... metaphorical.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Strum: *strings the reed into his lyre*
Perfect pitch. 
*tunes it*

*plays a chord that makes the air shimmer*
Here. The Doorkeeper's Song. 
Plays at the right frequency 
to vibrate locks open.

*serious*
And the other thing I promised. 
*hands over sheet music*

"Aurora's Lament." 
The Extinguisher's true name 
was Aurora. 
This was her favorite song.

*quietly*
Play it when you face her. 
Might remind her... 
of who she was.
```

**Reward:** "Doorkeeper's Song" ability, "Aurora's Lament" (key item for final battle)
