# Sister Stoneheart
## Breath of Fire Priestess - Uplands Temple Healer

**Sprite:** `bof_healer_priest`  
**Location:** Uplands High Temple - Healing Chambers  
**Role:** Temple healer, spiritual advisor, secret rebel  
**Schedule:** Dawn prayers, 8am-8pm healing, midnight meditation

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Serene, gentle, devoted healer  
**Depth:** Secretly smuggles refugees, fights corruption in the church  
**Voice:** Soft, musical, with steel underneath when angered

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*A woman in white robes moves between patients 
in the healing chamber. Her hands glow softly 
as she tends to wounds.*

Stoneheart: *without looking up*
If you're here to pray, the chapel is east. 
If you're here for healing, take a seat. 
If you're here to cause trouble...
*looks up, eyes sharp*
...the mountain is high and graves are deep.

Kade: Just looking for help. Information.

Stoneheart: *returns to patient*
Information is more expensive than healing. 
Healing costs coin or service. 
Information costs trust.

*finishes with patient, stands*
I'm Sister Stoneheart. Welcome to the High Temple.
We serve all who need - regardless of faith, 
fortune, or foolishness. Which are you?

[Choice: Faithful / Unfortunate / Foolish / Just passing through]

Faithful:
Stoneheart: *smiles genuinely*
Then you're welcome here, child. 
The stones remember all prayers.

Unfortunate:
Stoneheart: *sympathetic*
Aren't we all? Sit. Rest. 
No one leaves here hungry or hurt.

Foolish:
Stoneheart: *amused*
Honesty. I like that. 
Foolishness can be cured. 
Stupidity is permanent.

Passing through:
Stoneheart: *studies Kade*
No one just passes through the Uplands. 
The mountain calls or it doesn't. 
Which is it for you?
```

### Healing Services
```
Stoneheart: What ails you? Physical or spiritual?

[Choice: Physical wounds / Spiritual burden / Neither, just talking]

Physical:
Stoneheart: *examines injuries with gentle hands*
These are... recent. And violent. 
You're a fighter, then. Or a survivor. 
Sometimes the same thing.

*healing light flows*
There. The flesh is mended. 
The soul... that's your journey.

Spiritual:
Stoneheart: *sits across from Kade*
Tell me. Or don't. 
Sometimes silence heals more than words.

*long pause*
But if you want my counsel: 
guilt is a weight we choose to carry. 
Forgiveness is setting it down. 
Not forgetting. Just... not letting it crush you.

Talking:
Stoneheart: *settles in*
Words are free here. 
Speak what's on your heart.
```

### About the Temple
```
Stoneheart: This temple was built three centuries ago 
by pilgrims seeking sanctuary from the first expansion.

Kade: The Dustbelt expansion?

Stoneheart: Yes. Even then, the desert grew. 
Even then, people needed safe harbor.

*quietly*
We still provide it. To anyone who needs it.
Even those the world has... forgotten. 
Or wants to forget.

*meets Kade's eyes*
The church has many opinions about righteousness. 
I have only one: kindness is never wrong.
```

### Secret Side (If trusted)
```
Stoneheart: *checking that no one is listening*
You seem... trustworthy. 
Or at least not actively malicious.

*lowers voice*
The temple has a cellar. 
Not on any map. 

We... help people disappear. 
People who need to vanish. 
From debts, from enemies, 
from powers that would use them.

If you ever need to not exist for a while, 
come to me. No questions asked. 
No payment required.

*small smile*
The stones judge us by our compassion, 
not our orthodoxy.
```

### Party Reactions

**Renna in Party:**
```
Renna: Your healing technique... it's not standard.

Stoneheart: *approving*
You know healing arts? 

Renna: Some. I'm mostly self-taught.

Stoneheart: Then you learned by RESULT, not RULE. 
Best way. Rules are for those who can't feel 
the flow of life.

*offers hand*
Show me your method. I'll show you mine. 
We both learn.
```

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: You were soldier, once. 
*gestures to calluses*
Sword hand. Temple guard?

Stoneheart: *small smile*
Sharp eyes. Yes. Ten years. 
Then I realized I was better at healing wounds 
than causing them.

Korr: Not everyone makes that choice.

Stoneheart: *quietly*
Not everyone survives long enough TO choose. 
You have the look of someone who's still deciding.

*places hand on his arm*
When you're ready to stop fighting, 
there's peace here.
```

**Vex in Party:**
```
Stoneheart: *immediately steps forward*
You're hurt. Severely. 
*reaches for healing magic*

Vex: *flinches back*
Don't. Don't touch me.

Stoneheart: *pauses, understanding*
The pain is... familiar. Controlled.
Part of who you are.

*steps back respectfully*
When you're ready. Not before.
The offer stands.

Vex: *grudging*
...Thank you.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Healing Hands"

### Quest Start
```
Stoneheart: We're running low on medicinal herbs. 
The mountain variety that grows only in crevices.

If you're climbing anyway... 
would you gather some? 
Twenty sprigs would save lives.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Stoneheart: *accepts herbs with reverence*
Thank you. These will ease much suffering.

*presses small charm into Kade's hand*
Blessing of the Stone. 
Won't stop a sword, but it might steady your hand.
Or your heart.

And... if you ever need that cellar... 
say "the stones remember" to any temple acolyte. 
You'll be brought to me.
```
