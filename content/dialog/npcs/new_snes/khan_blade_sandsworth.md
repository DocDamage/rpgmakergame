# Khan "The Blade" Sandsworth
## Breath of Fire Merchant - Dustbelt Weaponsmith

**Sprite:** `bof_merchant_weapon`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Main Street - Sandsworth Steel  
**Role:** Weaponsmith, former arena fighter, secret softie  
**Schedule:** 8am-8pm daily, closed Sundays for "maintenance" (visits orphanage)

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Gruff, no-nonsense businessman who judges customers by their grip  
**Depth:** Secretly funds the Dustbelt orphanage, names every weapon after a child there  
**Voice:** Deep, slightly gravelly, uses weapon metaphors for everything

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
Khan: *doesn't look up from sharpening a blade*
You hold that sword like it's a dinner fork. 
Let me guess... first blade?

Kade: It's... served me well enough.

Khan: *finally looks up, eyes narrowing*
"Well enough" gets you killed in the Belt.
Name's Khan. I make weapons that make legends.
You wanna be a legend, or you wanna be a statistic?

[Choice: Legend / Just need something reliable / Just browsing]

Legend:
Khan: *grins, showing gold tooth*
Now THAT'S what I like to hear.
Come back when you've got coin and stories.
I'll make you something that sings.

Reliable:
Khan: *sighs, sets down blade*
Reliable. Boring word. Smart word.
Fine. Bottom shelf, third from left.
"Trusty" - plain name, plain blade, won't let you down.

Browsing:
Khan: *waves hand dismissively*
Window's for browsing. I got work.
Come back when your hands stop shaking.
```

### Shop Open (Morning)
```
Khan: Sandsworth Steel. If you can't kill with it, 
I don't sell it. What'll it be?

[SHOP MENU OPENS]
```

### Shop Open (Afternoon)
```
Khan: *wiping sweat from brow*
Heat's good for the metal. Bad for the temper.
What do you need?

[SHOP MENU OPENS]
```

### Random Banter
```
Khan: You know the difference between a good blade 
and a great one? About five years of practice.

Khan: Had a customer once wanted a sword that could 
cut through anything. Sold him a map to the quarry.
Some problems aren't solved with steel.

Khan: Names matter. My blade "Mercy" has ended 
more lives than "Reaper" ever will. Think about that.

Khan: Ironhawk boys tried to shake me down last week.
Still picking their teeth out of my doorway.

Khan: *sharpening a blade with rhythmic strokes*
Every weapon tells a story. Some are tragedies. 
Some are comedies. None are boring.
```

### Evening (Closing Time)
```
Khan: Sun's setting. Time to close up.
Come back tomorrow if you're still alive.

Kade: Big plans for the evening?

Khan: *pauses, expression softening slightly*
Maintenance. Always maintenance.
*quickly changes subject*
You buying or leaving?
```

### After Weapon Purchase
```
Khan: Treat her right. Clean the blood off, 
oil the leather, whisper sweet nothings.
She'll do the same for you.

Kade: Whisper sweet nothings?

Khan: *dead serious*
Every blade has a name. Every name has a soul.
You don't believe me? 
Wait 'til you're in the dark with only her for company.
You'll believe.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: *examining a blade with professional eye*
Good weight distribution. Balanced for thrust and cut.

Khan: *impressed*
You know steel. Military?

Korr: Something like that.

Khan: *nods respectfully*
Take 10% off anything. Soldiers' discount.
But only 'cause you know what you're looking at.
```

**Twist in Party:**
```
Twist: *fingers trailing over hilts*
Pretty. Very pretty. How much for the shiny one?

Khan: *grabs Twist's wrist*
Touch like you're counting coins, not checking for traps.
And "shiny one" is 500 crowns. Non-negotiable.

Twist: *winces but grins*
Worth every crown, I'm sure.

Khan: *releases, almost smiling*
You're trouble. I like trouble. 450.
```

**Renna in Party:**
```
Renna: Do you have anything... lighter? For someone 
who's never held a sword?

Khan: *looks her over critically*
Small hands, smart eyes. You'd be a dagger person.
*reaches under counter*
"Whisper" - twelve ounces, sharp as regret, 
balanced for the wrist, not the arm.

Renna: *accepts it, surprised by the weight*
It's... perfect.

Khan: *gruff*
Don't thank me. Thank my teacher. She was smaller 
than you and twice as deadly.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The Perfect Edge"

### Quest Start
```
Khan: You want something special? 
Something with YOUR name on it?

Kade: A custom blade?

Khan: Not just custom. Perfect. 
But perfection needs perfect materials.

I need three things:
1. Sand Worm Tooth - from the deep dunes
2. Moonlit Crystal - only found at night in the mine
3. Quicksand Essence - from the Mire border

Bring me those, and I'll make you a blade 
that'll outlast your legends.
```

### During Quest (1/3 items)
```
Khan: *counts on fingers*
One down, two to go. 
Don't rush. Good steel can't be rushed.
```

### During Quest (2/3 items)
```
Khan: *inspecting materials*
Worm tooth's good quality. Crystal's pure.
Just need the essence now.
Careful with that stuff - it'll swallow 
anything not tied down.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Khan: *eyes widen at the essence*
Ha! You actually got it. 
*carefully takes all three items*

Give me three days. No - don't come early. 
Don't come late. Exactly three days.

*three days later*

Khan: *presents the blade wrapped in cloth*
"Edge of Dust" - named for where you found her soul.

*unwrapped: a curved blade that seems to shimmer 
like heat waves, with three gems set in the hilt*

Worm tooth for the edge - never dulls.
Crystal for balance - always true.
Essence for the spirit - adapts to your hand.

First one's free. After that, you pay like everyone else.
```

**Reward:** Unique weapon "Edge of Dust" (scales with level)
