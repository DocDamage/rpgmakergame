# Professor Cliffside
## Breath of Fire Scholar - Uplands Historian

**Sprite:** `bof_scholar_wise`  
**Location:** Uplands Library/Archives  
**Role:** Historian, keeper of ancient records, knows the truth about the Extinguisher  
**Schedule:** Always reading, 9am-9pm in library, sleeps in archive basement

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Absent-minded professor, forgets to eat, buried in books  
**Depth:** Trauma survivor, watched the first Extinguishing, carries guilt  
**Voice:** Soft, distracted, becomes forceful when speaking of history

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*The old man is surrounded by books stacked 
to the ceiling. He doesn't look up as you enter.*

Cliffside: Third shelf, second row, 
"Common Plant Identification." 
If you're looking for the restroom, 
it's down the hall. 
If you're looking for the exit, 
it's wherever you came from.

Kade: I'm not looking for either.

Cliffside: *finally looks up*
Oh? Then you're either lost, 
a fellow scholar, or...
*studies Kade intently*
...someone seeking answers the world 
has forgotten.

*closes book with a snap*
I'm Professor Cliffside. 
I teach history that the world would 
rather forget. Which are you?

[Choice: Looking for answers / Just browsing / Tell me about the Extinguisher]

Answers:
Cliffside: *nods slowly*
Answers are dangerous. Questions are worse.
But sit. I'll tell you what I can.

Browsing:
Cliffside: *disappointed but hiding it*
The third shelf has light reading. 
Fiction. Mythology. Lies that feel good.

Extinguisher:
Cliffside: *freezes, book falling from hands*
*whispers*
Where did you hear that name?
*looks around nervously*
Not here. Too many ears. 
The back room. One hour.
```

### About History
```
Cliffside: History isn't dead. 
It's alive, watching, waiting for us 
to make the same mistakes.

Kade: That's... ominous.

Cliffside: *small smile*
Reality often is. 

*holds up ancient book*
This records the first Extinguishing. 
Not the one you're thinking of. 
The one before. And the one before that.

Kade: It's happened before?

Cliffside: Three times. 
Each time, someone rises to stop it. 
Each time, they succeed... 
but at cost.

*quietly*
The last hero became the next threat. 
As they all do, eventually.
```

### The Truth (if trusted)
```
*Back room, surrounded by restricted texts*

Cliffside: The Extinguisher wasn't always... that.
*opens locked case, removes portrait*

She was a hero. Like you. 
Fought the darkness before her. 
Saved the world. 
*bitter laugh*
World didn't save her back.

The corruption she fought... 
it didn't die. It changed. 
Merged with her. 

Now she IS the darkness. 
Trapped in an endless cycle of 
"saving" the world by destroying it.

*meets Kade's eyes*
You want to beat her? 
Don't fight her. 
Reach her. 
The person she was... 
is still in there. 
Somewhere.

*hands over portrait*
Her name was Aurora. 
Remember that when you face her.
```

### Party Reactions

**Renna in Party:**
```
Renna: *scanning books with hungry eyes*
These records... some are pre-Collapse.

Cliffside: *interested*
You know the old scripts?

Renna: I... studied. Before.

Cliffside: *slides book over*
Translate this. If you can.

Renna: *reads, pales*
This is... a prophecy. About now.

Cliffside: *nods grimly*
Prophecies are just history 
written before it happens. 
Let's discuss what it means... 
*glances at Kade*
...privately.
```

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: You were there. The first time.

Cliffside: *doesn't deny it*
...Yes.

Korr: You watched the Extinguisher rise.

Cliffside: *old pain in eyes*
I watched Aurora fall. 
I knew her. Trained with her. 
*voice breaks*
I should have stopped it. 
Should have seen the signs.

Korr: Survivor's guilt.

Cliffside: *laughs bitterly*
Survivor's privilege. 
The guilty get to remember. 
The innocent get to forget.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The First Sanctuary"

### Quest Start
```
Cliffside: There's a shrine. 
Older than this temple. 
Older than the mountains.

The first sanctuary, they called it. 
Where the first hero received their power.

I need to know if it still stands. 
What condition it's in. 
*hands over map fragment*

Go carefully. 
Some places don't like being remembered.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Cliffside: *trembling hands take the rubbings*
It stands. After all this time... 
it stands.

*reads ancient text, tears forming*
The blessing is still there. 
Dormant. Waiting.

*looks at Kade with new hope*
You could claim it. 
The hero's blessing. 
The same power Aurora had.

*serious*
But power changes people. 
Corrupts them. Remember that.
```

**Reward:** Access to "Hero's Blessing" power upgrade, Story revelation about Aurora
