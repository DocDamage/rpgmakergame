# Captain Sandstone
## Breath of Guard Captain - Dustbelt Town Guard

**Sprite:** `bof_guard_captain`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Main Gate / Patrols town  
**Role:** Town guard captain, secretly corrupt (Ironhawk), conflicted  
**Schedule:** 6am-10pm patrol, reports to secret location at midnight

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Stern, dutiful, protective of the town  
**Depth:** Secretly on Ironhawk payroll, increasingly guilty about it  
**Voice:** Commanding, gravelly, occasionally wavers when lying

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting (Day)
```
Sandstone: *blocks path with pike*
Halt. State your business in Dusthaven.

Kade: Just passing through.

Sandstone: *eyes narrow*
Everyone's "just passing through" until they're 
not. We had bandit trouble last week. 
Lost good people.

*steps closer, inspecting*
You don't look like bandit. 
Too well-equipped. 
*beat*
Mercenary? Bounty hunter?

Kade: Something like that.

Sandstone: *grunts*
Keep your weapons sheathed in town. 
Ironhawk runs the underground, 
but I run the streets. 
Don't make me regret letting you pass.

*steps aside*
Welcome to Dusthaven. Watch your back.
```

### First Meeting (Night)
```
Sandstone: *jumping slightly*
Didn't see you there. 
*tries to hide something - a coin pouch?*

Kade: Late for a patrol.

Sandstone: *defensive*
Captain's business. 
*too quickly*
Checking the perimeter. 
Making sure the gate is secure.

*forces normal tone*
You should be at the inn. 
Streets aren't safe after dark. 
For anyone.

*hurries away*
```

### Patrol Conversations
```
Sandstone: Keep moving. Nothing to see.

Sandstone: *if spoken to*
Problem? No? Then move along.

Sandstone: Had to break up three fights today. 
Desert makes people thirsty. 
Thirsty makes people stupid.

Sandstone: You again. Still not causing trouble? 
*almost impressed*
Maybe you're not completely useless.

Sandstone: Heard rumors about bandits east of here. 
Big operation. 
*glances away*
If I were you, I'd avoid that direction.
```

### The Secret (If followed at midnight)
```
*Sandstone meets with a hooded figure behind the saloon.*

Hooded Figure: *hands over pouch*
Information. Now.

Sandstone: *takes it, counts coins*
Merchant caravan. Three days. 
Eastern route. Six guards.

Hooded Figure: *nods*
Ironhawk appreciates your service.

Sandstone: *quietly*
When does it end? 
I've given you everything. 
Routes, schedules, vulnerabilities...

Hooded Figure: *coldly*
It ends when we say it ends. 
Or when you end. 
Your choice, Captain.

*figure leaves*

Sandstone: *alone, looking at coin pouch with disgust*
*notices Kade watching*
*freezes*

You... you saw.

[Choice: Who were they? / I'm turning you in / Your secret's safe]

Who:
Sandstone: *laughs bitterly*
Who do you think? Ironhawk. 
They own this town. Own ME.
Started with gambling debts. 
Now... *gestures helplessly*
Now I sell out the people I swore to protect.

Turn in:
Sandstone: *resigned*
Do it. Please. 
*surprising*
I'm tired. So tired.
Better a cell than this.

Safe:
Sandstone: *relieved but wary*
Why? What do you want?
*beat*
No, don't tell me. I don't want to know.
Just... stay out of my way.
```

### Redemption Path
```
*If player helped expose Ironhawk:*

Sandstone: *in stocks in town square*
*looks up as Kade approaches*
Come to gloat?

Kade: Come to ask why.

Sandstone: *long pause*
Because I was weak. Because I thought 
I could control it. Borrow from them, 
pay back, walk away.

*shakes head*
Nobody walks away from Ironhawk.

Kade: You could have told someone.

Sandstone: *meets Kade's eyes*
Who? Who in this town isn't owned by someone? 
The mayor? In their pocket. 
The merchants? Paying protection. 
The guards? Following my example.

*quietly*
I thought if I cooperated, 
I could limit the damage. 
Stupid. Arrogant.

*looks at hands*
I'm going to hang, aren't I?

[Choice: Probably / Maybe not / That depends on you]

Probably:
Sandstone: *nods, accepting*
Fair. Deserved. 
*small smile*
Tell them... tell the town I'm sorry. 
Won't help. But say it anyway.

Maybe:
Sandstone: *hope flickers*
How? I confessed everything. 
Treason. Corruption. Accessory to...
*trails off*
To things I can't even say.

Depends:
Sandstone: *interested*
What do you mean?

Kade: Help me take down Ironhawk. 
Properly. All the way.

Sandstone: *stares*
You want me to... help?
After everything?

*long pause*
*struggles against stocks*
Get me out of these. 
I know where the bodies are buried. 
Metaphorically. 
*darkly*
And literally.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: You're military. Or were.

Sandstone: Twenty years. 
Desert campaigns. Border wars. 

Korr: Then you know honor.

Sandstone: *flinches*
Knew. Past tense.
*meets Korr's eyes*
Don't be me, soldier. 
Don't think you can compromise with evil 
and stay clean. It doesn't work that way.

Korr: *quietly*
I'm learning that.

Sandstone: Learn faster than I did.
```

**Twist in Party:**
```
Twist: * sizing up Sandstone*
Ironhawk pay well?

Sandstone: *guarded*
Why? You looking for work?

Twist: Maybe. Maybe I already found it.
*meaningful look*

Sandstone: *understanding dawning*
You're... no. You're playing them?

Twist: *grins*
Let's say I take their money 
and give them... disappointment.

Sandstone: *actually laughs*
Oh, that's good. That's very good. 
Wish I'd thought of it.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Bandit Troubles"

### Quest Start
```
Sandstone: Bandits hitting caravans east of town. 
Lost three this month.

I should handle it myself, but... 
*conflicted*
...I'm spread thin. 

You look capable. 
Clear out their camp, 
I'll see you're compensated. Officially.
```

### Quest Complete (Normal)
```
Sandstone: *counts coins*
Standard rate. Nothing extra.
Town budget's tight.

*slips something extra anyway*
...Take this too. 
Found it on a bandit. 
"Not official."
```

### Quest Complete (Exposed Corruption)
```
Sandstone: *in chains, but cleaner somehow*
Thank you. For... everything.
The truth hurts, but lies were killing me.

*hands over ledger*
Names. Locations. Operations. 
Everything I know about Ironhawk.

Make it mean something.
```
