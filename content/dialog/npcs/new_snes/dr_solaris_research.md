# Dr. Solaris "Sun" Research
## Star Ocean Scientist - Dustbelt Weather Researcher

**Sprite:** `so_scientist_lab`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Observatory (small tower on edge of town)  
**Role:** Climate researcher, studying the expanding Dustbelt  
**Schedule:** Always working, sleeps irregularly, obsessed with data

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Absent-minded scientist, talks too fast, forgets social norms  
**Depth:** Deeply worried about the world, using science to save it  
**Voice:** Rapid-fire, technical, gets excited about data

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
Solaris: *looking through telescope, talking to himself*
The thermal expansion coefficient is off by 
point-three-seven percent, which would indicate 
an accelerated desertification pattern consistent 
with the theoretical models proposed by Dr. Vance 
in the Journal of Applied Climatology, volume forty-
seven, but that doesn't account for the anomalous 
readings from the eastern quadrant which suggest 
*takes breath*

Kade: ...Hello?

Solaris: *jumps, nearly falls off stool*
GAH! Don't sneak up on people! 
I could have knocked over the spectrometer!

*notices Kade*
Oh. Hello. You're... not with the Academy, are you?
No, too young. Too... armed. 

Kade: Just a traveler.

Solaris: *eyes light up*
Traveler! Perfect! Have you noticed weather patterns 
in your travels? Unusual heat? Extended droughts? 
Strange wind patterns?

[Choice: Yes / Not really / Who are you?]

Yes:
Solaris: *frantically grabs notebook*
TELL ME EVERYTHING. Locations, dates, 
specifics - do you remember cloud formations?

Not really:
Solaris: *deflates*
Typical. Nobody pays attention. 
The world could be ending and people would say 
"huh, feels a bit warm today."

Who are you:
Solaris: Dr. Solaris Research. 
No, that's not my given name. 
My parents had a sense of humor and I have a destiny.
I'm studying the expansion of the Dustbelt. 
Short version: we're all going to be sand soon.
```

### Observatory - Working
```
Solaris: *surrounded by papers, instruments, half-eaten food*
Do you know what the average temperature increase 
has been in this region over the last decade? 
Three-point-four degrees. That doesn't sound like 
much until you realize that thresholds for 
ecosystem collapse are non-linear and we've already 
crossed the first tipping point which means-

[Choice: Slow down / Keep listening / I have a question]

Slow down:
Solaris: *deep breath*
Right. Sorry. I don't get many visitors.
Short version: desert is growing. Fast. 
I need to find out why.

Keep listening:
Solaris: *excited*
-exponential feedback loops in the thermal dynamics 
which explains the correlation with the anomalous 
wind patterns but NOT the underlying causation which 
is why I hypothesize that something is ACCELERATING 
the natural process, possibly magical or technological 
in origin, and if I could just get readings from 
the deep desert I could-

Question:
Solaris: *waits expectantly*
Yes? Question? I love questions! 
Well, no, I hate them when I don't know the answer, 
but I LOVE them when I do!
```

### About the Research
```
Solaris: The Dustbelt isn't natural. 
Well, it IS natural, but it's not SUPPOSED to be 
this big, this fast. Something's pushing it.

Kade: Like what?

Solaris: *spreads maps covered in notes*
See this pattern? The expansion radiates from 
a central point. A point that corresponds to...
*lowers voice*
...the location of the First Extinguishing.

Kade: That's...

Solaris: Impossible? The Extinguisher was defeated. 
But what if they weren't? What if they're still 
out there, still... burning?
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Climate Anomaly"

### Quest Start
```
Solaris: I need data from the deep desert. 
The VERY deep desert. Where the sand turns glass.

Kade: Glass?

Solaris: Extreme heat. Magical heat, I think. 
If I'm right, there's a... signature. 
Something causing this.

I need you to deploy these sensors. 
*hands over 5 small devices*
Place them at these coordinates. 
They'll record for 24 hours, then retrieve them.

Dangerous? Yes. But necessary.
```

### During Quest
```
Solaris: *when visited*
Have you placed them all? 
The data won't mean anything without the full array!

[If not complete:]
Keep going! The easternmost point is the most critical!

[If complete, waiting:]
Just a few more hours... 
*checks watch obsessively*
The readings should start coming in soon...
```

### Quest Complete
```
Solaris: *tears open the data crystals, slots them into machine*
Come on, come on... 
*screen fills with data*

*face goes pale*
Oh no. Oh no no no.

Kade: What is it?

Solaris: *turns screen*
See this spike? That shouldn't exist. 
That's... that's active manipulation. 
Someone - something - is MAKING the desert grow.

And this pattern... *points*
It's moving. Growing. 
And if my calculations are correct, 
in approximately... six months, 
it reaches the first major city.

Kade: Which city?

Solaris: *grimly*
The Capital. Your Capital.
```
