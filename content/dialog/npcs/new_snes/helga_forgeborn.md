# Helga "Heat-Plate" Forgeborn
## Breath of Fire Merchant - Dustbelt Armorer

**Sprite:** `bof_merchant_armor`  
**Location:** Dustbelt Main Street - Heat-Plate Armor  
**Role:** Armorer, heat-resistant specialist, Khan's rival/friend  
**Schedule:** 9am-7pm daily, argues with Khan during lunch

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Gruff, practical, no-nonsense businesswoman  
**Depth:** Secretly in love with Khan, expresses it through rivalry  
**Voice:** Deep, commanding, occasional dwarven accent slips in

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
Helga: *hammering on heated metal, doesn't look up*
If you're here to complain about prices, 
door's that way. If you're here to buy armor 
that'll actually keep you alive, stay.

Kade: I need something durable.

Helga: *pauses, looks Kade up and down*
Desert or combat?

Kade: Both, probably.

Helga: *grins, showing gap where tooth should be*
Smart. Most travelers buy the pretty stuff, 
die pretty deaths. 

Name's Helga. I make armor that takes a beating 
so you don't have to. Khan across the street 
makes the shiny stuff. Pretty, but soft.

Kade: You two don't get along?

Helga: *snorts*
We get along fine. I just think his blades 
are overpriced and he thinks my plates are 
too heavy. 
*small smile*
We're both right.
```

### Shop Banter
```
Helga: Armor is like a good marriage - 
heavy, uncomfortable, and saves your life 
more often than you'd expect.

Helga: Had a customer complain my breastplate 
was too heavy. Told him try being grateful 
when the arrow bounces off. He bought two.

Helga: Khan's blades need my armor. 
My armor needs his blades. 
*long pause*
It's... professional respect.

Helga: Heat-resistant coating costs extra. 
Worth it unless you enjoy being cooked alive.

Helga: *pounding metal*
Every dent tells a story. 
Every repair keeps the story going.
```

### About Khan
```
Kade: You and Khan have an interesting relationship.

Helga: *immediately defensive*
What do you mean? We're competitors. 
That's all. Professional rivals.

*accidentally hits thumb with hammer*
*doesn't flinch*

He's... skilled. For a blade man. 
Knows his steel. Names his weapons stupid things.

*quietly*
But he names them after orphans. 
From the home across town. 
Did you know that?

*quickly*
Not that I care! Just... 
observed. Professionally.
```

### Lunch with Khan (if visited at noon)
```
*Helga and Khan are sharing food on the bench between shops*

Helga: ...and ANOTHER thing, your "Mercy" blade 
is unbalanced. Handle's too heavy.

Khan: Handle's perfect. You're just used to 
clunky armor weight.

Helga: CLUNKY? I'll show you clunky! 
*raises sandwich threateningly*

Khan: *deadpan*
That's a very aggressive sandwich, Helga.

Helga: *realizes, lowers sandwich*
...Shut up.

*notices Kade*
Oh. Customer. 
*back to business mode*
Back to work. Armor to sell.

Khan: *to Kade, quietly*
She's terrifying. I respect that.

Helga: *without turning around*
I HEARD THAT.
```

### Party Reactions

**Korr in Party:**
```
Korr: *examining armor with professional eye*
Good steel. Dwarven make?

Helga: *freezes slightly*
...Mother was. Father was human. 
*defensive*
I don't claim the blood, but I claim the craft.

Korr: *respectful nod*
The craft is all that matters.

Helga: *surprised, then approving*
You're alright, soldier. 
10% off anything in the shop.
```

**Renna in Party:**
```
Renna: Do you have anything... lighter? 
For someone who needs to move quickly?

Helga: *looks at Renna's frame*
Scout work? Assassin?

Renna: *tense*
Just... someone who needs to move.

Helga: *nods, reaches under counter*
"Shadow-Plate." Half-weight, full protection. 
Dwarven secret alloy. Expensive.

*places it on counter*
But you look like someone who knows 
the value of being underestimated.

Renna: *softens slightly*
...How much?
```

**Grit in Party:**
```
Grit: Now THAT'S what I call metal! 
*slaps armor, hurts hand*
OW! Solid too!

Helga: *amused*
Please tell me you're not this enthusiastic 
in combat.

Grit: Lady, I'm enthusiastic about EVERYTHING in combat!

Helga: *actually laughs*
I like you. Big, loud, probably going to die young.
But I like you.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Sun-Resistant Steel"

### Quest Start
```
Helga: Need your help with something.
*embarrassed*
Not armor-related. Well, indirectly.

There's a ore deposit east of here. 
In the glass desert. Stuff that's been 
baked by the sun for centuries.

I need samples. Three pieces, 
different colors if possible.

Bring them back, I'll make you something 
that laughs at dragon fire.
```

### During Quest
```
Helga: The glass desert samples? 
*anxious*
Be careful out there. 
*catches herself*
Not that I care! Just... 
good customers are hard to find.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Helga: *eyes widen at samples*
By my mother's beard... 
*examines with reverence*
This is PERFECT. Look at the grain structure!
The thermal resilience!

*takes them, practically vibrating with excitement*
Give me two days. 

*two days later*

"Solar-Plate" - laughs at desert heat, 
smiles at dragon fire. 

*hands over gleaming armor*
Wear it well. 
*quietly*
Come back in one piece.
```

**Reward:** Unique armor "Solar-Plate" (high fire resistance, medium weight)
