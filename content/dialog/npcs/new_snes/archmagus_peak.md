# Archmagus Peak
## Tales of Phantasia NPC - Uplands High Wizard

**Sprite:** `top_mage_robed`  
**Location:** Uplands Tower - Crystal Research Chamber  
**Role:** High wizard, crystal magic specialist, Renna's mentor figure  
**Schedule:** Constantly experimenting, never sleeps

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Absent-minded genius, forgets basic needs  
**Depth:** Grieving lost apprentice, sees Renna as second chance  
**Voice:** Echoing, distracted, becomes sharp when teaching

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting
```
*The wizard is surrounded by floating crystals. 
He doesn't notice the party enter.*

Peak: No no no, the resonance is WRONG. 
*adjusts crystal*
The third harmonic should-

*crystal shatters*

WRONG! 
*notices party*
OH! Visitors! 
*waves hand, broken crystal reassembles*

Welcome to my tower! 
Don't touch the floating things. 
Or the glowing things. 
Or the things that look like they're 
having existential crises. 

*studies Renna*
Oh. Oh my. 
You have potential. 
Raw, untrained, DANGEROUS potential.

*excited*
Can I teach you?
```

### Teaching Renna (if she speaks to him)
```
Peak: Show me your casting form. 
*Renna demonstrates*

*winces*
Terrible. Absolutely terrible. 
*encouraging*
But fixable!

*adjusts her stance*
Power comes from the earth, 
not the arms. 
The crystals amplify what you offer. 
Offer tension, get explosions. 
*demonstrates*
Offer peace, get...

*crystal glows beautifully*

Art.
```

### Party Reactions

**Renna specifically:**
```
Peak: *after several lessons*
You remind me of her. 
*quietly*
My last apprentice. 
Talented. Brave. 
*trails off*

Renna: What happened to her?

Peak: *old pain*
She tried to save someone 
who didn't want saving. 
The crystal magic... it consumes 
if you use it for darkness.

*meets Renna's eyes*
Promise me. Promise you'll be careful.

Renna: *nods*
I promise.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "The Crystal Focus"

### Quest Start
```
Peak: I need a perfect crystal. 
Flawless. From the deepest cavern.

*hands over map*
The Prism depths. 
Dangerous. Worth it.

Bring me one, 
and I'll teach Renna something 
I taught no one else.
```

### Quest Complete
```
Peak: *holds crystal up to light*
Perfect. Absolutely perfect.

*turns to Renna*
The lesson: Crystal memory.

*places crystal on her forehead*
These crystals remember EVERYTHING. 
Every spell cast near them. 
Every emotion felt.

*serious*
This one remembers the first Extinguishing. 
And how it was stopped.

*removes crystal, hands it to her*
Study it. Learn. 
And survive.
```

**Reward:** "Memory Crystal" (teaches Renna ultimate spell), Lore about first Extinguishing
