# Mirage "The Fetch" Runner
## Breath of Fire NPC - Dustbelt Courier

**Sprite:** `bof_villager_female_young`  
**Location:** Dustbelt - Travels between towns, stops at inn between runs  
**Role:** Courier, message carrier, knows all the shortcuts  
**Schedule:** Constantly moving, can be found at different locations based on day/time

---

## 🎭 PERSONALITY

**Surface:** Hyperactive, talks too fast, always in a hurry  
**Depth:** Desperately saving money to buy her sister's freedom from debt  
**Voice:** Rapid-fire, breathless, skips words when excited

---

## 💬 DIALOG

### First Meeting (at inn)
```
Mirage: *bursting through doors, covered in dust*
Package for... *checks scroll* ...Blacksmith Kellen! 
Package for Kellen! 

*spots Kellen, hands over package*
Sign here, here, and initial here... 
*while he signs*
...and if anyone asks, I totally didn't cut 
through the canyon even though it's faster 
because that's technically bandit territory 
but I didn't see any bandits well maybe one 
but he was sleeping so it doesn't count!

*takes signed scroll, spins around*
Now I need to get to Mirewatch by tomorrow 
which means I need to leave in five minutes 
which means I need to eat in two minutes 
which means-

*notices Kade*
OH! New face! Hi! I'm Mirage! 
I run messages and packages between towns! 
Fastest in the Belt! Guaranteed delivery 
or your money back which has never happened 
because I'm VERY fast and also VERY careful!

*shakes Kade's hand at super speed*
Nice to meet you bye I need to eat now!

*sits down, inhales food*
```

### Random Encounters (various locations)
```
Mirage: *running past*
Can't talk delivering urgent-
*stops, spins*
Wait you're that new hero person! 
Cool sword bye!

Mirage: *at a crossroads, studying map*
Left is safer but slower, right is faster 
but there's that rockslide, straight is 
NEW and therefore EXCITING but also UNKNOWN...
*decides in an instant*
STRAIGHT!
*dashes off*

Mirage: *catching breath at oasis*
Seventeen miles. Three hours. 
New personal best!

*sees Kade*
Oh hi! You should try running sometime! 
Great for the lungs! The legs! 
The sense of impending doom when bandits 
chase you!

*drinks water in one gulp*
Anyway bye!
```

### The Sister (if trusted)
```
Mirage: *uncharacteristically still, counting coins*
Two hundred... two twenty... 
*deflates*
Still not enough.

Kade: Saving for something?

Mirage: *tenses, then relaxes*
Might as well tell you. You'll hear anyway. 
Small towns, big mouths.

My sister. Lila. Got in debt with Ironhawk. 
Bad debt. They're... keeping her. 
Working it off, they say. 

*quietly*
Three years now. Debt never goes down. 
Funny how that works.

*determined*
I'm going to buy her freedom. 
Five hundred crowns. 
*counts again*
I'm at three twenty.

*brightens, back to full speed*
Which is why I need to GO because 
Mirewatch has an urgent package and 
urgent means EXTRA PAY and-

*stops, looks at Kade seriously*
Don't tell anyone. Please.
```

## 📜 MINI-QUEST: "Delivering Hope"

### Quest Start
```
Mirage: Okay okay okay I have a PROBLEM.

*paces rapidly*
Three packages. All urgent. 
All different directions. 
Can't clone myself. 
*looks at Kade*
...Can you clone yourself?

No? Darn.

Look, if I pay you, would you take the 
Dustbelt-to-Uplands run? It's the easiest. 
Relatively. Mostly. There's a bridge 
that's maybe unstable but probably fine!

Payment is... *calculates* ...half what I get!
Which isn't much but it's something!
```

### Quest Complete
```
Mirage: *counting coins excitedly*
Made it! All three! On time! 
*gives Kade share*

You're good at this! 
Ever consider courier work full time?

*serious for a moment*
Really though. Thanks. 
Every crown gets me closer to Lila.

*hands over small charm*
Luck charm. Been in my family for generations. 
Supposed to keep you safe on the road.

*quietly*
You're on the road a lot. Keep it.
```

**Reward:** "Runner's Charm" (+movement speed), Gold, Mirage as potential ally
