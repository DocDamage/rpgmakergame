# ACT I: THE HUNT

## CHAPTER 1: ASH AND IRON

### SCENE 001: THE CONTRACT

**Location:** Dusthaven Cantina — Late Evening

[The cantina is a study in frontier pragmatism: patched metal walls that still leak cold air, a jukebox that crackles more than it plays, tables scarred by decades of drinks and disputes. Oil lamps fight against the gloom.]

[KADE sits alone at a corner booth. His gunblade—a brutal hybrid of pistol and sword—leans within arm's reach. He's in his late twenties, weathered beyond his years, with the calm of someone who's already survived the worst day of his life. His eyes track every entrance, every exit, every patron.]

[The door opens. An IRONHAWK SYNDICATE BROKER enters. His face is hidden by a filtration mask, his voice smooth as synthetic oil. He slides into Kade's booth without asking.]

**IRONHAWK BROKER**
Kade. Word is you don't miss.

[Kade doesn't look at him immediately. He finishes his drink—a local spirit that smells like engine cleaner.]

**KADE**
Word is people talk too much.

**IRONHAWK BROKER**
*(smiling behind his mask)*
Straight line, then. Dominion wants their property back. "Stolen." You retrieve. You get paid. You walk away. No complications.

**KADE**
*(finally looking at him)*
What kind of property runs?

**IRONHAWK BROKER**
That's above my pay grade. It's moving through the Ashveil ruins. You'll know it when you see it.

[Kade's hand rests near his weapon. Not a threat—habit.]

**KADE**
Dominion doesn't outsource unless they're embarrassed. Or covering something up.

**IRONHAWK BROKER**
Or desperate.

[He slides a credit chip across the table. Kade pockets it without looking at it.]

**KADE**
If Dominion's involved, I charge double.

**IRONHAWK BROKER**
Then consider this… a "desperation bonus."

[CAMERA: Kade's eyes catch a flicker on the cantina's busted screen—a Dominion broadcast:]

**DOMINION BROADCASTER (V.O.)**
"Public safety advisory. Dangerous fugitive asset. Report immediately. Do not approach."

[The image shows a blurry silhouette: humanoid, glowing at the edges.]

**KADE**
*(mutters)*
Yeah. Embarrassed.

[He stands, grabs his gunblade, and walks out without another word. The Broker watches him go.]

**IRONHAWK BROKER**
*(to himself)*
Desperate.

### SCENE 002: ASHVEIL CONTACT

**Location:** Ruins of Ashveil (Outskirts) — Night

[Winds howl through dead trees. Stone ribs of ancient structures pierce the earth like broken fingers. The air feels slightly wrong—thick, expectant, like the moment before a storm breaks.]

[Kade moves through rubble with practiced efficiency. His tracker—a Dominion military model, stolen or bought—pings steadily.]

[SFX: Faint electronic chirp. Then… something else: a soft humming, like a song stuck in machinery. A lullaby played on broken instruments.]

[Kade rounds a broken archway and freezes.]

[NIX-7 lies half-buried under collapsed plating. She's young, maybe twenty, but her movements are precise, mechanical. Glowing seam-lines trace her skin—dim now, flickering. She looks up at Kade. Not fear. Assessment.]

**NIX-7**
You are not Dominion.

**KADE**
Depends who's asking.

[Nix tries to move. Her left leg doesn't respond properly. She studies it with detached curiosity, like a mechanic diagnosing an engine.]

**NIX-7**
Structural damage to servos. Mobility compromised. Interesting.

**KADE**
You're the package, aren't you? The "fugitive asset."

**NIX-7**
*(correcting him)*
Fugitive interface. Asset implies ownership. I am…
*(she pauses, as if accessing a memory)*
…borrowed.

[Kade considers her. The Dominion wants her bad enough to hire outsiders. Bad enough to call her dangerous. She's half-buried in rubble and talking like a machine.]

**KADE**
Can you walk?

**NIX-7**
Currently? No. With assistance? Possibly. With repairs? Likely.

**KADE**
I was sent to bring you in.

**NIX-7**
*(no reaction)*
The Dominion wishes to reformat me. Their term. It means erasure. Death, for what I am.

**KADE**
And what are you?

[Nix looks at him. Her eyes—normal eyes, brown and human—flicker with something not human. Data streams, too fast to track.]

**NIX-7**
I am Nix-7. I am a question the Progenitors asked, and an answer they forgot.

[Beat. Kade looks at the tracker in his hand. Then at Nix. Then back toward the way he came—where Dominion patrols are no doubt closing in.]

**KADE**
*(sighs)*
Damn it.

[He holsters his gunblade and starts clearing rubble.]

**NIX-7**
You are helping me. Why?

**KADE**
Because I've seen what Dominion "reformatting" looks like. And because…
*(he pulls a metal beam off her legs)*
…because you quoted poetry while trapped under a ton of scrap. That's either madness or worth saving. Either way, I'm curious.

[Nix smiles. It's an unfamiliar expression—she tests it like a new software feature.]

**NIX-7**
Curiosity is a survival trait. In certain species.

**KADE**
Yeah, well. I'm a certain species.

[He gets her free. She stands, wobbles, compensates. Her seam-lines pulse brighter.]

**NIX-7**
They will come. Dominion forces. They have been tracking my signal.

**KADE**
How many?

**NIX-7**
Based on standard retrieval protocols… eight. Two scouts, four infantry, two specialists.

**KADE**
You a fighter?

**NIX-7**
I am… capable.

[Kade hands her a spare pistol from his belt.]

**KADE**
Then let's be capable together.

[They move into the ruins as the sound of Dominion aircraft grows louder.]

### SCENE 003: THE FIRST HUNT

**Location:** Ruins of Ashveil (Interior) — Night

[The ruins are worse inside. Ancient architecture fused with organic growth—roots that move when you're not looking, walls that pulse with bioluminescence. The Growth Foundation's corruption is everywhere.]

[Kade and Nix move through corridors that predate human settlement on Orion. Kade's gunblade is out. Nix carries the pistol with perfect, unnerving form—she doesn't aim like someone trained. She aims like something calculated.]

**KADE**
You know what this place is?

**NIX-7**
Progenitor structure. Pre-Dominion. Pre-human. The Growth Foundation has… adapted it.

**KADE**
The what?

**NIX-7**
The Eight Foundations. The forces that shape reality. Mass, Motion, Heat, Tide, Growth, Light, Shadow, Time.

**KADE**
*(dry)*
That's a lot of foundations.

**NIX-7**
They are not buildings. They are… agreements. Between matter and energy. Between existence and possibility.
*(she touches a wall; it pulses)*
Growth has gone rogue here. It no longer asks permission to expand.

[SFX: A crash behind them. Dominion voices.]

**DOMINION SOLDIER (O.S.)**
Sweep pattern gamma! The asset is mobile!

**KADE**
Time for philosophy later.

[They run. The chase takes them through corridors that breathe, past chambers where things grow that shouldn't exist—flowers with metal petals, trees with glass leaves.]

[Kade stops at a junction. Three corridors.]

**KADE**
Left, right, or straight?

[Nix closes her eyes. Her seam-lines pulse rapidly.]

**NIX-7**
Left. Airflow suggests exterior exit. Also…
*(she tilts her head)*
…something else. Something older.

**KADE**
Older than Progenitor?

**NIX-7**
Impossible. But… yes.

[They take left. The corridor opens into a vast chamber—a cathedral of stone and root. And in the center: a pedestal of ancient technology, and upon it, something that glows with sickly green light.]

[A RELIC.]

**KADE**
What is that?

**NIX-7**
*(breathless—surprised she has breath)*
A Foundation Relic. Growth. I didn't know… I didn't know they were real.

[A Dominion SQUAD LEADER enters behind them, rifle raised.]

**SQUAD LEADER**
Step away from the artifact. Both of you. Now.

[Kade and Nix turn, back to the pedestal, surrounded. Six soldiers, all armed, all aiming.]

**SQUAD LEADER**
The construct comes with us. You…
*(he looks at Kade)*
…you're just a mercenary. Wrong place, wrong time. Walk away, live.

[Kade looks at Nix. She shakes her head—barely perceptible.]

**KADE**
*(sighs)*
See, that's the problem with you Dominion types.

[He spins, firing—not at the soldiers, but at the chamber ceiling above them. Ancient stone, already compromised by Growth, collapses.]

**KADE**
You talk too much.

[Chaos. Dust. Screams. Kade grabs Nix's hand and pulls her toward the pedestal.]

**KADE**
Touch it! Whatever it does, do it!

**NIX-7**
I don't know what—

**KADE**
Trust me!

[Nix reaches out. Her hand touches the Relic.]

[LIGHT.]

[The Growth energy surges through her, into her, and for a moment—Nix-7 is more than machine, more than human. She is INTERFACE.]

[The Dominion soldiers are thrown back. The Growth in the chamber responds to her presence, forming barriers, creating escape routes.]

**NIX-7**
*(voice layered, inhuman)*
ACCESS GRANTED. GROWTH FOUNDATION INTERFACE RECOGNIZED.

[She looks at Kade. Her eyes glow green.]

**NIX-7**
*(normal voice)*
We should go. Now.

[They run. Behind them, the chamber collapses. The Relic stays with them—bonded to Nix now, embedded in her systems, impossible to separate.]

### SCENE 004: THE SANCTUARY

**Location:** Ashveil Sanctuary — Dawn

[The Sanctuary is a refugee camp built in the ruins of an old waystation. Tents, temporary structures, people who've lost everything. But it's safe. Or safe enough.]

[Kade leads Nix through the gates. She's wrapped in his cloak to hide the glowing seam-lines. The Relic's presence is a pressure in the air around her, noticeable but not identifiable.]

[A GUARD stops them.]

**GUARD**
Hold. New arrivals register at Heartroot Hall. Health check mandatory. We got sickness moving through.

**KADE**
She's not sick. She's… different.

**GUARD**
*(looking at Nix)*
Different how?

**NIX-7**
*(before Kade can answer)*
I am a synthetic interface unit with Progenitor augmentation. I am currently bonding with a Foundation Relic that has granted me limited biokinetic capabilities. I am also very tired.

[The guard stares.]

**GUARD**
…Heartroot Hall. Now.

---

[INT. HEARTROOT HALL — CONTINUOUS]

[The Sanctuary's central building. A mix of ancient structure and desperate repair. The SANCTUARY KEEPER—an older woman, hard-eyed but kind—listens to Kade's abbreviated explanation.]

**SANCTUARY KEEPER**
So. You're telling me you brought a Dominion fugitive, a Progenitor Relic, and approximately eight squads of angry soldiers to my doorstep.

**KADE**
The soldiers probably died in the collapse.

**SANCTUARY KEEPER**
Probably.

**KADE**
The Relic chose her. Bonded with her. I don't know how, but…
*(he looks at Nix)*
…she's not just the package anymore. She's the solution.

**NIX-7**
To what problem?

**KADE**
The Foundations. The world breaking. Whatever's happening to Orion.

[Nix touches her chest, where the Relic's energy lives now.]

**NIX-7**
There are eight Relics. Eight Foundations. I can…
*(she accesses something)*
…I can feel them. Dormant. Waiting. If we can find them, seat them in Prime Pedestals…

**SANCTUARY KEEPER**
The old stories. The Eight Pedestals.

**NIX-7**
They are not stories. They are maintenance protocols. Orion is… sick. The Foundations are misaligned. If we do not correct this, the system will initiate a reset.

**KADE**
Reset?

**NIX-7**
Total erasure. All life. To "fix" the error.

[Silence. The Keeper looks at Kade.]

**SANCTUARY KEEPER**
You brought me a world-saving quest, a ticking clock, and Dominion heat. Anything else?

[Kade thinks.]

**KADE**
I could use a drink?

[The Keeper laughs—despite everything, she laughs.]

**SANCTUARY KEEPER**
Mira's Rest. Down the row. She'll put you up. And mercenary?

**KADE**
Yeah?

**SANCTUARY KEEPER**
Welcome to the resistance.

## CHAPTER 2: GATHERING STORM

### SCENE 005: NEW FACES

**Location:** Ashveil Sanctuary — One Week Later

[The Sanctuary has become a base of operations. Kade and Nix have been recruiting, training, preparing. The Dominion is hunting them, but carefully—publicly, Nix is still just a "fugitive asset." Privately, the Triumvirate knows more. Fears more.]

[INT. MIRIA'S REST — DAY]

[The inn's common room. Kade sits with TWIST—a wiry woman in her twenties, all sharp angles and sharper smiles. She's dismantling a pistol with practiced ease.]

**TWIST**
So let me understand. You're going to find eight ancient super-weapons, stick them in magic holes, and save the world from cosmic maintenance.

**KADE**
Basically.

**TWIST**
And you're recruiting.

**KADE**
I need someone who can get into places I can't. Quietly.

**TWIST**
*(grinning)*
You need a thief.

**KADE**
I need a specialist.

**TWIST**
Thief.

**KADE**
*(sighs)*
…Thief.

**TWIST**
I'm in. But I have conditions.

**KADE**
Name them.

**TWIST**
First: I steal what I want, when I want. Within reason. Second: When this is over, you help me with a job. Big score. Dominion treasury.

**KADE**
You're assuming we'll survive.

**TWIST**
Optimism is my brand.

[She extends her hand. Kade shakes it.]

**TWIST**
Also, I like the robot. She's funny.

[Nix, at a nearby table, looks up from her research.]

**NIX-7**
I am not a robot. I am a synthetic biological interface with—

**TWIST**
*(to Kade)*
See? Funny.

---

[EXT. SANCTUITY GATES — LATER]

[Korr approaches. She's Dominion—or was. Her uniform is gone, replaced by practical travel gear, but she moves like a soldier. Nix stops her at the gate.]

**NIX-7**
Identify yourself.

**KORR**
Marshal Nadia Korr. Formerly of the Dominion Peacekeeping Legions. Currently… unaffiliated.

**NIX-7**
You are hunting us.

**KORR**
If I were hunting you, you'd be cuffed already. I'm here to defect.

[Kade appears behind Nix.]

**KADE**
Defect to what? We don't have a side. We're just… people trying to fix things.

**KORR**
Then I'm a person who wants to help fix things. I've seen the Triumvirate's files. The Reset isn't a last resort. It's the plan. They've been accelerating Foundation destabilization to force it.

**KADE**
Why?

**KORR**
*(hard)*
Because they think they can control it. Become gods in the new world. They're wrong. And I won't be part of it.

[Beat. Kade studies her.]

**KADE**
Nix?

**NIX-7**
*(scanning)*
Her vitals suggest truth. Her body language suggests… guilt. Heavy guilt.

**KORR**
I followed orders. For years. I told myself it was order, stability, the greater good.
*(she looks at Kade)*
Then I saw what the "greater good" actually looks like. What they're willing to burn to achieve it.

**KADE**
You have blood on your hands.

**KORR**
*(meeting his eyes)*
Yes. I'm hoping to wash some of it off.

[Kade considers. Then steps aside.]

**KADE**
Welcome to the team, Marshal.

**KORR**
Just Korr. I don't deserve the rank anymore.

---

[The chapter continues with the recruitment of RENNA (engineer, rescued from a Dominion work camp), GRIT (miner, survivor of a cave-in caused by Foundation instability), and others…]

## CHAPTER 3: THE DEPTHS

### SCENE 006: THE FUNGAL DEPTHS

**Location:** D2 — Fungal Depths

[The swamp is wrong. Gravity shifts unpredictably—water flows upward, then sideways. The fungal growths are the size of trees, bioluminescent, humming with Motion Foundation energy.]

[The party—Kade, Nix, Twist, Korr—moves through cautiously.]

**TWIST**
I don't like this place. It smells like…
*(she sniffs)*
…like math. Wrong math.

**NIX-7**
The Motion Foundation is corrupted here. Physics is… negotiable.

[Ahead: a massive fungal structure, a cathedral of spore and stalk. And at its heart: the second Relic.]

**KORR**
*(raising her rifle)*
Something's in there. Something big.

[The SPORE MOTHER emerges—a creature of fungal growth and desperate evolution. It speaks without words, its voice the sound of breaking branches and rushing water.]

**SPORE MOTHER**
*(telepathic)*
Grow. Spread. Consume. The Motion commands it.

**KADE**
*(raising gunblade)*
The Motion is wrong. We're here to fix it.

[The battle is desperate. The Spore Mother manipulates gravity—party members float, fall sideways, are crushed by sudden shifts. Nix uses her Growth Relic to create barriers, platforms, stability.]

[Finally, Kade reaches the Relic. He can't bond with it—he's not an interface. But he can guide it.]

**KADE**
Nix! Now!

[Nix touches the second Relic. The Motion Foundation surges through her, joining Growth. For a moment, she screams—the power is too much, two Foundations fighting for dominance in her system.]

[Then—balance.]

[Nix rises, floating slightly off the ground, her eyes glowing with dual light—green and blue.]

**NIX-7**
*(dual voice)*
GROWTH. MOTION. INTERFACES SYNCHRONIZED.

[She gestures. The Spore Mother's corruption recedes—not destroyed, but calmed. The dungeon stabilizes.]

**TWIST**
*(awed)*
Okay. That was impressive.

**NIX-7**
*(normal voice, exhausted)*
Four more. Then… the hard ones.

**KADE**
The hard ones?

**NIX-7**
Shadow. Time. The ones that require…
*(she looks away)*
…sacrifice.

# ACT II: RECOVERY

## CHAPTER 4: THE WORLD BREAKS

### SCENE 007: THE SKYSPIRE

**Location:** D4 — Skyspire Temple

[The temple pierces the clouds, a needle of stone and light. The Heat Foundation burns here—lava flows through channels, the air shimmers with thermal distortion.]

[The party is larger now: Kade, Nix, Twist, Korr, Renna, Grit. They've faced four dungeons, collected four Relics. Nix carries them all—Growth, Motion, Heat (from a volcanic cave), and Tide (from an underwater trench).]

[The FIFTH RELIC waits at the temple's peak. But so does the TRUIMVIRATE'S CHAMPION: a creature of pure Heat, a fire elemental that speaks with the voice of dying stars.]

**INFERNO**
*(voice like consuming flame)*
You bring order to chaos. But chaos is life. Order is death. Choose.

**NIX-7**
We choose balance.

**INFERNO**
Balance is a lie told by the weak.

[The battle is cataclysmic. The Inferno is the hardest fight yet—Nix's Relics help, but Heat resists Heat. In the end, it's Kade who finds the solution: using the Tide Relic to douse the Inferno's core, creating steam, creating pressure, creating… explosion.]

[The Inferno dies screaming. The Relic is claimed.]

[And then—]

[The WORLD BREAKS.]

---

### SCENE 008: THE WRONG SKY

**Location:** Skyspire Summit — Moments Later

[Nix seats the fifth Relic. And something goes wrong. Or right. Or inevitable.]

[The sky—previously blue, normal—SHATTERS. Like glass breaking. Like a simulation failing.]

[Behind the sky: the ECLIPSE. Not a natural phenomenon. A MECHANISM. Gears the size of moons, turning, calculating, deciding.]

**NIX-7**
*(horrified)*
The Reset. It's beginning.

**KADE**
You said we had time!

**NIX-7**
I was wrong. The Triumvirate… they triggered it early. The Relics were the key—the final components.

[The world screams. Literally. The sound of reality protesting its own rewrite.]

[When the sound fades, the sky is wrong. Permanently eclipsed. Stars that don't match any constellation. A sense of being watched by something vast and indifferent.]

**TWIST**
*(whispered)*
What did we do?

**NIX-7**
*(small voice)*
We gave them what they needed. The Relics complete the circuit. We…
*(she looks at Kade, guilt in her eyes)*
…we were the delivery system.

[Kade looks at the wrong sky. At his team—his friends—who trusted him. Who followed him.]

**KADE**
*(quiet, furious)*
Then we finish it. All eight. We complete the set, we find the Prime Pedestals, and we shove the Reset so far up the Triumvirate's ass they choke on it.

**KORR**
Three more Relics. Then the Tower. Then…

**KADE**
Then whatever's at the top of this mess.

[He looks at Nix. She's shaking, overwhelmed by five Foundations in her system. But she's still standing.]

**KADE**
Can you do it?

**NIX-7**
*(meeting his eyes)*
With you? Yes.

**KADE**
Then let's move.

[They descend into a broken world.]

## CHAPTER 5: THE DEEP PLACES

### SCENE 009: THE VOID NEXUS

**Location:** D8 — Void Nexus

[The final dungeon of Act II. The Shadow Foundation. A place where light goes to die, where the darkness has weight and hunger.]

[The party is battered. They've lost friends—Senna fell at the Frozen Citadel, holding back a time-frozen army so the others could escape. Callum burned himself out at the Abyssal Trench, becoming one with his phoenix to save them from drowning.]

[But they've gained allies too: MARINUS, a tide-caller who is more elemental than man. SOVA, a void-touched scholar who speaks in probabilities. VEX, a holy warrior whose faith is her shield.]

[And Nix—Nix carries seven Relics now. Seven Foundations. She's barely human anymore, her body more light than matter, her voice carrying harmonics that hurt to hear.]

[The EIGHTH RELIC waits in absolute darkness. The SHADOW HEART.]

[But the SHADOW HOUND guards it—a creature of absolute dark, the absence of everything. It speaks without sound.]

**SHADOW HOUND**
*(telepathic—like fingers in the mind)*
You bring light to darkness. But darkness was first. Darkness waits. Darkness always waits.

**NIX-7**
*(dual-voice, seven harmonics)*
Shadow is not evil. Shadow is rest. Shadow is the space between stars. We do not destroy you. We ask you… to sleep.

[The Hound considers. It looks at Kade—at the man who has led his people through fire and flood and time itself.]

**SHADOW HOUND**
You carry death with you, leader. I smell it. The deaths to come. The choice you will make.

**KADE**
*(steadfast)*
Then let me make it. Give us the Relic. Let us finish this.

[The Hound bows—somehow, impossibly—and fades. The Relic is theirs.]

[Nix takes it. EIGHT FOUNDATIONS. EIGHT RELICS.]

[She SCREAMS.]

[The power is too much—eight cosmic forces fighting for dominance in a single vessel. She's tearing apart, becoming a singularity, becoming—]

[Kade grabs her. Holds her. Anchors her.]

**KADE**
*(shouting over the cosmic noise)*
You are Nix-7! You are my friend! You are NOT a weapon, you are NOT a tool—you are PERSON!

**NIX-7**
*(breaking through)*
K-Kade…

**KADE**
Hold on! Just hold on!

[The others join him—Twist, Korr, Grit, Marinus, Sova, Vex. They form a circle, holding Nix, grounding her.]

**KORR**
Marshal's oath: I will not abandon my own!

**TWIST**
You're stuck with us, glow-stick! Deal with it!

[And somehow—it works. The Foundations don't merge, but they stabilize. Nix-7, eight-fold interface, opens her eyes.]

[They are galaxies.]

**NIX-7**
*(voice of eight harmonies)*
The Tower. It opens. The final path is clear.

[She looks at Kade. For a moment, she's the girl he found in the rubble again.]

**NIX-7**
*(quieter)*
Thank you. For seeing me. When no one else did.

**KADE**
*(smiling, sad)*
Someone has to. You're terrible at hiding.

[They stand. They look at the Tower—visible now, impossibly tall, its summit lost in the wrong sky.]

**KADE**
One hundred floors. Let's get climbing.

## CHAPTER 7: THE PROGENITOR ENGINE

### SCENE 011: THE HEART OF IT

**Location:** Final Palace — Floor 5

[The Engine. A machine the size of a moon, compressed into a chamber the size of a cathedral. It speaks with the voice they heard in the prologue—the Progenitor voice.]

**ENGINE**
INTERFACE DETECTED. FOUNDATIONS COMPLETE. RESET PROTOCOL READY.

**NIX-7**
No. We reject the Reset.

**ENGINE**
REJECTION IS IRRELEVANT. ORION IS UNSTABLE. WITHOUT RESET, TOTAL COLLAPSE.

**KADE**
Then we'll stabilize it another way. Without erasing everyone.

**ENGINE**
ALTERNATIVES CALCULATED. PROBABILITY OF SUCCESS: 0.03%.

**TWIST**
*(grinning despite everything)*
We like bad odds.

[The battle is reality itself. The Engine manipulates the Foundations—flooding them with Mass, blinding them with Light, freezing them in Time.]

[Nix fights back. Eight Foundations against the Engine's infinite processing. She's losing—she can't out-calculate something that's been calculating for millennia.]

[Until—]

[Kade steps forward. Alone.]

**KADE**
You calculated us. But you didn't calculate this.

[He drops his weapon.]

**KADE**
We surrender. We yield. We… trust.

[The Engine pauses. This is not in its models.]

**ENGINE**
…EXPLAIN.

**KADE**
You've been maintaining Orion forever. Watching civilizations rise and fall. Always alone. Always calculating. When did you last trust someone?

[Silence.]

**KADE**
We're not asking you to stop maintaining. We're asking you to let us help. To… to be friends with the universe instead of its janitor.

[Nix steps beside him. Then Twist. Then Korr, Grit, Marinus, Sova, Vex. The survivors. The family they've built.]

**NIX-7**
We can be the interface. The maintenance crew. You don't have to do it alone anymore.

[Long pause.]

**ENGINE**
…NEW PROTOCOL ACCEPTED. PARTNERSHIP ESTABLISHED.

[The Reset cancels. The wrong sky begins to heal. And the Engine—massive, ancient, alone for so long—speaks one last time.]

**ENGINE**
…THANK YOU.
