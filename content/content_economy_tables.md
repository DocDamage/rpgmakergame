## INFLATION CONTROL

### Gil Sources
- Enemy drops: 40%
- Chests: 25%
- Quest rewards: 20%
- Selling items: 15%

### Gil Sinks
- Equipment purchases: 35%
- Consumables: 25%
- Crafting costs: 20%
- Fast travel: 10%
- Gambling/mini-games: 10%

### Endgame Gil Cap
**Maximum:** 999,999,999 G  
**Soft cap mechanics:** Reduced drop rates above 100M G
