# CHROMA'S EDGE - ACT STRUCTURE MASTER DOCUMENT

---

## Overview

This document provides the explicit beat-by-beat breakdown of Chroma's Edge's three-act narrative structure across 14 script parts, 186 main story scenes, plus postgame content. Serves as the master narrative scaffold for all story implementation.

---

## THREE-ACT BREAKDOWN

### ACT 1: HUNT (Script Parts 1-2)
**Duration:** Scenes 1-26 (2 script parts)
**Level Range:** 1-50
**Story Arc:** Party assembly, relic hunt, Dominion pursuit
**Key Mechanic:** Learning to use Lattice terminals to command world systems

#### Thematic Focus
Establish the world as vast, dangerous, and controllable through ancient infrastructure. Party members are driven by personal goals (Kade's bounty, survival, redemption) but gradually realize their individual quests interconnect toward planetary stakes.

**Scene 1: The Contract**
- Location: Dusthaven (Broker Vane's office)
- NPC: Broker Vane (DT_VANE_MAIN)
- Objective: Kade accepts relic-hunting contract from Ironhawk Syndicate
- Gameplay: Tutorial on contract system, bounty hunting introduction
- Character Focus: Kade established as cynical bounty hunter, mentions lost mentor
- Significance: Story inciting incident

- NPC: Scout Captain Rooke Vale (DT_ROOKE_MILITIA)

**Duration:** Scenes 27-93 (5 script parts)
**Level Range:** 50-120
**Story Arc:** Party reassembly, Prime pedestal seating, Korr's liberation arc
