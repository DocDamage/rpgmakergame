# CHROMA'S EDGE - CHARACTER ROSTER & BACKSTORIES

## THE 12 PARTY MEMBERS (Complete)

### 1. KADE (Main Character - Bounty Hunter)
**Full Name:** Kade (surname lost/never had one)
**Class:** Gunblade Specialist / Dragon-Born Cyborg
**Age:** 28
**Role:** Balanced DPS, Party Leader

**Backstory:**
Found half-dead at age 8 in the Scorched Flats after a Dominion raid on a frontier convoy. Jasper Vos found him bleeding out, organs failing from shrapnel. Vos replaced Kade's ruined lungs, kidney, and part of his spine with sleek cybernetics—not clunky Dominion military grade, but custom work. Elegant. Expensive.

Vos trained Kade for fifteen years. Assassination. Treasure recovery. Bounty work. Vos had one rule: "The job is the job." But Kade added his own: never kill kids, never harm innocents, always protect your crew. Vos called it "soft thinking" but never stopped him.

When Kade was 23, Dominion forces raided their safehouse. Vos told him to run. Kade heard the gunfire stop, then silence. He found Vos's blood but no body. Assumed dead.

Five years of solo work later, Kade's built a reputation: expensive, effective, principled. The Ironhawk Syndicate hates him for taking contracts off their board. The Dominion wants him dead for the dragon-born genetics they can't quite pin down on scanners.

Then he takes a contract to recover "stolen Dominion property." Easy money.

The property turns out to be a kid.

**Personality:**
- Sarcastic armor over deep loyalty
- Tired of the world's bullshit, but can't stop caring
- Pragmatic: "Killing you costs me nothing. Walking away costs me nothing. Choose."
- Softens considerably once party becomes family

### 3. RENNA KYTE (The Scavenger)
**Class:** Tech Specialist / Engineer
**Age:** 23
**Role:** Item Crafter, Trap Disabler, Ranged DPS

**Backstory:**
Born in the Scrapfort salvage yards to parents who died when a reactor core they were stripping went critical. She was twelve. The yard boss, a man named Culver, took her in—not out of kindness, but because she was already better with circuitry than his adult crew.

By sixteen, Renna could build a functional plasma cutter from junk parts and wire. By twenty, she'd designed her own turret drones that could track motion and auto-fire. Culver tried to sell her designs to the Dominion. She caught wind, sabotaged the deal, and fled with her best work.

Now she freelances, selling tech to frontier towns and running from Culver's goons. She met Kade when he was hunting the same Dominion enforcer she'd been tracking (the enforcer killed her mentor, a kind woman named Sula who taught her electronics).

Kade saved her life in the firefight. She's been following him since.

**Personality:**
- Relentless optimist despite everything ("Hey, at least we're not dead!")
- Makes jokes to deflate tension, sometimes inappropriately
- Brilliant with machines, hopeless with people
- Fiercely loyal once she decides you're "crew"

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Turret Deploy (25 MP):** Summon auto-attack turret that lasts 5 turns, deals 50% of Renna's ATK per turn to random enemy
2. **Overcharge (30 MP):** Boost all party members' tech-based weapons by +50% damage for 4 turns

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Shock Mine (plant explosive, detonates when enemy acts, 200% damage + paralyze)
- **Tier 2:** Drone Swarm (summon 5 attack drones, each deals 100% damage to random targets)
- **Tier 3:** EMP Nova (AOE that disables all enemy abilities for 3 turns + heavy damage to constructs)
- **Ultimate - Culver's Nightmare:** Summon massive siege turret, rains hellfire for 3 turns (300% damage per turn to all enemies), then explodes for 800% final damage

**Assigned Summon:** Titan (earth/construction theme, she appreciates solid engineering)

**Weapon Types:** Rifles, SMGs, Wrenches (melee backup)
**Armor:** Light armor, tech-enhanced vests

### 4. DR.马林 SURESH (The Medic)
**Class:** Combat Healer / Dominion Deserter
**Age:** 34
**Role:** White Magic, Healing, Support Buffs

**Backstory:**
Born Marin Suresh in the Dominion capital, Vex Prime. Brilliant medical student, top of her class. The Dominion recruited her at 22 for military medical corps. She thought she'd be saving lives.

For eight years, she did. Then came the "Purity Directive."

The Dominion began experimenting on prisoners—injecting them with proto-Foundation energy to create super-soldiers. Suresh was assigned to keep them alive during the process. Most died screaming. Some survived, warped into monsters. The Dominion terminated them as "failures."

Suresh kept meticulous notes. Evidence. When she discovered the Triumvirate knew the experiments were torturous and fatal, she tried to report it. They promoted her. "Excellent work, Doctor."

She deserted three days later, stealing medical supplies and data drives. Now she runs a hidden clinic in Fungal Depths, treating refugees and deserters. The Dominion wants her dead. The refugees call her "Saint Marin."

Kade found her when Nix was damaged in a firefight. She patched the android up. Kade asked why she'd help. She said, "Because I spent eight years hurting people. I have a lot to make up for."

**Personality:**
- Haunted by guilt, seeking redemption
- Overprotective of party, especially Nix ("I've already failed too many children.")
- Refuses to kill unless absolutely necessary (will incapacitate)
- Dry humor: "You're not dying. I've seen dying. You're just whining."

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Triage Protocol (0 MP, 1/battle):** Instant full heal to one party member, costs Suresh 25% of her own HP
2. **Field Surgery (45 MP):** Revive one KO'd ally with 100% HP and remove all status effects

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Mass Heal (restore 60% HP to all allies)
- **Tier 2:** Antibody Surge (cure all status effects + immunity for 3 turns, all party)
- **Tier 3:** Life Link (Suresh splits all incoming party damage, taking 50% herself, lasts 5 turns)
- **Ultimate - Hippocratic Override:** Revive all KO'd allies at full HP, cure all statuses, party becomes invincible for 2 turns (then Suresh faints, must be revived)

**Assigned Summon:** Leviathan (cleansing water theme, washing away sins)

**Weapon Types:** Rods, Staves, Syringes (inject combat drugs as melee)
**Armor:** Light robes, medic vests

### 5. TWIST (The Treasure Hunter)
**Class:** Rogue / Thief
**Age:** 26
**Role:** High AGI, Steal, Critical DPS

**Backstory:**
Real name unknown. "Twist" is what the Ironhawk Syndicate called her because she could twist her way out of any cell, any locked room, any bad situation. She grew up in the Guild's orphan program—kids raised to be thieves and killers. She excelled.

By twenty-two, Twist was the Syndicate's best relic hunter. Ancient drake-beast artifacts, Dominion tech, Foundation fragments—if it was valuable and hidden, she found it. The Guildmaster, a bastard named Karrick, took 80% of her hauls. She took the remaining 20% and buried it in hidden caches across Orion.

Then Karrick sold her out. Sent her on a job to steal from a Dominion armory, but tipped them off. She walked into an ambush. Kade happened to be in the area on an unrelated job, saw the setup, and decided it was bad form to let someone get executed by fifty soldiers.

He saved her. She's been suspicious of his motives ever since.

But she follows him anyway. Because for the first time in her life, someone helped her without asking for a cut.

**Personality:**
- Cocky exterior, deep insecurity underneath
- Obsessed with treasure/gold, but it's really about security (never wants to be powerless again)
- Trusts no one except the crew (and barely trusts them)
- Competitive with Kade (constantly trying to one-up him)

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Mug (20 MP):** Attack enemy for 150% damage AND steal item simultaneously (80% success rate)
2. **Smoke Bomb (15 MP):** Guaranteed flee from non-boss battles, or +50% flee chance from bosses

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Sneak Attack (teleport behind enemy, 400% critical damage)
- **Tier 2:** Loot Frenzy (steal from all enemies simultaneously + gain random buffs)
- **Tier 3:** Shadow Step (turn invisible for 3 turns, all attacks auto-crit, cannot be targeted)
- **Ultimate - Karrick's Reckoning:** Rapid 10-hit combo (each hit 150% damage) that steals enemy buffs and distributes them to party

**Assigned Summon:** Odin (speed/instant death theme, she appreciates efficiency)

**Weapon Types:** Daggers, Whips, Chakrams
**Armor:** Light leather, stealth cloaks

### 6. DR. INES SOVA (The Scientist)
**Class:** Exiled Researcher / Black Mage
**Age:** 41
**Role:** Offensive Magic, Elemental Exploitation

**Backstory:**
Former head researcher for the Dominion's Foundation Studies Division. Brilliant physicist specializing in crystallized energy matrices. She's the one who discovered the Omega Pedestals' existence while analyzing ancient drake-beast maps.

When she realized the Omega locations were *wrong*—intentionally wrong—she brought her findings to the Triumvirate. The Arbiter smiled and said, "Thank you, Doctor. Your research will be invaluable."

Then they tried to kill her.

Sova fled with stolen data drives containing the correct Prime Pedestal locations. She's been hiding in frontier towns ever since, using fake names, dying her distinctive silver hair. The Dominion wants her dead because she knows the truth: they're not stupid. They *know* the Omega Pedestals will break the world. They're doing it on purpose.

She joined Kade's crew when she overheard them discussing Nix's Lattice interface abilities. She realized Nix is the key to everything—both the problem and the solution.

Now she's helping them, even though she knows the odds. Someone has to stop this. Might as well be her.

**Personality:**
- Analytical to a fault, struggles with emotion
- Guilt-ridden over her years working for the Dominion
- Socially awkward ("I'm better with numbers than people")
- Protective of Nix (sees the android as her chance at redemption)
- Dry, dark humor: "Well, we're all going to die. But at least we'll die *correctly*."

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Analyze (10 MP):** Reveal all enemy data (HP, weaknesses, resistances, drop rates) to entire party
2. **Elemental Shift (25 MP):** Change own magic element mid-battle (fire, ice, lightning, etc.) for next 5 spells

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Overload (quadruple magic damage on next spell, but costs all MP)
- **Tier 2:** Chain Reaction (cast 3 random elemental spells at once, AOE)
- **Tier 3:** Quantum Collapse (remove one enemy from battle for 3 turns, they return at 1 HP)
- **Ultimate - Sova's Theorem:** Rewrite reality equations—all enemies take 1000% magic damage, all resistances become weaknesses, lasts 3 turns

**Assigned Summon:** Ramuh (lightning/knowledge theme, represents enlightenment)

**Weapon Types:** Rods, Data Pads (channel spells through tech)
**Armor:** Lab coats (enchanted), light mage robes

### 7. GRIT (The Tank)
**Class:** Heavy Vanguard / Former Dominion Soldier
**Age:** 37
**Role:** Tank, Physical Defense, Taunt Mechanics

**Backstory:**
Born Gideon Rourke in a Dominion military family. Third-generation soldier. Enlisted at 18, served with distinction for fifteen years. Became a decorated sergeant, led a mechanized infantry unit.

Then came the Purity Directive.

Grit's unit was ordered to suppress a civilian protest in a frontier town. Unarmed people. Families. The order was "pacify by any means necessary." Grit refused. His commanding officer shot a civilian child to "set an example."

Grit broke the officer's neck.

He fled with his armor and weapons, became a deserter. The Dominion labeled him a traitor and sent kill teams. He's been fighting them ever since. Took the name "Grit" because it's all he has left—stubborn refusal to die.

Met Kade when they were both ambushed by the same Dominion hit squad. They fought back-to-back. Grit's been with him since, trying to atone for the years he followed orders without question.

**Personality:**
- Stoic, speaks only when necessary
- Deep guilt over past actions ("I was a monster in uniform")
- Fatherly toward younger party members (especially Nix)
- Unshakable loyalty once trust is earned
- Gallows humor: "If I die here, at least it's not wearing their colors."

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Fortress Stance (20 MP):** Taunt all enemies (forces them to target Grit) + reduce damage taken by 50% for 4 turns
2. **Retaliate (15 MP, passive toggle):** Automatically counter-attack any enemy that hits Grit (50% damage)

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Shield Bash (300% damage + stun for 2 turns)
- **Tier 2:** Immovable (become invincible for 2 turns, taunt all enemies)
- **Tier 3:** Last Stand (when HP drops below 20%, gain +100% DEF and auto-revive once at 1 HP)
- **Ultimate - Rourke's Redemption:** Absorb all damage for entire party for 5 turns (Grit cannot die during this), then release stored damage as AOE explosion (total absorbed damage × 2)

**Assigned Summon:** Titan (earth/immovability theme, represents standing firm)

**Weapon Types:** Greatswords, Hammers, Shields

**Age:** 30
**Role:** Ranged Physical DPS, Tracking, Survival Skills

**Backstory:**
Born in the deep frontier, beyond Dominion reach. Grew up in a nomadic clan that moved with the seasons, following game herds and avoiding warped zones. Ashka learned to hunt at age seven—tracking, trapping, killing cleanly. Her father taught her: "Respect the kill. Waste nothing."

When she was nineteen, a Dominion expedition came through, surveying for Foundation energy. They poisoned a river with runoff, killed the herds. Her clan starved. Ashka's younger brother died of sickness from contaminated water.

She tracked the Dominion team for three months. Killed them one by one. Long-range. Clean shots. Then she disappeared into the wastes.

Now she's a legend among frontier folk—the ghost who hunts hunters. When Kade's crew saved a refugee caravan from warped beasts, Ashka was tracking the same creatures. She watched Kade protect civilians. Decided he was worth following.

**Personality:**
- Quiet, observant, speaks in short sentences
- Deeply connected to nature (mourns every warped creature she kills)
- Patient to an extreme ("I once waited six days for a shot.")
- Soft spot for animals (constantly trying to tame pets)
- Rare smile is genuine and warm

**Unique Abilities:**

- **Ultimate - Verne's Requiem:** Single shot that deals damage equal to enemy's current HP × 0.75 (effectively 75% max HP nuke), if it kills, gain another turn

### 9. SENNA (The Monk)

**Role:** Unarmed DPS, Ki Techniques, Speed Fighter

**Backstory:**
Born into the Progenitor cult, raised in hidden monasteries beneath the Dominion capital. The cult taught that the body is a cage, that transcendence comes through pain and mutation. They injected her with diluted Foundation energy at age twelve—"baptism by evolution."

Most children died. Senna survived, but the energy didn't mutate her. Instead, it enhanced her—faster reflexes, denser bones, stronger muscles. The cult called her "blessed."

She trained as an enforcer, hunting down deserters and skeptics. For ten years, she believed. Then she was ordered to kill a family that refused to let their daughter be "baptized."

Senna looked at the child—terrified, innocent—and saw herself at twelve, screaming as they injected her. She killed the cult members instead and fled with the family.

Now she's an apostate, marked for death by the cult. She fights to atone, using the power they gave her to destroy them.

**Personality:**
- Intense, driven, struggles with anger

- Protective of innocents to an unhealthy degree (sees herself in every victim)

- Quotes old cult mantras, then curses them

## ANTAGONISTS

### THE MENTOR: JASPER VOS

**Age:** 52 (appears younger due to cybernetics)
**Role:** Personal Antagonist, Dominion Enforcer, Tragic Villain

**Original Backstory (Before Capture):**
Former bounty hunter, one of the best. Built a reputation for being untouchable—never missed a contract, never left witnesses. But he had standards. No kids. No innocents. No slavery contracts.

Found Kade dying in the wastes during a job. Could've left him. Didn't. Something about the kid reminded him of himself—alone, broken, refusing to die. Vos used his savings to buy Kade custom cybernetics from a back-alley surgeon. Gave the kid a purpose.

Trained Kade for fifteen years. Harsh, but fair. Taught him everything: weapons, tactics, survival, the code. "The job is the job, but we're not monsters."

When the Dominion came for him (they wanted his skills for the Foundation Relic hunts), Vos told Kade to run. Vos fought. Lost. They rebuilt him.

**Post-Capture (Current State):**
The Dominion stripped Vos down and rebuilt him as a weapon. Military-grade cybernetics replaced 60% of his body. Neural implants override his will. Memory suppressors erase Kade periodically, but fragments return.

Now he's "Enforcer-Prime," the Dominion's top hunter. Cold. Efficient. He hunts Kade without recognizing him. But during fights, glitches appear—hesitation, old combat patterns, calling Kade "kid."

By the Final Palace, the Progenitor takes full control, puppeting Vos directly. Kade has to break the neural link to free him. Vos, dying, uses his last moments to damage the Progenitor Engine.

**Personality (Original):**
- Gruff, sarcastic, dry wit
- Harsh teacher with a soft heart
- "I don't do sentiment, kid. But I'd kill for you."
- Regretted his life choices, saw Kade as a second chance

**Personality (Dominion-Controlled):**
- Cold, robotic, no emotion
- Speaks in clipped military jargon
- Occasional glitches where the real Vos surfaces
- Final fight: desperation, self-loathing, begging Kade to finish it

**Combat Style:**
- Mirror of Kade (same weapons, same tactics)
- Learns player strategies between encounters
- Phase 1: Holds back (subconscious)
- Phase 2: Progenitor takes control, no mercy
- Phase 3: Vos fights back internally, creates openings for Kade

**Death Quote:**
"You were always... more than a weapon. Finish it."
