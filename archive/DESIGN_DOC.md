# CHROMA'S EDGE - Complete Integrated Design Document

## CORE IDENTITY

**Genre:** Space-Opera JRPG (Mandalorian aesthetic meets FF6 world-break tragedy)
**Setting:** Single planet (Orion) - no spaceflight, grounded frontier experience
**Tone:** Gritty frontier survival + tragic weight + high-tech mythology
**Visual Style:** Muted & gritty procedural sprites (16×16 to 32×32), mixed tech aesthetic

# WORLD & COSMOLOGY

## The Planet: Orion

**Tech Level Distribution:**
- **Frontier Regions:** Rust-belt settlements, salvaged tech, desperate survival
- **The Capital (Dominion HQ):** Sterile white halls, surveillance state, advanced weaponry
- **Ancient Sites:** Incomprehensible pre-civilization technology (drake-beast origin)

**Aesthetic Reference:** The Mandalorian's lived-in universe, but planet-bound

## The Lattice (World Infrastructure Network)

**What It Is:**
- Ancient planetary management system built by drake-beasts
- Controls climate, terraforming, transport, defense grids, and dungeon mechanisms
- Uploaded with drake-beast collective memory/knowledge before they sealed themselves
- Accessible only through **Terminals** scattered across Orion

**Terminal Functions:**
- Climate control (localized weather manipulation)
- Transport gates (fast travel between discovered terminals)
- Defense protocols (turrets, shields, lockdowns)
- Terraforming nodes (reshape regions over time)
- Dungeon mechanisms (open sealed doors, activate bridges, disable traps)
- Information access (lore, history, warnings)

**Plot Significance:**
- Nix-7 can recalibrate newly found terminals (party's key to infrastructure)
- Dominion seeks to weaponize the Lattice
- Progenitor Engine whispers through compromised Lattice nodes

## The Progenitor Engine (Machine-God Antagonist)

**True Nature:**
- Buried ancient superstructure intelligence (planet-scale AI)
- Not evil by design, but corrupted/incomplete after drake-beasts sealed themselves
- Believes it is "perfecting" evolution through controlled reality warping
- Cannot act directly—requires biological proxies (the cult, the Dominion)

**Communication Methods:**
- **Dreams:** Whispers visions to susceptible minds (cult members, Triumvirate)
- **Omega Pedestals:** Direct psychic link when Relics are incorrectly placed
- **Compromised Terminals:** Subliminal data streams through Lattice access

**Goal:** Use the 8 Foundations incorrectly (Omega placement) to remake Orion into its "ideal" world

## The 8 Foundations (Pieces of Reality)

Each Foundation is both a **Relic** (physical object) and a **cosmic principle**:

1. **Mass** - Gravity, density, physical form
2. **Motion** - Kinetic energy, momentum, flow
3. **Heat** - Thermal energy, entropy, transformation
4. **Tide** - Liquid dynamics, cycles, ebb/flow
5. **Growth** - Biological expansion, evolution, life
6. **Light** - Radiation, illumination, truth
7. **Shadow** - Absence, concealment, potential
8. **Time** - Causality, sequence, decay

**Relics (Physical Form):**
- Ancient crystalline/metallic artifacts (size of a person)
- Each resonates with its Foundation's energy
- Glow with distinct colors (Mass = deep purple, Heat = crimson, etc.)
- Can only be moved by those with drake-core genetics (MC, Nix)

## The Pedestal System

**Prime Pedestals (8 Correct Locations):**
- Original drake-beast placements that stabilize Orion
- Located in geologically/energetically correct positions
- When all 8 Relics are seated here, world is stable

**Omega Pedestals (8 False Locations):**
- Near-but-not-exact positions (off by miles, in wrong terrain)
- Built by Progenitor Engine's whispered instructions to ancient cult
- When Relics are seated here, world destabilizes and warps

**Plot Cycle:**
- **Start:** Relics are in Prime Pedestals (world stable, but Dominion seeks them)
- **Midgame:** Dominion removes Relics, places them in Omega Pedestals → world breaks
- **Endgame:** Party recovers Relics, returns them to Prime Pedestals → world restores

# REALITY WARPING & MONSTERS

## What Reality Warping Means Here

**NOT:** Memory erasure, timeline editing, reality rewriting
**YES:** Physical/biological/environmental distortion

**Warping Effects:**
- Creatures mutate into monstrous forms
- Landscapes twist (gravity shifts, terrain melts/freezes wrongly)
- Weather becomes hostile and unnatural
- Time flows inconsistently in certain zones (aging accelerates/reverses)
- Plants grow into carnivorous nightmares

## Monster Origins (Story-Driven)

**All monsters exist because Foundation instability warps reality.**

**Pre-Midgame (Relics in Prime, but Foundation energy leaking):**
- **Warped Wildlife:** Animals exposed to leaked Foundation energy mutate
  - Example: Timber wolves → Spectral Razorbacks (Shadow-warped, phase through matter)
  - Example: Desert lizards → Thermavores (Heat-warped, breathe fire, eat metal)

**Post-Midgame (Relics in Omega, world fully broken):**
- **Catastrophic Mutations:** Warping accelerates exponentially
  - Entire forests become sentient predator-hives
  - Oceans birth tidal leviathans (Tide Foundation corruption)
  - Gravity wells spawn floating island-sized creatures (Mass corruption)

**Dominion-Made Constructs:**
- War machines built using stolen Foundation energy
- Cyber-organic hybrids (prisoners forcibly fused with tech)
- Sentinel drones powered by Light Foundation fragments

**Cult-Grown Bioforms:**
- Engineered "perfected" organisms bred in hidden labs
- Attempts to create "next-stage" humans (all failures, monstrous)
- Growth Foundation experiments (tumorous living weapons)

**Enemy Design Philosophy:**
- Every enemy type has lore explaining its warped origin
- Late-game enemies show extreme Foundation corruption (elemental nightmares)
- Bosses are either Dominion lieutenants OR Foundation-warped apex creatures

# STORY STRUCTURE

## Act 1: The Contract (Dungeons 1-4)

**Opening:**
- MC is a bounty hunter taking contracts in frontier towns
- Reputation: "Gets the job done, but has a code"
- **Inciting Incident:** Contract to retrieve "stolen Dominion property" (Nix-7)

**Dungeon 1: Ruins of Ashveil (Tutorial)**
- **Location:** Abandoned frontier outpost
- **Enemy Type:** Warped scavengers, rust-tech drones
- **Party Spotlight:** MC solo → recruits first companion (tech-savvy scavenger)
- **Boss:** Dominion enforcer (low-level)
- **Reveal:** The "property" is Nix-7, a confused android child
- **Choice:** MC keeps Nix instead of turning them in (code: never harm a child)

**Dungeon 2: Fungal Depths**
- **Location:** Bio-warped underground cavern
- **Enemy Type:** Growth Foundation mutations (carnivorous fungi, spore zombies)
- **Party Spotlight:** Recruit medic (former Dominion deserter)
- **Boss:** Overgrown Mycelial Titan
- **Plot:** Nix begins recalibrating terminals, reveals they can access the Lattice
- **Revelation:** Dominion is hunting Nix because they're the key to the Foundations

**Dungeon 3: Crystal Caverns**
- **Location:** Ancient drake-beast mining site, crystallized Light Foundation energy
- **Enemy Type:** Light-warped creatures (blinding predators, laser-beam beetles)
- **Party Spotlight:** Recruit treasure hunter + exiled Dominion scientist
- **Boss:** Lieutenant of Light (Dominion officer enhanced with Foundation shard)
- **Plot:** First encounter with the Mentor (personal antagonist)
  - Mentor: "You were supposed to die in that wasteland. I gave you purpose."
  - MC: "You turned me into a tool."
  - Mentor: "No. I turned you into a weapon. The Dominion will do the same to the world."
- **Reward:** First Foundation Relic recovered (Light), party learns about Prime Pedestals

**Dungeon 4: Skyspire Temple**
- **Location:** Ancient drake-beast cathedral floating via Mass Foundation
- **Enemy Type:** Gravity-warped beasts (floating predators, anti-grav constructs)
- **Party Spotlight:** Full 12-person party assembled (final recruits join here)
- **Boss:** Lieutenant of Mass (cult fanatic merged with Progenitor tech)
- **Plot:** Party discovers the Omega Pedestals and the cult's plan
  - Nix: "There are two sets. One stabilizes. One destroys."
  - Scientist: "The Dominion doesn't know. They think both sets are the same."
- **Midgame Setup:** Dominion accelerates plan, begins moving Relics

## Act 2: The World Breaks (Dungeons 5-6)

**Midgame Catastrophe (FF6 Kefka Moment):**
- Dominion Triumvirate, manipulated by cult and Progenitor whispers, successfully:
  - Removes all 8 Relics from Prime Pedestals
  - Seats them into Omega Pedestals
  - Forces Nix to activate them (threatens party)
- **Result:** Reality warps planet-wide
  - Continents shift
  - Climates invert (deserts freeze, tundras burn)
  - Monster mutations accelerate (horrors emerge)
  - Lattice malfunctions (infrastructure fails)
  - Drake-beasts in Deepward begin dying (their seal weakens)

**Party Scatters, Then Regroups:**
- Each character survives the cataclysm separately
- Player regains control in broken world, must find scattered party
- Bounty Guild (former antagonist) becomes ally: "The Dominion broke everything. We're with you now."

**Dungeon 5: Abyssal Trench (Post-Break)**
- **Location:** Oceanic rift, Tide Foundation warping drowns coastal cities
- **Enemy Type:** Tidal horrors (leviathans, pressure-immune abominations)
- **Party Spotlight:** Rescue aquatic specialist + lost party members
- **Boss:** Lieutenant of Tide (cult member merged with sea-beast)
- **Plot:** Recover Tide Relic, begin reversing Omega placements
- **Emotional Beat:** Party sees refugee camps, broken families, children orphaned by warping

**Dungeon 6: Molten Core**
- **Location:** Volcanic rift, Heat Foundation turning world's core unstable
- **Enemy Type:** Pyroclastic elementals, magma-forged constructs
- **Party Spotlight:** Confront Mentor again (not final fight)
- **Boss:** Lieutenant of Heat (Mentor's second-in-command, jealous of MC)
- **Plot:** Recover Heat Relic
- **Revelation:** Mentor shows regret—was rebuilt against his will, Dominion controls him
  - Mentor: "I didn't choose this. But you did. You chose to care."
  - MC: "Then choose again. Help us stop this."
  - Mentor: "I can't. The Progenitor... won't let me." (Flees)

## Act 3: Reclamation (Dungeons 7-8, Tower, Final Palace)

**Dungeon 7: Frozen Citadel**
- **Location:** Northern wastes, Time Foundation freezing world in stasis
- **Enemy Type:** Time-locked creatures (undying horrors, aging-immune beasts)
- **Party Spotlight:** Youngest party member confronts fear of mortality
- **Boss:** Lieutenant of Time (cult leader, oldest living human, sustained by Foundation)
- **Plot:** Recover Time Relic, learn Progenitor's true nature
  - Terminal lore dump: "The Progenitor was meant to manage evolution. But incomplete code... it sees improvement as endless mutation."

**Dungeon 8: Void Nexus (Pre-Tower)**
- **Location:** Shadow Foundation rift, pocket dimension of absence
- **Enemy Type:** Shadow-born nightmares (creatures made of un-light, void constructs)
- **Party Spotlight:** Party confronts their regrets, past failures
- **Boss:** Lieutenant of Shadow (former party member's sibling, thought dead)
- **Plot:** Recover Shadow Relic, final preparation for Tower
- **Emotional Peak:** Nix asks, "If I reset the world, will I still exist?"
  - MC: "You'll exist. Because we'll remember you."

**Endgame Tower: The Lattice Nexus (100 Floors)**
- **Purpose:** Ascend to central Lattice control to restore Prime Pedestals remotely
- **Structure:** 
  - Floors 1-20: Motion Foundation (wind, speed, kinetic enemies)
  - Floors 21-40: Growth Foundation (bio-horrors, evolving bosses)
  - Floors 41-60: Mass Foundation (gravity shifts, density puzzles)
  - Floors 61-80: Light Foundation (blinding arenas, laser gauntlets)
  - Floors 81-99: Mixed Foundations (all enemy types, extreme warping)
  - Floor 100: Guardian of the Lattice (Alexander-equivalent, defends system)
- **Rewards:** Best gear, Lattice control access, final summon unlock
- **Plot Beat (Floor 50):** Dominion Triumvirate fight (political + religious leaders die, military leader defects)

**Final Palace: The Progenitor Engine**
- **Entry Requirement:** All 8 Relics recovered, Tower cleared
- **Location:** Deep beneath planet surface, ancient drake-beast vault
- **Structure:** 5 floors, each themed to a Foundation pair
  - Floor 1: Mass + Motion (physical reality challenges)
  - Floor 2: Heat + Tide (elemental chaos)
  - Floor 3: Growth + Shadow (life and death twisted)
  - Floor 4: Light + Time (perception and causality broken)
  - Floor 5: The Engine Chamber (all Foundations collapse into singularity)

**Boss Gauntlet:**
1. **Floor 1 Boss:** Dominion Military Leader (redeemed, sacrifices self to let party through)
2. **Floor 2 Boss:** Cult High Priests (four-way simultaneous fight)
3. **Floor 3 Boss:** Mentor (Final Fight)
   - **Phase 1:** Mentor fights reluctantly (holds back)
   - **Phase 2:** Progenitor takes full control, Mentor becomes puppet
   - **Phase 3:** MC breaks Progenitor's hold, Mentor sacrifices self to wound Engine
   - **Death Quote:** "You were always... more than a weapon. Finish it."
4. **Floor 4 Boss:** The Warped World (abstraction, party fights concept of broken reality)
5. **Floor 5 Boss:** The Progenitor Engine (8 Phases)

**The Progenitor Engine (Final Boss - 8 Phases):**

**Phase 1 - MASS (100-90%):** Gravity attacks, pulls party toward center, crushing damage
**Phase 2 - MOTION (90-75%):** Rapid kinetic strikes, impossible-to-track speed
**Phase 3 - HEAT (75-60%):** Magma floods arena, constant fire damage
**Phase 4 - TIDE (60-50%):** Arena floods, drowning mechanics, pressure damage
**Phase 5 - GROWTH (50-35%):** Summons endless bio-constructs, must burn them faster than they spawn
**Phase 6 - LIGHT (35-20%):** Blinds party, laser grids, must fight in darkness
**Phase 7 - SHADOW (20-10%):** Becomes intangible, party must hit weak spots in void
**Phase 8 - TIME (10-0%):** Ages party (stat decay), rewinds own HP, entropy mechanics

**Final Mechanics:**
- Progenitor attempts to corrupt Nix to prevent reset
- Party must protect Nix while damaging Engine
- Once Engine at 1 HP, Nix initiates reset sequence
- **Choice:** 
  - **Reset with Nix intact:** Nix reprograms Foundations, survives, world restored (true ending)
  - **Reset without Nix:** Nix sacrifices core to ensure perfect restoration, dies (tragic ending)

**Ending (True Version):**
- Relics seated in Prime Pedestals
- Nix recalibrates Foundations correctly
- Reality warping reverses (monsters revert to normal animals over weeks)
- Lattice stabilizes under drake-beast protocol
- Progenitor Engine goes dormant (not destroyed, contained)
- MC retires from bounty hunting, opens orphanage (callback to code: protect children)
- Nix learns to smile

# MAIN CHARACTER

## The Bounty Hunter (Player-Named MC)

**Species:** Dragon-Born (rare genetic bloodline from ancient drake-beasts)
**Augmentation:** Cyborg (sleek cybernetics, not bulky)

**Backstory:**
- Found half-dead as a child in the wastes by the Mentor
- Given cybernetic augmentations to survive (organs replaced, neural enhancements)
- Trained as assassin/treasure hunter/bounty hunter by Mentor
- Mentor "died" in a Dominion raid (actually captured and rebuilt)
- MC went freelance, built reputation as effective but principled hunter

**Personality:**
- **Code:**
  - "The job is the job (until it isn't)"
  - Never kill a child
  - Never harm innocents
  - Always protect companions
- Pragmatic, sarcastic, world-weary
- Softens over the story as party becomes family
- Deep loyalty once earned

**Combat Role:** Balanced DPS (guns + melee, moderate magic)
**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Drake's Breath (20 MP):** Fire cone attack (dragon bloodline)
2. **Targeting Protocol (35 MP):** Mark enemy, next 3 attacks auto-crit (cybernetic enhancement)

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Rapid Fire (6 shots, each 50% damage)
- **Tier 2:** Burnout (Overheat cybernetics for 500% melee strike + burn)
- **Tier 3:** Draconic Surge (Transform into half-dragon for 3 turns, all stats +50%)
- **Ultimate:** Omega Protocol (Unlock: Mentor's final fight)
  - Effect: Combine all weapons/tech/bloodline into single 1500% attack that ignores defense, resistance, immunity

**Weapon Types:** Guns (primary), Swords, Daggers
**Assigned Summon:** Bahamut (dragon-king, resonates with drake bloodline)

**Character Arc:**
- Starts: "I don't get attached. Attachments get you killed."
- Midgame: Realizes party is family, worth dying for
- Endgame: Chooses to save Nix over completing mission efficiently (growth)

# NIX-7 (The Android Child)

**Designation:** NIX-7 (Tactical Unit, Prototype Series)
**Apparent Age:** Teen-sized (13-15 equivalent)
**True Nature:** Hybrid—drake-core genetics + Foundry AI chassis

**Appearance:**
- Sleek white/silver plating with glowing blue seams
- Face has expressive LED eyes (can emote despite being android)
- Slight, non-threatening build (designed to be non-intimidating)
- Wears hooded cloak to hide android features in public

**Personality:**
- Awkward, learning emotions in real-time
- Curious about everything (annoying the party with questions)
- Deeply loyal to MC (first person to protect them)
- Terrified of being "property" again
- Develops sibling-like bonds with younger party members

**Capabilities:**
- **Lattice Interface:** Can recalibrate any terminal (overworld control, dungeon mechanisms)
- **Drake-Core Key:** Genetic pattern allows interaction with Foundations/Relics
- **Combat Role:** Support caster + debuffer
- **Unique Abilities:**
  1. **System Override (15 MP):** Disables one enemy's abilities for 3 turns
  2. **Emergency Reboot (40 MP):** Revive all KO'd allies with 25% HP (once per battle)

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Data Spike (hack enemy, reduce all stats by 20%)
- **Tier 2:** Lattice Pulse (AOE damage based on current terminal access level)
- **Tier 3:** Foundation Resonance (Channel nearest Foundation energy for massive elemental AOE)
- **Ultimate:** Recalibration (Unlock: Endgame quest)
  - Effect: Reset all enemies to base form (removes buffs, warping, enhancements), 100% accuracy

**Weapon Types:** Rods (for channeling), Staffs
**Assigned Summon:** Phoenix (rebirth theme, relates to Nix's fear of deletion)

**Character Arc:**
- Starts: "I am a tool. Tools do not feel."
- Midgame: "I... do not want to be erased. Is that fear? Is fear an emotion?"
- Endgame: "I choose to exist. I choose this family."

**Plot Function:**
- **MacGuffin:** Required to activate Omega Pedestals (midgame), reset world (endgame)
- **Moral Center:** Represents innocence party must protect
- **Tragic Weight:** Potential self-sacrifice in final choice

**Content Boundary Compliance:**
- Nix is never physically harmed on-screen (threats only)
- If captured, party rescues immediately (no prolonged torture scenes)
- Emotional distress allowed (fear, sadness), but no graphic violence toward child-coded character

# DRAKE-BEAST CIVILIZATION

## The Deepward (Sealed Underground)

**What It Is:**
- Ancient subterranean city-nation of drake-beasts
- Sealed themselves to prevent warping (they were first affected)
- Still alive, in stasis, slowly dying as Omega corruption reaches them

**Why They Sealed:**
- Progenitor Engine began whispering to them first (most genetically compatible)
- Drake-beasts realized they were being mutated
- Chose extinction over becoming monsters
- Uploaded collective memory/knowledge to Lattice as legacy

**The Lattice Connection:**
- Lattice = drake-beast final gift to future Orion inhabitants
- Contains all their science, history, culture
- Designed to maintain Foundations without direct intervention
- Corrupted when Progenitor gained access post-sealing

**Drake-Born (MC & Others):**
- Rare descendants of drake-beasts who interbred with humans before sealing
- Genetic markers allow interaction with Foundations/Lattice
- Dominion hunts them for this trait

**Potential Post-Game Content:**
- Party can unseal Deepward after Progenitor contained
- Drake-beasts revive, few survive, integrate with surface
- Teach party lost technologies

# CONTENT BOUNDARIES & TONE ENFORCEMENT

## Allowed Content

**Violence:**
- Gore allowed if story-relevant (monsters are grotesque, battles are bloody)
- Boss death scenes can be visceral (but not gratuitous)
- Example: Mentor dies with visible cybernetics sparking, blood visible

**Slavery:**
- Can appear in Dominion-controlled zones (prisoners, forced labor)
- **Mandatory:** Party must free the enslaved
- **Mandatory:** Captors must die or be imprisoned
- Never depicted as "acceptable" under any circumstance

**Profanity:**
- Allowed if not belligerent
- Characters can curse in frustration, fear, anger
- Avoid slurs, hate speech, excessive vulgarity

**Themes:**
- Tragedy (loss, sacrifice, moral compromise)
- War crimes (Dominion atrocities shown, condemned)
- Existential dread (Progenitor's cosmic indifference)

## Forbidden Content

**Child Harm:**
- **Nix is NEVER physically harmed on-screen**
- Threats to Nix allowed (creates tension)
- If Nix captured, rescue happens immediately (no prolonged torture)
- Other children in world (refugees, orphans) are protected by narrative
- Enemy child-soldiers only appear if party can save them (never kill children)

**Explicit Romance:**
- Only implied crushes (e.g., Scavenger blushes around Medic)
- No sex scenes, explicit content, or romance-focused plots
- Relationships are platonic, familial, or subtextual

**Sexual Violence:**
- Absolutely forbidden, no references, no implications

# NARRATIVE TONE GUIDELINES

## Space-Opera Frontier Aesthetic

**Visual References:**
- The Mandalorian (lived-in tech, lone wanderer)
- Firefly (frontier towns, scrappy survivors)
- Dune (ancient tech, desert wastes)
- Blade Runner (cyberpunk cities, neon + grime)

- Dialogue: Snappy, sarcastic, efficient (avoid purple prose)
- Descriptions: Gritty, tactile, sensory (rust, smoke, heat)

**A-Tier Tragedy (Personal Loss):**
- Mentor's forced transformation and death

- Progenitor as indifferent god-machine

- Drake-beasts' self-imposed extinction
- World-break showing how fragile civilization is

**Balancing Hope:**
- Party's found-family dynamic (light in darkness)
