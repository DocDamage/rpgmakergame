# ORION — PRE-SCRIPT GAME BIBLE (v1)

This document consolidates the locked canon and “before script” production specs for **Chroma’s Edge** (one-planet space-opera JRPG).

---

## 0) Non‑Negotiables
- **One planet only:** *Orion*. No spaceflight.
- **Tone:** gritty frontier + tragic weight; implied crushes are **comedic** (teasing/banter), not romance arcs.
- **Content boundaries:** no on-screen child harm; gore ok when story-necessary; if slavery appears, victims must be freed and captors killed; profanity as needed (not belligerent).

---

## 1) Core Myth & Stakes
### The Lattice
- Ancient planetary infrastructure network controlling **climate / transport / defenses / terraforming**.
- Accessed via **terminals** in the overworld and dungeons.

### The Progenitor Engine
- Buried machine-god superstructure intelligence.
- Manipulates via **dreams** and via **Omega pedestal proximity** (whispering).

### The 8 Foundations (Pieces of Reality)
1) Mass
2) Motion
3) Heat
4) Tide
5) Growth
6) Light
7) Shadow
8) Time

### Pedestals
- **Prime Pedestals (8):** correct placements that stabilize Orion.
- **Omega Pedestals (8):** separate ancient set, near-but-not the same locations. Seating relics here destabilizes Orion once activated.

### Monsters (Global Rule)
All monsters exist because Foundation instability causes warping:
- Warped wildlife
- Dominion constructs
- Cult-grown bioforms

---

## 2) Plot Structure (FF6-style midgame break)
### Act 1 — Hunt, Terminals, Relics
- Party learns terminals can talk to/command the Lattice.
- Dominion pursues relics under “make a better world” propaganda.

### Catastrophe (After Dungeon 4)
- Relics are seated into **Omega Pedestals** and **Nix** is used to activate them.
- Orion destabilizes: regions warp, monsters surge, Lattice behavior becomes hostile/erratic.

### Act 2 — Recovery & Restoration
- Party re-collects relics and re-seats them into **Prime Pedestals**.
- **Nix** performs the final stabilization/reset when all Prime placements are correct.

## 3) Factions

### Dominion (Triumvirate)
- Political head: **Arbiter (Lucien Marr)** — reformer-turned-believer
- Religious head: **Hierophant (Orin Vask)** — fanatical cult prophet
- Military head: **Marshal (Nadia Korr)** — career soldier, 30 years service
- Public goal: "remake Orion into a better world."
- True driver: hidden cult (Aurora Foundry) + Progenitor whispering.

**Marshal Nadia Korr's Arc (Key Story Detail):**
- Aurora Foundry implanted an **Omega-sigil neural tether** into Korr's command augments early in her career
- The Progenitor Engine whispers through this tether as "duty" and "orders" — Korr isn't evil, she's **conditioned**
- Acts 1–early 2: Korr hunts party hard, especially once Nix becomes known
- **Post-Dungeon 4 (World Break):** Korr "defects" and fights beside party for one act, but the tether keeps snapping her will back toward the Engine
- **The Break (Mid-Act 2):** Nix uses a Prime terminal recalibration to **burn the Omega signal out of Korr's implants**
- **Full Freedom:** Korr regains full agency and becomes **Playable Party Member #13** — uses command codes, tactics, and insider Dominion knowledge to dismantle the Triumvirate from within and reach the Progenitor Engine
- Character arc: from puppet soldier → reluctant defector → fully liberated warrior seeking redemption

### Aurora Foundry (Hidden Cult)
- Belief: the Progenitor is a **teacher** that will perfect evolution.
- Operates inside Dominion. Builds/augments weapons, constructs, and cult bioforms.

### Ironhawk Syndicate (Bounty Guild)
- Early: rival pressure on Kade.
- Midgame: flips to pragmatic ally after the world breaks.

## 5) Boss Ladder

### 8 Foundation Lieutenants (Dungeon Endbosses)
One lieutenant per dungeon, themed to a Foundation. Each is a primary relic gatekeeper.

### Tower Captains (Non-lieutenant elites)
Named Dominion/Cult elites placed in the tower as escalation and setpieces (no Foundation requirement).

### Personal Antagonists (Non-Lieutenant Story Bosses)
In addition to the 8 Foundation Lieutenants and Tower Captains, the following antagonists appear as recurring story bosses tied to specific character arcs:

**JASPER VOS (Enforcer-Prime)**
- Personal antagonist to Kade (his mentor-turned-weapon)
- Appears as: ICEBOX ambush (Part 3, Scene 32), Tower Floor 58 glitch encounter (Part 10), Final Palace confrontation
- Status: Dominion-controlled cyborg with neural implants, tragic arc
- Resolution: Kade breaks neural link; Vos sacrifices himself to weaken Progenitor Engine
- Thematic significance: Explores themes of identity, control, and redemption

**ELLIS VARN (Light Foundation Lieutenant → Defector)**
- Dungeon 3 (Crystal Caverns) boss, later becomes temporary ally
- Boss fight mechanics: Blinding flashes, laser beams, light constructs, weakness to shadow
- Defection: After defeat, discovers Light Foundation rejected him; seeks redemption by helping party
- Story function: Demonstrates that some Dominion operatives can change sides; provides insider knowledge

## 6) Gear & Progression
### Weapons
- Guns behave like swords (no ammo management).
- Core weapon families: blasters, gunblades, rifles, heavy rifles, daggers, staves/rods, fists.

### 5 Gear Tiers (Material-Based Progression)
- **Tier 1: Scrap** (Levels 1–15) — basic stats, no special effects
- **Tier 2: Common** (Levels 15–40) — 10% chance for minor effect
- **Tier 3: Rare** (Levels 40–80) — guaranteed special effect
- **Tier 4: Epic** (Levels 80–150) — two special effects
- **Tier 5: Legendary** (Levels 150–255) — three special effects + elemental property

**Gear Tier Mapping to Story Progression:**
- Players acquire **Tier 1–2 gear** in Act 1 (early dungeons)
- **Tier 3–4 gear** unlocked after Dungeon 4 world break
- **Tier 5 (Mythic)** represents the ultimate/legendary weapons earned in NG+ or Tower endgame

### Equipment Slots & Stats
- **Head, Body, Arms/Legs, Accessory, Necklace** (5 armor slots)
- Each slot contributes DEF, MDEF, resistances, and special effects
- **Weapon Properties:** Base ATK/MAG, Element, Critical Rate, Special Effects (Lifesteal, Manaburn, Counterattack, Double Strike, Armor Pierce, Spellblade, Vampire, etc.)

## 7) Relationship & Affinity System

### Tone
- Pair dialogue and affinity scenes are allowed.
- Any crush energy must read **comedic** (banter, teasing, awkward moments), never explicit romance.

### Mechanics (Full Affinity System)
- **Affinity Gains:** Characters build bond levels by fighting together in battles
- **Affinity Levels:** 1 (Strangers) → 10 (Bonded)
- **Dialogue Scenes:** Unlock at Affinity milestones (Levels 1, 3, 5, 7, 10)
- **Max Affinity (Level 10):** Unlocks **Dual Limit Break** (combined ultimate attack, 2000% damage + unique effects)

### Affinity Stat Bonuses
- **Level 3:** +5% all stats when both characters in active party
- **Level 5:** Support ability unlocks (character auto-assists partner once per battle)
- **Level 7:** +10% all stats when both in party
- **Level 10:** Dual Limit Break unlocked (can only be used when both characters have full Limit gauge)

### Dual Limit Break Examples
- **Fire Mage + Ice Mage:** "Steam Explosion" (water AOE damage + burn)
- **Tank + Healer:** "Guardian's Blessing" (tank invincible + full party heal)
- **Rogue + Ranger:** "Rapid Assault" (10 hits to random targets)
- **Kade + Callum (both drake-touched):** "Draconic Ascension" (3000% AOE + party gains elemental immunity 3 turns)

### Pair Combinations
- **Total Possible Pairs:** 78 (13 choose 2)
- Each pair has unique dialogue, comedic moments, and a signature Dual Limit Break
- Affinity is tracked **per-party composition** (building the same pair in different battles stacks progress)
