# Chroma's Edge — Character & Sprite Master List

## Pixel Art Shot Sheet (v1.0)

---

## 0) Technical Specs

* **Grid:** 24×32 px (Standard Character) / Larger for Bosses
* **Angles:** 4-Directional (Down, Left, Right, Up)
* **Animation Cycles:**
  * **Idle:** 4 frames (Breathing, bobbing)
  * **Walk:** 4–6 frames (Leg extension, arm sway)
  * **Action/Attack:** 3–5 frames (Punch, shoot, cast)
  * **Downed/KO:** 1 frame (Lying on ground)

---
