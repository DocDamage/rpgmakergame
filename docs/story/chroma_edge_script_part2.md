Chroma's Edge — Script Draft (Part 2: D2–D4 + World Break)

SCENE 008 — "THE SWAMP TALKS" — Fungal Depths Approach — Day

[CAM: low, tracking boots through wet moss. The air is thick. Tiny motes drift like spores.]

RENNA
You ever notice how swamps are just… the planet's armpit?

KADE
Keep talking and I'll test if you float.

[Nix pauses. Her glow-lines dim like a system throttling.]

NIX-7
This region's Motion frequency is unstable.

RENNA
Translation?

NIX-7
The wind is wrong.

[A refugee wagon crawls past. A tired woman stares at Nix like she's seeing a ghost.]

REFUGEE WOMAN
(half to herself)
Everybody's been dreamin' the same dream. A voice under the dirt.
Makes you wake up with blood in your mouth.

KADE
You hear that, Renna? Planet's got opinions.

RENNA
Yeah. And apparently bad breath.

[Cutaway: On a ridge, Nadia Korr watches through a scope. Her finger rests on the trigger… then lifts away. She exhales like it hurts. A faint, unnatural pulse runs through her augments.]

KORR (V.O.)
Find the asset. Contain the interface.
…Execute.

[She clenches her jaw—like the last word wasn't hers.]

SCENE 009 — "SAINT MARIN" — Hidden Clinic (Fungal Depths) — Late Day

[The "clinic" is a burrow carved into rock and root. Cots. Lanterns. A sterile corner that's trying its best.]

[Nix stumbles—knees buckle. Her glow-lines flicker hard, then dim to almost nothing.]

RENNA
Oh no—no no no—don't you dare blue-screen on me now.

KADE
Where's your miracle doctor?

[A figure steps out from behind a curtain: DR. MARIN SURESH. Mid-30s. Eyes like she hasn't slept since the last war.]

SURESH
If you're bleeding, sit. If you're dying, lie.
If you're dramatic, do it quietly.

[She sees Nix and instantly shifts into professional focus.]

SURESH
Put her on the table.

RENNA
Her who—yeah, okay. Table. Got it.

[Suresh opens a diagnostic kit. Not Dominion standard—stolen and rebuilt.]

SURESH
(to Nix, gentle but firm)
I'm Marin. I'm going to fix what they did.
You're going to let me.

NIX-7
I do not… have a choice.

SURESH
You always have a choice. Sometimes they're just all terrible.

[Suresh works fast. Her hands don't shake, but her voice does—barely.]

SCENE 010 — "THE PURITY DIRECTIVE" — Clinic Back Room — Night

[The clinic quiets. Rain. Distant insect calls. The party eats whatever passes for stew.]

KADE
Dominion's going to keep coming. For her. For you.

SURESH
(flat)
I know.

RENNA
Why help us then? You could've kicked us out and saved yourself the trouble.

SURESH
Because I spent eight years hurting people.
I have a lot to make up for. 

[Nix looks at Suresh like she's trying to file the concept under "human logic."]

NIX-7
You are… protecting me.

SURESH
Don't make it weird. I'm protecting my conscience. You're collateral.

KADE
So you're coming with us.

SURESH
I'm not a fighter.

RENNA
Doc—respectfully? Everybody's a fighter now.

[Suresh's mouth twitches—almost a smile, but it dies before it becomes one.]

SURESH
Fine.
But if any of you die, I'm billing your ghosts.

SCENE 011 — "DOWN INTO MOTION" — Dungeon 2: Fungal Depths — Morning

[Dungeon vibe: bioluminescent fungi, air currents that shouldn't exist underground, spores moving like they're directed.]

NIX-7
Motion Foundation saturation is high.

KADE
Meaning?

SURESH
Meaning things that should be slow… won't be.

RENNA
Oh good. Murder, but faster.

[BATTLE TRIGGER: Dungeon 2 — first encounter wave]

SCENE 012 — "KINETIC SWARM" — Fungal Depths Core — Later

[The cavern opens into a vertical space. Wind whips in circles. Spores spiral up like a cyclone.]

[SFX: hundreds of wings. Not random—synchronized.]

RENNA
That's… a lot of birds.

SURESH
Those aren't birds.

[A tornado-shaped silhouette forms out of bird/insect-like bodies—perfect geometry. A hive mind.]

NIX-7
Telepathic link detected.

KADE
Dominion did this?

SURESH
Dominion does everything it can't afford to admit.
[BATTLE TRIGGER: Boss — KINETIC SWARM]

SCENE 013 — "FLUX CATALYST" — Fungal Depths Exit — After Boss

[The swarm collapses into drifting ash-feathers. The wind dies. Something heavy settles in the air.]

[In the center: a device like a gyroscope made of crystal and metal—spinning without touching anything.]

[SYSTEM POPUP: RELIC ACQUIRED — FLUX CATALYST]
RENNA
Okay, that's… objectively cool.

[Nix holds it and her glow-lines pulse in sync with its spin.]

NIX-7
This is a shard of Motion's seat.

SURESH
And the Dominion turned the Foundation into a weapon.
(quiet)
Again.

KADE
We move. Crystal Caverns next.

SCENE 014 — "IRONHAWK SNARE" — Swamp Road — Same Day

[The path narrows. Fog thickens. A metallic click.]

RENNA
…That click was not nature.

[Net-launchers snap out. Smoke bombs. Shapes in scavenged armor—IRONHAWK SYNDICATE.]

IRONHAWK CAPTAIN
Kade. Heard you found yourself a rare piece of "property."
We're here to collect.

KADE
I'm gonna guess "please" isn't in your vocabulary.

IRONHAWK CAPTAIN
Not for free.

[Two Ironhawks grab Suresh from behind.]

SURESH
—Hey—!

RENNA
Oh hell no. That's our doctor.

IRONHAWK CAPTAIN
She's worth a bonus. Dominion pays for loose ends.
[BATTLE TRIGGER: Ironhawk Ambush]

[After the fight, Suresh stands there breathing hard, shocked she's still upright.]

SURESH
…So that's what it feels like to fight back.

KADE
Welcome to the crew.

SURESH
Don't get sentimental. I'm still charging your ghosts.

SCENE 015 — "THE CAVERNS THAT WATCH BACK" — Crystal Caverns Approach — Dusk

[Mountains. Thin air. The rock face glows faintly from within.]

NIX-7
Light Foundation resonance is abnormal.

RENNA
You sure you don't just mean "it's spooky"?

SURESH
Both can be true.

[Cutaway: Korr again—closer now. She's alone. She lowers her rifle, staring at her own trembling hand like it disgusts her.]

KORR (V.O.)
Why won't you just run?

[She says it like she's begging them.]

SCENE 016 — "COMMANDER ELLIS VARN" — Dungeon 3: Crystal Caverns — Later

[The party enters a chamber of mirrored crystal. A man waits in Dominion officer whites—glowing from the inside like a lantern filled with rage.]

ELLIS VARN
You're trespassing on Dominion reclamation.
Kneel. Hand over the interface.
I will be merciful.

RENNA
Merciful? You're literally a walking flashbang.

VARN
I volunteered for enlightenment.
You cling to filth.

KADE
You glow because you're radioactive. Not because you're holy.
[BATTLE TRIGGER: Boss — COMMANDER ELLIS VARN]

SCENE 017 — "TAMING UNLOCKS" — Crystal Caverns — After Boss

[Varn drops to one knee. The light in him sputters like a dying sun.]

VARN
(hoarse)
You… don't understand what the Triumvirate is preventing.

SURESH
Oh, I understand.
They're preventing you from admitting you're a monster.

[Nix steps forward, looking past Varn to the cavern's terminal—like it's calling her.]

[A sound echoes through the cavern—not hostile. Curious. A creature emerges from the crystal shadows: a LUMINOUS STRIDER, built like a deer made of light and stone, eyes intelligent and wary.]

RENNA
Uh… guys? Wildlife.

KADE
Not attacking?

[The Strider approaches Nix slowly, drawn to her stable Light resonance.]

[It circles once around the party, pausing at each person like it is taking measurements.]

SOVA
It is mapping intent, not scent.
If we panic, it bolts.

GRIT
Then don't panic.

NIX-7
Light frequency stabilized.
The creatures… can sense balance now.

[SYSTEM POPUP: BEAST AFFINITY UNLOCKED]
[SYSTEM POPUP: NEW ABILITY — TAMING]
TUTORIAL: Creatures affected by Foundation instability can now be calmed and tamed when their HP is low and Foundation energy is stable.

SURESH
So we can… befriend monsters now?

NIX-7
Not monsters.
Creatures. The instability made them hostile.
Stability makes them… negotiable.

RENNA
Wait—so killing Mister High-Beams unlocked pet adoption?

KADE
Planet's teaching us tricks.

[The Luminous Strider kneels, offering itself.]

[The Strider lowers its head under Nix's hand, then turns and offers a shoulder-height step to the group.]

[SYSTEM POPUP: MOUNT BOND STARTED — Luminous Strider (Light Attuned)
]
[SYSTEM POPUP: NEW COMMANDS — PET STANCES (Assist / Guard / Search)
]

[SYSTEM POPUP: TUTORIAL — Use TAMING STONE on weakened creatures (below 30% HP) to attempt to tame them. Tamed creatures can serve as MOUNTS (travel) or PETS (combat companions).]

RENNA
Do we… keep it?
Also, can I name it?

KADE
(to the Strider)
You want to help?

[The Strider's glow brightens once—affirmation.]

KADE
Then welcome to the chaos.

[SYSTEM POPUP: TAMED — LUMINOUS STRIDER (Common Land Mount)]
MOUNT REGISTERED: Luminous Strider (Speed 1.5×, Stamina 60s, Light-aligned)

RENNA
Okay. That's officially the second-coolest thing that's happened today.
(beat)
Can I ride it?

KADE
Later. Move.

[Renna gets exactly three seconds in the saddle before Kade points down the corridor.]

RENNA
Worth it.

SCENE 018 — "RADIANT BEACON" — Crystal Caverns Reliquary — Continuous

[On a pedestal of crystal sits a shard so bright it hurts to look at—Light made solid.]

[SYSTEM POPUP: RELIC ACQUIRED — RADIANT BEACON]
[Nix holds it. Her glow-lines flare—then a whisper rides through the light.]

UNKNOWN SIGNAL (V.O.)
Bring it… to me.

[Nix's face tightens—pain, recognition.]

NIX-7
It is… the same voice.

SURESH
Whose voice?

NIX-7
The one under the planet.

KADE
Then we stop collecting shiny rocks for strangers.
We figure out who's steering the whole damn chase.

SCENE 019 — "SKYSPIRE" — Badlands Road — Next Morning

[The landscape turns red. Heat haze. A temple spire in the distance like a blade stuck into the earth.]

RENNA
So. Skyspire Temple. Foundation of Heat.
What could go wrong?

KADE
Everything.

SCENE 020 — "THE TEMPLE UNDER THE TEMPLE" — Dungeon 4: Skyspire Temple — Later

[Inside: basalt halls, molten veins behind walls like the building is alive. The deeper they go, the more the floor vibrates.]

SURESH
This place feels like a fever dream.

NIX-7
It is… a pressure point.
There is a terminal below the altar.

KADE
Of course there is.

SCENE 021 — "INFERNUS PRIME" — Skyspire Inner Sanctum — Boss

INFERNUS PRIME
(voice like stones in a furnace)
SEAT… THE HEAT…
FEED… THE ENGINE…

RENNA
It talks. I hate it.

SURESH
That's not speech. That's programming.

KADE
Then we rewrite it.

[BATTLE TRIGGER: Boss — INFERNUS PRIME]

SCENE 022 — "THE ARBITER" — Skyspire Altar — After Boss

[Infernus Prime collapses, magma hardening into black stone.]

[Slow clap. Echoing.]

[ARBITER LUCIEN MARR steps forward with elite Dominion forces. Clean coat. Clean smile. Eyes like paperwork.]

MARR
Well done.
You collected everything exactly as predicted.

KADE
Who the hell are you?

MARR
Arbiter Lucien Marr.
And you—
(to Nix)
—are the key that refuses to behave.

[His soldiers raise rifles. Marr gestures casually—like this is a formality.]

MARR
You've been herded.
Those relics in your pack? They're not "saving" anything.
They're the wrong seats.
RENNA
No. No, no—those relics are ours.

MARR
They were always mine.

[Soldiers seize the relic case. Kade lunges—barrels level at his head.]

SURESH
Kade—!

[Marr turns to a hidden mechanism. The altar slides, revealing an elevator shaft plunging into darkness.]

MARR
Come.
We're going to the Burning Pit.

SCENE 023 — "LATTICE RESET INITIATED" — Burning Pit (Omega Pedestal) — Minutes Later

[The world below is a cavern of heat and black metal. In the center: an ancient terminal—too old to be "built," more like it grew.]

[Nix stares at it like it's staring back.]

NIX-7
I will not—

[Marr's pistol presses to the side of her head. Cold metal against warm skin.]

MARR
You will.

KADE
Touch her and—

MARR
And what?
You'll die angry. A classic.

[Nix steps forward. Her glow-lines ignite violently as she places a hand on the terminal.]

[The terminal responds—LOUD. Planet-deep.]

TERMINAL (V.O.)
—INTERFACE CONFIRMED—
—OMEGA SEATING: ACCEPTED—

[Nix's voice changes—like someone else is speaking through her.]

NIX-7
(flat, forced)
Lattice reset initiated… 
[The cavern shakes. The air splits—a seam through reality.]

PROGENITOR ENGINE (V.O.)
RESET COMMENCING. 

SCENE 024 — "WORLD BREAK" — Across Orion — Montage

[VFX MONTAGE: earthquakes; a coastline folding inward; an island sinking; a mountain erupting where flatland was; early monsters warping into wrong shapes.]

[In towns: people screaming, praying, laughing hysterically.]

[In the sky: faint lattice-lines glowing like veins.]

PROGENITOR ENGINE (V.O.)
LATTICE RESET INITIATED. 

SCENE 025 — "CAPTURE" — Burning Pit — Continuous

[Smoke. Falling debris.]

[Kade fights through soldiers, reaching for Nix—]

[Dominion shock-cuffs snap onto Nix's wrists. Her glow-lines flicker, stuttering.]

NIX-7
KADE—

[They drag her away into a dropship's shadow.]

KADE
NIX!

[The ship lifts. Gone.]

SCENE 026 — "KORR, BLEEDING" — Shattered Badlands — Night

[Everything is different now. The map is wrong.]

[Kade staggers through ash and broken rock. Finds Korr alone—on her knees, bleeding, weapon beside her like she doesn't trust herself to hold it.]

KADE
…You.

[Korr looks up. No squad. No orders. Just a person who's been used too long.]

KORR
I tried to stop it.

KADE
You helped it happen.

[Korr flinches—like a collar yanked tight.]

KORR
I didn't have a choice.

RENNA
(off to the side, furious)
Everybody has a choice!

KORR
Not when the tether is screaming in your skull.

[Kade's hands shake with rage and grief.]

KADE
They took her.
I swear—
I'm going to break her link… and yours.

[Beat. Korr laughs once—small and broken.]

KORR
Then you'll be the first person who ever tried.
[CAM: pull up. The sky's lattice-lines glow faintly over a wounded planet.]

END ACT 1 STINGER.
