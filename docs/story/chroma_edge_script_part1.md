Chroma's Edge — Script Draft (Part 1: Ashveil Opening)

SCENE 000 — "THE LATTICE DREAM" — Nowhere — Night

[VFX: total black. faint geometric lines like a planetary circuit map. the "Lattice" pulses.]

PROGENITOR (V.O.)
(soft, ancient, too calm)
Orion is unstable.

[The lines form eight symbols. They flicker like failing neon.]

PROGENITOR (V.O.)
Mass. Motion. Heat. Tide. Growth. Light. Shadow. Time.
Pieces of reality… mis-seated.

[SFX: a distant metal choir. a whisper threading through it.]

PROGENITOR (V.O.)
A reset is not cruelty.
It is maintenance.

[FLASH—Nix's face for half a second: glowing seam-lines, eyes wide, not breathing—then gone.]

PROGENITOR (V.O.)
Interface… detected.

[CUT TO BLACK.]

SCENE 001 — "THE CONTRACT" — Dusthaven Cantina — Late Evening

[Location vibe: gritty frontier bar, patched metal walls, a jukebox that crackles more than it plays.]

[Kade sits alone. Gunblade leaning on the table. He's calm like someone who's already survived the worst day of his life.]

[An Ironhawk Syndicate rep slides into the booth: IRONHAWK BROKER (face hidden, voice smooth).]

IRONHAWK BROKER
Kade. Word is you don't miss.

KADE
Word is people talk too much.

IRONHAWK BROKER
(smiles)
Straight line, then. Dominion wants their property back. "Stolen."
You retrieve. You get paid. You walk.

KADE
What kind of property runs?

IRONHAWK BROKER
That's above my pay grade. It's moving through the Ashveil ruins.
You'll know it when you see it.

[Kade's hand rests near his weapon, more habit than threat.]

KADE
Dominion doesn't outsource unless they're embarrassed.

IRONHAWK BROKER
Or desperate.
(slides a chip)
Half now. Half when you deliver.

[Kade pockets the chip without looking at it.]

KADE
If Dominion's involved, I charge double.

IRONHAWK BROKER
Then consider this… a "desperation bonus."

[CAM: Kade's eyes catch a flicker on the cantina's busted screen—Dominion broadcast: "Public safety advisory. Dangerous fugitive asset. Report immediately."]

KADE
(mutters)
Yeah. Embarrassed.

SCENE 002 — "ASHVEIL CONTACT" — Ruins of Ashveil (Outskirts) — Night

[Winds. Dead trees. Stone ribs of ancient structures. The air feels slightly wrong.]

[Kade moves through rubble. His tracker pings.]

[SFX: faint electronic chirp. then… something else: a soft humming, like a song stuck in machinery.]

[Kade rounds a broken archway and sees her:]

NIX-7 — half-buried under collapsed plating, glowing seam-lines dim, movements precise but exhausted.

[She turns her head toward Kade. Not fear. Assessment.]

NIX-7
You are not Dominion.

KADE
Depends who's asking.

NIX-7
(stares)
You smell like oil and gunpowder.
And… old blood.

[Kade stiffens. He does not like being read that easily.]

KADE
What are you?

NIX-7
Nix-7.
I am… an interface.

[Before Kade can respond—]

[SFX: dropships in the distance. Searchlights combing ruins.]

DOMINION VOICE (O.S.)
Asset located. Containment squad, advance!

[Kade swears under his breath.]

KADE
(to Nix)
Can you move?

NIX-7
Yes.
(pauses)
But I am damaged.

[Kade reaches down—hesitates, then offers a hand.]

KADE
Then don't make me regret this.

[Nix takes his hand. Her grip is strong. Too strong.]

SCENE 003 — "THE HUNT BEGINS" — Ashveil Ruins — Continuous

[Searchlights sweep. Dominion boots pound stone.]

[BATTLE TRIGGER: Tutorial Fight — Dominion Scout Squad]

DOMINION SCOUT CAPTAIN
By order of the Triumvirate—hand over the asset and you walk away.

KADE
Nah.

[He draws the gunblade. Nix's seam-lines brighten as if reacting to danger.]

NIX-7
Your probability of survival increases if you do not stand still.

KADE
Noted.

[Combat. Kade is brutal and efficient. Nix moves like a dancer with a calculator brain.]

[After the last scout drops—]

[SFX: a new channel cuts in, colder, higher authority.]

UNKNOWN DOMINION COMMS (V.O.)
Marshal Korr. Confirm visual.

[On a distant ridge: a silhouette with a heavy rifle and command posture. NADIA KORR. She doesn't fire. She watches.]

KORR (distant, to comms)
Visual confirmed.

[Beat. Korr's jaw tightens like she's biting back a thought.]

KORR
(low)
…Orders acknowledged.

[CAM PUSH IN: for a frame, a faint Omega-like flicker across her augments—then gone.]

SCENE 004 — "RENNA'S GARAGE" — Dusthaven Outskirts (Scrap Garage) — Later

[The "garage" is a ship container welded to another ship container. Tools everywhere. A hand-painted sign: IF IT'S BROKE, I CAN MAKE IT WORSE.]

RENNA KYTE is under a half-dismantled bike, humming.

RENNA
If you're here to complain about my rates, I charge extra for whining.

KADE
I need a patch job. Fast.

[Renna slides out, wipes grease on her pants, and sees Nix. Her expression changes instantly—curiosity, then protective instinct.]

RENNA
Oh.
…You're pretty.

KADE
Don't flirt with the robot.

RENNA
I wasn't flirting. I was… appreciating design choices.
(leans closer to Nix)
Who did this to you?

NIX-7
The Dominion.

RENNA
Yeah, that tracks.
(to Kade)
You bring Dominion trouble to my door, you're buying me a new door.

KADE
Deal.

[Renna opens a panel on Nix's arm. The glow stutters.]

RENNA
Okay. You're not just "damaged."
You've got Lattice burn in your conduits.
That's… not supposed to happen.

[Nix's voice drops to almost a whisper.]

NIX-7
They tried to make me open something.
I refused.

RENNA
Good. Keep refusing.
(to Kade, firm)
I'm coming with you.

KADE
That wasn't part of the—

RENNA
Kade, sweetie, if Dominion's chasing her, they'll burn half this town to get her.
I live here.
So now it's part of the plan.

[KADE exhales, defeated.]

KADE
Fine. But you listen when I say run.

RENNA
I'm great at running. I just usually run toward explosions.

SCENE 005 — "THE FIRST TERMINAL" — Ruins of Ashveil (Inner Gate) — Dawn

[Renna, Kade, Nix enter deeper. An ancient terminal rises from the floor like a black obelisk. It hums.]

[The air gets colder around it, like the world is holding its breath.]

RENNA
That's not Dominion tech.

KADE
Then what is it?

NIX-7
The Lattice.
This planet's bones.

[Nix steps forward. Her seam-lines flare bright.]

KADE
Hey—don't just—

[Nix places her hand on the terminal.]

[SFX: a chord like the planet itself is being tuned.]

[VFX: geometric light spreads outward across the ground, forming a map grid.]

TERMINAL (V.O.)
—INTERFACE DETECTED—
—ACCESS REQUEST—

RENNA
(stunned)
It's talking to you.

KADE
Nix—can you understand it?

NIX-7
Yes.

[Beat. Nix's eyes flicker like she's reading something too large to fit inside her skull.]

NIX-7
It is… asking why the relic is unseated.

KADE
What relic?

[A pulse from the terminal answers by projection: a seed-shaped crystal. PRIMAL SEED.]
TERMINAL (V.O.)
—STABILITY: FAILING—
—FOUNDATION: GROWTH—
—CORRECTION REQUIRED—

RENNA
So… we find the seed thing, we put it where it goes, and we stop the planet from breaking?

KADE
Sounds too clean.

NIX-7
Nothing is clean.

[The terminal suddenly emits a second signal—faint, like a whisper riding the system.]

UNKNOWN SIGNAL (V.O.)
(soft)
Bring it to me.

[Kade snaps his head around. No one else reacts.]

KADE
…Did you hear that?

RENNA
Hear what?

[Nix goes very still.]

NIX-7
I heard it.

SCENE 006 — "THE BLOOM" — Ruins of Ashveil (Relic Chamber) — Morning

[The chamber is half garden, half cathedral. Vines crawl over stone statues that look older than language.]

[In the center: a crystalline plant mass blooming out of a cracked pedestal. It breathes. It learns.]

RENNA
That is… absolutely not a normal plant.

KADE
Nothing here is normal.

[The creature unfurls. THE BLOOM.]

[BATTLE TRIGGER: Boss — THE BLOOM]
THE BLOOM (V.O.)
(in layered voices, human-ish)
UNSEATED.
UNMADE.
RETURN… THE PIECE…

NIX-7
It is not speaking.
It is echoing.

KADE
Either way, it's in our way.

SCENE 007 — "PRIMAL SEED ACQUIRED" — Ashveil Exit — After Boss

[The Bloom collapses into petals that evaporate into glittering dust.]

[At the center remains the relic: PRIMAL SEED—organic crystal, tiny vines sprouting and retracting.]

RENNA
Okay. We got your magic seed.

KADE
Don't call it that.

RENNA
You're the one making it weird.

[Nix holds the relic near her chest. Her glow calms, but her expression is troubled.]

NIX-7
This is not the right route.

KADE
What does that mean?

NIX-7
There are… two sets of seats.
Two intentions.
RENNA
Can you tell which is which?

NIX-7
Not yet.

[SFX: distant dropship engines. Closer now.]

KADE
Then we don't stop here. We move.
We find someone who can translate your "two intentions" into something we can shoot.

RENNA
I know a doctor who hates Dominion more than she hates sleep.
If she's still alive, she'll help.

[Nix looks back once at the ruins.]

NIX-7
The planet is dreaming.

KADE
Then let's wake it up—carefully.

[CUT.]
