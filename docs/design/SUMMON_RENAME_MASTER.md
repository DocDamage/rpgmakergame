## Thematic Alignment

### Foundation Mapping:
- **Mass:** Tremor (Grit), Agonis (Petra)
- **Motion:** Voltaris (Renna), Zephyros (Ashka)
- **Heat:** Pyraxis (Senna), Ashborn (Callum)
- **Tide:** Marinus (Suresh)
- **Growth:** *(No direct summon)*
- **Light:** Aegis (Vex)
- **Shadow:** Mortis (Twist), Nihilus (Sova)
- **Time:** Glaciem (Nix), Ashborn (Callum - rebirth theme)
- **Authority/Freedom:** Liberator (Korr)

### Visual/Thematic Descriptions:

**DRAKONIS** (Kade)
- Form: Massive draconic entity, scales of obsidian and crimson, wings that tear the sky
- Voice: Thunder and dominance
- Power: Raw destruction, dragon fire, sovereign fury

**GLACIEM** (Nix-7)
- Form: Mechanized ice construct, crystalline plating, drake-tech frostframe
- Voice: Cold calculation, precise
- Power: Preservation through stasis, ice without cruelty

**VOLTARIS** (Renna)
- Form: Living lightning storm, crackling energy bound to humanoid shape
- Voice: Electric hum, controlled chaos
- Power: Engineering the storm, lightning bowing to understanding

**MARINUS** (Suresh)
- Form: Serpentine water guardian, crown-finned, ancient ocean embodiment
- Voice: Tidal depth, truthful
- Power: Healing tides, water that holds and mends

**MORTIS** (Twist)
- Form: Shadowed reaper, mounted on spectral steed, blade of absolute silence
- Voice: Whisper of ending
- Power: One strike, one death, perfect assassination

**NIHILUS** (Sova)
- Form: Void entity, horned and winged, entropy with consciousness
- Voice: The sound of stars dying
- Power: Knowledge without hope, gravity of absolute truth

**TREMOR** (Grit)
- Form: Mountain given form, obsidian and ancient stone, immovable presence
- Voice: Earth's deep rumble
- Power: Earthshaking force, vanguard immovable

**ZEPHYROS** (Ashka)
- Form: Wind embodied, feathered wings of gale, untamed sky
- Voice: Howling freedom
- Power: Scout eternal, witness to all horizons, never caught

**PYRAXIS** (Senna)
- Form: Living flame, humanoid silhouette of roaring fire, inner heat unleashed
- Voice: Crackling defiance
- Power: Pain transmuted to power, burning without consumption

**ASHBORN** (Callum)
- Form: Phoenix of ash and flame, wings eternally burning and reforming
- Voice: Renewal's promise
- Power: Death refused, rebirth as bloodline inheritance

**AGONIS** (Petra)
- Form: Twisted mass of shadow and mutation, beautiful in its horror
- Voice: Suffering acknowledged
- Power: Burden chosen and weaponized, pain as identity

**AEGIS** (Vex)
- Form: Fortress of light and faith, armored colossus of divine protection
- Voice: Oath unbroken
- Power: Shield eternal, line held forever, holy bastion

**LIBERATOR** (Korr) - NEW
- Form: War marshal of light and steel, broken chains as armor, authority reclaimed
- Voice: Command without tether
- Power: Freedom weaponized, chains shattered, authority self-determined
