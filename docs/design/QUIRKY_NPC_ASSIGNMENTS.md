## 💡 USAGE TIPS

1. **Scale Matters:** These are 3x scale - perfect for RMMV/MZ
2. **Animations:** Most include idle + 4-directional walk
3. **Color Variants:** Many have multiple color swaps - use for variety
4. **Shadow Versions:** Some have shadow variants for lighting effects
5. **Mix with Serious NPCs:** Balance quirky with standard NPCs for tone variety
