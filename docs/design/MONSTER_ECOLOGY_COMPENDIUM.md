## 🌍 ECOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK

### Monster Tiers:
- **Critter:** Weak, ambient (levels 1-10)
- **Hunter:** Standard combat (levels 10-30)
- **Predator:** Dangerous (levels 30-50)
- **Apex:** Zone boss-tier (levels 50-70)
- **Legendary:** Unique, quest-related (any level)

### Monster Types:
- **Beast:** Animal-based, natural
- **Elemental:** Magic-formed
- **Undead:** Reanimated dead
- **Void:** Corruption-spawned
- **Mechanical:** Constructed
- **Humanoid:** Sentient enemies
