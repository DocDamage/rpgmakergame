## 📖 EXPANDED STORY ARCS

### NEW ARC 1: "The Beast War" (Levels 15-25)
**Trigger:** After completing first 3 zones

**Premise:**
The Beast Tribes (from beast_tribes assets) are migrating due to the Extinguisher's corruption spreading. Normally peaceful, they're now raiding human settlements. The party must:
1. Investigate the migration
2. Discover the corruption source
3. Choose: Help the beasts find new home, or drive them out
4. **Consequence:** Affects which beast tribes ally with you in final battle

**New Areas:**
- **Beast Haven** (hidden valley) - Neutral ground
- **Corrupted Burrows** - Dungeon where corruption originates
- **The Great Migration Path** - Overworld route

**Key NPCs:**
- **Chief Thunder-Hide** (Bear tribe leader)
- **Swift-Claw** (Wolf tribe scout, recruitable)
- **Old Mother Scale** (Lizard tribe matriarch, has prophecy)

**Boss:** **Corruption Beast Alpha** - Former beast tribe leader, now monster

---

### NEW ARC 2: "The Time Fracture" (Levels 35-45)
**Trigger:** After Chrono zone, before Capital

**Premise:**
Using the Chrono assets fully - time is fracturing. The party encounters:
- Past versions of themselves
- Future versions showing dark outcomes
- **The Paradox Merchant** - Sells items from alternate timelines
- Areas that phase between past/present/future

**Mechanic:** Time Crystals - collect to stabilize areas, wrong choices cause time loops (puzzle)

**New Areas:**
- **The Shattered Hourglass** - Dungeon where time flows backwards
- **Echo Ruins** - Capital as it was 100 years ago
- **Future's End** - Capital if Extinguisher wins

**Key NPCs:**
- **Paradox Jones** - Time merchant (recurring character)
- **Young Aurora** - Extinguisher before corruption (can be saved?)
- **Elder Kade** - Future version who failed

**Boss:** **The Paradox Itself** - Living timeline contradiction

---

### NEW ARC 3: "The Void's Children" (Levels 50-60)
**Trigger:** After Void zone discovery

**Premise:**
The Void isn't empty - it's where failed timelines go. The Void's Children are beings who remember being real. They offer:
- Forbidden knowledge about Extinguisher's true weakness
- Power at a cost (corruption mechanic)
- A way to save Aurora without killing her

**Moral Choice:** Use Void power (risk corruption) or find another way?

**New Areas:**
- **The Remembered City** - Buildings from erased timelines
- **Palace of Lost Things** - Where forgotten items go
- **The Void's Heart** - Where the first Extinguishing began

**Key NPCs:**
- **The Hollow King** - Ruler of Void's Children
- **Whisper** - Void child who remembers being your friend (in another timeline)
- **The First Extinguisher** - Original, not corrupted, just... tired

**Boss:** **The Void's Hunger** - What devours failed timelines
