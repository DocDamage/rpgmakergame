# Chroma's Edge — Complete Environment & Map List

## Visual Production Manifesto

---

## 0) Global Art Direction

**Style:** Neo-Pixel JRPG (think *Octopath* lighting meets *Sea of Stars* physics).
**Scale:** Tile-based (16x16 or 32x32) but with verticality (cliffs, stairs, layers).
**Lighting:** Dynamic. Every map needs a "primary light source" (sun, moon, bioluminescence, industrial lamps).

---

## 1) Towns & Hubs (Safe Zones)

### [MAP_T01] Dusthaven (Starting Hub)

* **Vibe:** "Scrap-built freedom." Rusty metal plates welded into canyon walls. Canvas tarps fluttering in dusty wind.
* **Key Landmarks:**
  * **The Cantina:** Neon sign buzzing, crowded interior, booth in shadow.
  * **Renna's Garage:** Open-air workshop, piled machinery, sparks flying.
  * **Market Square:** Crowded stalls, makeshift awnings, central water tank.
* **Lighting:** Harsh desert sun (Day) / flickering neon & campfires (Night).

### [MAP_T02] Ashveil Sanctuary

* **Vibe:** "Nature reclaiming the sacred." Ancient white stone ruins overgrown with aggressive, vibrant flora.
* **Key Landmarks:**
  * **The Elder Tree:** Massive central tree with glowing veins.
  * **Healer's Tent:** Gentle green light, herbs hanging.
  * **Ruinpath Gate:** Stone archway leading to the dungeon.
* **Lighting:** Dappled sunlight through leaves (Day) / bioluminescent spore drift (Night).

### [MAP_T03] Cinderstep (Heat Hub)

* **Vibe:** "Industrial grit meets volcanic heat." Black basalt stone, piping, steam vents, red iron structures.
* **Key Landmarks:**
  * **The Forge:** Massive central blast furnace, flowing molten metal channels.
  * **Vent Platforms:** Metal grates over lava flows.
  * **Obsidian Rail:** Minecart tracks weaving through town.
* **Lighting:** Orange glow from below (constant), smoky haze.

### [MAP_T04] Brinegate Port (Tide Hub)

* **Vibe:** "Survival on the edge of the abyss." Wood & coral structures built on stilts over dark, churning water.
* **Key Landmarks:**
  * **The Docks:** Fishing nets, hanging lanterns, wet wood.
  * **Salinity Ward:** High-tech pumps filtering water.
  * **Tide Bell:** Huge bronze bell at the pier's end.
* **Lighting:** Misty grey/blue (Day) / teal lantern glow reflecting on water (Night).

### [MAP_T05] Rimehold / Chronowake (Time Hub)

* **Vibe:** "Frozen in a perfect moment." Crystal spires, ice that doesn't melt, clocks that are stopped.
* **Key Landmarks:**
  * **The Grand Clock:** Frozen massive gear mechanism in town square.
  * **Echo Plaza:** Mirrors / reflective ice surfaces.
  * **Ferryman’s Pier:** Boat to the Frozen Citadel.
* **Lighting:** Pale blue, stark white, aurora borealis overhead.

### [MAP_T06] The Crown District (Endgame Hub)

* **Vibe:** "Oppressive perfection." Sleek white marble, gold trim, Dominion banners, pristine geometry.
* **Key Landmarks:**
  * **Progenitor Spire Base:** Looking up at the infinite tower.
  * **Parade Grounds:** wide open stone plaza for military reviews.
  * **Archive Entrance:** Heavily guarded technological gate.
* **Lighting:** Cold, bright artificial white light.

## 2) Main Dungeons (Combat & Exploration)

### [MAP_D01] Ruins of Ashveil (Growth)

* **Theme:** "Life that devours."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Vineway Approach:** Stone paths cracked by massive roots.
    2. **Outer Gate:** Puzzle area with vines blocking doors.
    3. **Bloom Atrium:** Central hub, multi-level garden with aggressive plants.
    4. **Reliquary Seat:** Deep underground chamber, roots cradling the Pedestal.

### [MAP_D02] Fungal Depths (Decay/Growth Variant)

* **Theme:** "Bioluminescent dark."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Spore Tunnels:** Narrow, claustrophobic, glowing mushrooms.
    2. **Mycelium Network:** Web-like organic floor patterns.
    3. **Rot Queen’s Den:** Boss arena, pulsating walls.

### [MAP_D03] Crystal Caverns (Light/Prism)

* **Theme:** "Refraction and clarity."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Prism Path:** Crystalline walls reflecting light beams.
    2. **Reflection Chamber:** Puzzle room with mirrors.
    3. **Geode Core:** Boss arena inside a massive hollow crystal.

### [MAP_D04] Skyspire Temple (Heat)

* **Theme:** "Vertical ascent."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Ember Stair:** Exterior climb, wind and smoke.
    2. **Spire Nave:** Interior cathedral, stained glass with fire motifs.
    3. **Furnace Choir:** Machinery and pipes, intense heat distortion.
    4. **Heat Prime Chamber:** Topmost alter, open to the sky/sun.

### [MAP_D05] Abyssal Trench (Tide)

* **Theme:** "Crushing depth."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Silt Tunnels:** Murky, drifting particles, seaweed forests.
    2. **Pressure Midpoint:** Industrial airlock station (safe zone).
    3. **Coral Throne:** Alien-looking coral architecture, vibrant but eerie.
    4. **Abyss Pearl Reliquary:** The deepest point, pitch black with spotlighting.

### [MAP_D06] Obsidian Quarry (Mass)

* **Theme:** "Heavy industry."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Gravemark Pit:** Open pit mine, stepped terraces.
    2. **Conveyor Maze:** Active machinery, crushing pistons, belts.
    3. **Quarry Core:** Central excavation site, massive drill bit.
    4. **Molten Core:** The bottom layer, lava meets machine.

### [MAP_D07] Frozen Citadel (Time)

* **Theme:** "Glitchy memory."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Rime Causeway:** Long bridge approach, frozen statues.
    2. **Shard Halls:** Broken architecture floating in stasis.
    3. **Clockheart Narthex:** The inner mechanism, giant gears.
    4. **Aeon Spire:** The peak, starry sky visible, time distortion effects.

### [MAP_D08] Void Nexus (Shadow)

* **Theme:** "Reality breaking apart."
* **Submaps:**
    1. **Breach Vestibule:** Reality tearing, static effects on edges.
    2. **Echo Galleries:** Mirrors that show alternate versions of the party.
    3. **Null Records:** Library with blank books and erased walls.
    4. **Stillpoint Hub:** Grey, soundless safe zone.
    5. **Nexus Heart:** Boss arena, floating platform in a purple void.

## 3) The Overworld (Field Zones)

* **[MAP_OW_01] Shattered Badlands:** Dusty canyons, jagged rocks (connecting Dusthaven to Ashveil).
* **[MAP_OW_02] Verdant Coast:** Green cliffs meeting the sea (connecting Ashveil to Brinegate).
* **[MAP_OW_03] Sable Expanse:** Flat black obsidian desert (connecting Dusthaven to Cinderstep).
* **[MAP_OW_04] Glacial Reach:** Snowy tundra, whiteout conditions (north).
* **[MAP_OW_05] The Progenitor Ring:** The massive technological ring structure encircling the world map (viewed from distance or traversed in late game).

---

## 4) Special / Interior Locations

* **[MAP_INT_01] The Ironhawk Airship (Interior):** Metal corridors, cockpit, engine room. Your mobile base later in game.
* **[MAP_INT_02] Marinus Sanctum:** Small, underwater shrine. Peaceful, "pause room" vibe.
* **[MAP_INT_03] Aurora Ascension Tower (Lobby):** The grand entrance to the 100-floor challenge.
* **[MAP_INT_04] Dream/Lattice:** Abstract digital space. Wireframe meshes, floating code, "The Matrix" but fantasy.

---

## 5) Boss Arenas (Specific Setpieces)

* **[MAP_ARENA_01] Rootbound Warden:** Circular stone platform surrounded by writhing vines.
* **[MAP_ARENA_02] Ember Priest:** Metal grate platform over lava.
* **[MAP_ARENA_03] Leviathan Remnant:** Underwater circular arena with 4 Pylons.
* **[MAP_ARENA_04] Obsidian Colossus:** Stone platform that tilts/shakes.
* **[MAP_ARENA_05] Eon Regent:** Giant clock face floor.
* **[MAP_ARENA_06] Void Nexus Regent:** Floating platform, background creates vertigo.
