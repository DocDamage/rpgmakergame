# Chroma's Edge — Location Bible (Source of Truth)

## 1) Overworld Regions (Macro Map)

**Planet: ORION**

The overworld is split into 4 major landmasses + a central anomaly + a hidden sky settlement:

### Ashwold Shelf (Starting Continent | Lv 1–30)
- Growth + Motion instability starts here
- Frontier, scrap, refugees, small sanctuaries

### Lumencrest Expanse (Eastern Continent | Lv 30–60)
- Light + Heat zones
- Mountains, harsh sun, Dominion pressure, Skyspire access

### Rimechain Isles (Northern Isles | Lv 60–100)
- Time instability + deep cold
- Fjords, ice ports, frozen relic routes

### Sable Expanse (Southern Wastes | Lv 100–150)
- Mass pressure + broken geology
- Quarries, gravity scars, old Dominion blacksites

### Progenitor Ring ("The Crown" | Endgame Hub Route)
- The approach to Chroma Tower
- Reality feels "too structured"

### Aetherreach (Hidden Sky Town | Lv 150+)
- Requires flight-capable mount
- "Above the Lattice" vibes

## 2) The 12 Towns (Complete Set)

### Starting Continent (4)

**Ashveil Sanctuary** (canon)
- Theme: Growth-held refuge, old stone + living vine-tech
- Connects: Ruins of Ashveil (D1)

**Dusthaven** (canon — script uses Dusthaven Cantina/Outskirts)
- Theme: scrap frontier town, Ironhawk-adjacent hustle, Dominion harassment
- Connects: early story hub + routes into badlands road network

**Mirewatch** (new)
- Theme: swamp boardwalk town, lantern culture, fungal medicine trade
- Connects: Fungal Depths (D2)

**Prismridge** (new)
- Theme: crystal-mining town, light-refraction streets, "glow-dust" economy
- Connects: Crystal Caverns (D3)

### Eastern Continent (3)

**Cinderstep** (new)
- Theme: mountain switchback town, heat vents, smiths + heat-resistant gear
- Connects: Skyspire Temple (D4)

**Brinegate Port** (new)
- Theme: stormbreak harbor, tide-scarred docks, deep-sea divers + relic rumor mill
- Connects: Abyssal Trench (D5) + route to Marinus's Sanctum (canon shrine site)

**Halcyon Freeport** (canon postgame hub — promoted to full town)
- Theme: neutral coastal freeport, refugee rebuild, market politics, remnant quests
- Connects: postgame Remnant arcs + travel spine

### Northern Isles (2)

**Rimehold** (new)
- Theme: ice-fort fishing town, whale lamps, "time-sick" weather
- Connects: Frozen Citadel (D7)

**Chronowake Pier** (new)
- Theme: shipyard built around time glitches; ships "arrive early"
- Connects: northern travel hub + time relic sidequests

### Southern Wastes (2)

**Gravemark Outpost** (new)
- Theme: quarry settlement under gravity strain; reinforced buildings + mass gear
- Connects: Obsidian Quarry (D6)

**Meridian Junction** (new)
- Theme: crossroads city built on old Progenitor rails; factions collide here
- Connects: Progenitor Ring Gate → Chroma Tower route

### Hidden Sky Town (1)

**Aetherreach** (new)
- Theme: floating terraces, wind-market, sky-salvage, "above the rules"
- Connects: hidden endgame merchants + sky-only hunts + superboss hints
