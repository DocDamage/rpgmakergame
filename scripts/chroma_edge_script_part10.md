SCENE 130 — "MERCER: SERMON BREAK" — Floor 50 (Sermon Hall) — Boss End

[Mercer staggers at low HP. The trance-soldiers around the hall twitch like puppets losing strings.]

MERCER
(laughing through pain)
Oh… good.
You're strong enough to be interesting.

He lifts a hand—tries to snap again.

Nothing happens.

Mercer blinks—confused for the first time.

Nix steps forward, glow-lines burning steady like a circuit finally grounded.

NIX-7
Prime network… denies your rewrite.

Mercer's smile returns—smaller, sharper.

MERCER
Prime doesn't deny.
Prime… negotiates.

Kade raises his gunblade.

KADE
Then negotiate with this.

FINAL STRIKE (CUTSCENE):

Suresh calls LEVIATHAN—a surge of water-light slams Mercer's illusions flat.

Grit's TITAN presence pins the floor, stopping Mercer's turn-order warp.

Korr fires one clean shot at Mercer's halo implant—not lethal, precise—breaking his "broadcast" channel.

Nix presses her palm to the dais and pulses Prime Recalibration.

TERMINAL (V.O.)
—PRIME OVERRIDE: LOCAL—
—SERMON THREAD: TERMINATED—

Mercer drops to one knee, breath ragged. The trance-soldiers' eyes go normal—confused, terrified, human.

FREED SOLDIER
What—
Where am I?

RENNA
Long story.
Run that way if you like being alive.

Mercer looks up at Nix. Not anger. Reverence.

MERCER
(soft, thrilled)
There it is.
That's the tone.
The Engine likes you when you're disobedient.

Nix's voice is ice.

NIX-7
I do not care what it likes.

Mercer's smile widens.

MERCER
That's why it will keep you.

Kade steps forward to finish it—

—and the Tower shifts.

Not a hallway shift. A whole-floor reconfiguration. The dais splits open behind Mercer like a throat swallowing.

Mercer falls backward into the opening, still smiling.

MERCER
(shouting as he drops)
Come up, Prime walkers!
The Triumvirate wants to meet you properly!

The opening seals. Silence slams into place.

SYSTEM POPUP: BOSS DEFEATED — HIGH CULTIST MERCER

SCENE 131 — "THE TRI-KEY DAIS" — Floor 50 (Sermon Hall) — Moments Later

[The air goes cold. The banners stop moving. Even dust seems afraid to drift.]

A new set of lights ignites along the far wall—Dominion clean-white.

A door that wasn't there opens.

ARBITER LUCIEN MARR steps in like he's arriving to a meeting on time.

Behind him, HIEROPHANT ORIN VASK glides like a priest who thinks gravity is optional.

And between them—an empty space that feels occupied.

The Tower hum deepens like it recognized authority.

MARR
Congratulations.
You've climbed farther than projected.

RENNA
I hate his voice. It sounds like a contract.

VASK
(smiling, serene)
Beloved rebels.
Look at you… dragging hope up stone stairs like it's a wounded animal.

Korr steps forward, rifle steady.

KORR
Where's Mercer?

Vask's smile is almost tender.

VASK
Mercer is… a mouth.
And mouths are replaceable.

Marr glances at Nix with cool interest.

MARR
Interface restored. Prime network online.
You did our work beautifully.

Kade's blade lifts an inch.

KADE
We're not your workforce.

MARR
No.
You're the test group.

Nix tilts her head.

NIX-7
You needed Prime online to open the upper Tower.

Marr's eyes sharpen—approval.

MARR
Correct.
The Progenitor's upper domain requires balanced authority to access.
Law. Faith. Voice.
And Prime is the only stable base layer.

Vask spreads his hands like he's about to bless the room.

VASK
Prime makes the world feel safe again.
That's why people will accept what comes next.

Renna's eyes narrow.

RENNA
What comes next?

Marr answers like he's reading a memo.

MARR
Compliance.

Kade takes one step forward.

KADE
Over my dead body.

Marr's expression doesn't change.

MARR
That can be arranged.

The empty space between Marr and Vask suddenly speaks—Mercer's voice, but stripped of warmth. No smile. Just signal.

THE ENGINE'S MOUTH (V.O.)
—ASCENT AUTHORIZED—
—CLEANSE SEQUENCE: PENDING—

Nix's glow-lines flare defensively.

NIX-7
That is not Mercer.

MARR
No.
It's what Mercer was useful for.

Vask's eyes glitter.

VASK
Come, Prime walkers.
Climb.
Witness the truth at the top.

Marr turns to leave, already bored.

MARR
Floor 100 is where Orion becomes stable forever.
You wanted to "save" the world.
Now you can watch us fix it properly.

Korr's voice is low, lethal.

KORR
We're coming to stop you.

Marr pauses—half a beat—then speaks without looking back.

MARR
Good.
I prefer my history to include opposition.
It makes the victory feel… inevitable.

They exit. The door seals like it never existed.

SYSTEM POPUP: OBJECTIVE UPDATED — REACH FLOOR 100
SYSTEM POPUP: OBJECTIVE UPDATED — STOP THE CLEANSE SEQUENCE

We were singing…

Welcome back.

Twist points at the sealed Mercer-throat panel.

Meaning: Mercer's not done.

SOVA
Meaning: the Engine is using him as a channel.
Or wearing him like a glove.

CALLUM
That's a horrible image.

RENNA
Keep it. It's accurate.

So the building's fighting dirty now.
