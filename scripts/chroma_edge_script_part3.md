SCENE 027 — "ASH AND ORDERS" — Shattered Badlands — Pre-Dawn

[CAM: close on Kade's boots dragging through gray dust. The horizon is wrong—mountain ranges where flatland used to be.]

[Korr walks beside him like a ghost in uniform. She's keeping pace out of spite.]

KADE
So what now, Marshal? You going to "order" the planet back into shape?

KORR
If I could, I would.

KADE
(looks at her)
That almost sounded like a person.

[Korr's jaw tightens. A faint electronic whine in her augments.]

KORR
Don't start.
(beat)
The tether's… quiet right now. It won't stay that way.

KADE
Then we move before it wakes up.

[In the distance: floodlights. A perimeter made of scrap walls and jury-rigged turrets.]

RENNA (V.O.) (memory echo)
"If Dominion's chasing her, they'll burn half this town to get her."

KADE
That better not be Dominion.

KORR
Not clean enough.

SCENE 028 — "IRONHAWK TRUCE" — Ironhawk Forward Camp — Dawn

[The gate swings open. Ironhawk grunts level rifles… then hesitate when they see Korr.]

IRONHAWK GUARD
Marshal—

[Korr doesn't flinch.]

KORR

IRONHAWK GUARD
…Fair.

[A familiar silhouette steps out: the IRONHAWK BROKER from the cantina. Same smooth voice. Same "I'll sell your organs with a smile" energy.]

IRONHAWK BROKER
Kade.
Looks like your contract went… planet-shaped.

KADE
Where's my crew?

IRONHAWK BROKER
Alive. Mostly.
We started pulling survivors after the sky split.
Even we're not stupid enough to fight the Dominion and the weather.

KADE
Since when do Ironhawks do charity?

IRONHAWK BROKER
Since "business as usual" fell into the ocean.

[Korr's augments twitch—like something inside her wants to bark an order.]

KORR
(low)
Don't talk too long.

IRONHAWK BROKER
Oh, I'm terrified.
(leans in, to Kade)
Dominion's moving a "high-value interface" to a blacksite.
They're calling it ICEBOX.

[Kade's eyes go cold.]

KADE
Nix.

SCENE 029 — "SCATTERED, NOT BROKEN" — Ironhawk Med Bay — Morning

[A row of cots. Bandages. Old blood. New bruises.]

SURESH

You say the sweetest things.

[Kade enters. Renna sees him and basically collides with his chest.]

RENNA
YOU'RE ALIVE.

KADE

SURESH
(relieved, but hiding it)

[Korr steps in behind him. The room temperature drops about two degrees just from the tension.]

SURESH
…Marshal.

KORR
Doctor.

RENNA
Okay! New rule! No murdering in my line of sight until breakfast digests.

[A huge shadow fills the doorway: GRIT. Built like a bunker. He nods once to Kade—respect, not warmth.]

GRIT
You made it.

KADE
You too.

GRIT
Hard to die.
(beat)
Unfortunately.

[On the far cot, someone lounging like this is a luxury suite: CALLUM DRAKE, smirking through a split lip.]

CALLUM
Look who crawled back.
(eyes Korr)
And you brought your apocalypse chaperone. Cute.

KORR
Say that again.

CALLUM
No.

[In the corner, a woman in battered templar silver kneels with hands clasped: VEX. She looks up, eyes haunted.]

VEX
If the world is punishment… I accept.
If it's a test… I'll pass.

RENNA
Love the enthusiasm. Hate the vibe.

[A quieter presence near the door: ASHKA VERNE, cleaning a rifle with calm ritual.]

ASHKA
Dominion patrols shifted.
They're scared.

KADE
Good.

[And then—like a problem walking in late on purpose—TWIST appears from nowhere, flipping a coin.]

TWIST
Miss me?

KADE
No.

TWIST
Liar.

[Suresh points a scalpel at Twist without looking.]

SURESH
If you stole anything from my kits, I will remove your fingers and donate them to science.

TWIST
Wow. Doctor's spicy now.

RENNA
She's always been spicy. It's just… medically regulated.

[MONTAGE CUT: These eight joined during the scattered aftermath of the World Break.]

[Twist is halfway through Kade's pack when he catches her wrist without looking.]

CUT 2 — DR. INES SOVA (Black Mage / Analyst)

[A woman in tattered Dominion analyst blacks sits on a crate, glaring at a broken datapad like it personally offended her.]

SOVA
Then you need me.

Grit.

We're going after the Dominion.
You in?

GRIT
Yeah.

CUT 4 — ASHKA VERNE (Scout / Ranger)

[Ashka kneels on a ridge, scope trained on Dominion patrol routes. Her hands are steady. Her eyes aren't.]

KADE
Ironhawk said you've been tracking Dominion movements for three days.

ASHKA
(doesn't lower the scope)
They took my brother.
Patrol sweep. Six months ago.
Called it "desertion."

Kade's jaw tightens—he knows that lie.

KADE
We're hitting a Dominion site tonight.
You want in?

Ashka finally lowers the scope. Her eyes are sharp, tired, furious.

ASHKA
What are you taking?

KADE
A friend. And anything we can burn on the way out.

Ashka stands, slinging her rifle.

ASHKA
Then I'm in.
And if I see the squad that took Kellen…
I'm not asking permission.

KADE
Good.

CUT 5 — SENNA (Enhanced Monk)

[Senna sits cross-legged in the med bay, fists wrapped, knuckles bleeding. She's been hitting a support beam like it wronged her family.]

SURESH
You're going to break your hands.

SENNA
They're already broken.
The Foundry made sure of that.

Suresh kneels, checking the damage—old scars, new splits, augment burns.

SURESH
Aurora Foundry?

Senna nods, jaw tight.

SENNA
They said pain was enlightenment.
I believed them.
Until the world broke and they left me to burn.

RENNA
So you punched your way out?

SENNA
More or less.

Kade steps in.

KADE
We're going after Dominion. Their blacksite. Their people.
You want revenge or you want purpose?

Senna looks up—eyes burning.

SENNA
Both.

KADE
Good answer.

CUT 6 — CALLUM DRAKE (Summoner / Noble)

[Callum lounges on a cot like he's at a resort, despite the refugee camp chaos. He's polishing a signet ring.]

KADE

CALLUM

My family's fortune is currently underwater.

RENNA

CALLUM
Broke, yes. Useless?
(his eyes glow faintly—summoner resonance)
I can call things most people can't pronounce.

NIX-7
Ashborn resonance detected.

CALLUM
You can feel that?
Impressive.

KADE
You fight with us, you earn your keep.

CALLUM
Bold of you to assume I need to earn anything.
(beat)
But fine. I'm bored and the world's broken.
Let's see if we can fix it with style.

CUT 7 — PETRA (Controlled Mutation Berserker)

[Petra sits alone, chewing jerky, staring at her hands like they're weapons she's learning to trust.]

SURESH
Mutation scars. Controlled, not wild.

PETRA
Foundry project.

KADE

PETRA
Three guards. Two scientists.

KADE
We're going after a Dominion site.

PETRA
Good.

To who?

I want penance.

VEX
Then my blade is yours.
