Chroma's Edge — Script Draft (Part 5: Marinus's Sanctum → Obsidian Quarry → Colossus Mercy Flag)

SCENE 058 — "SEARCHLIGHTS" — Western Coastline (Flooded) — Night

[The party moves along half-submerged streets. A Dominion patrol boat idles offshore. A hard white searchlight sweeps the water like a knife.]

RENNA
If that light hits me I'm going to pretend I'm a rock.

PETRA
Rocks don't talk.

RENNA
Exactly. Observe.

[Renna immediately whispers anyway.]

CALLUM
We could just… leave dramatically faster.

ASHKA
Two spotters on the bow. One aft gun.
They're not here for survivors. They're here for us.

[Kade shifts Nix closer behind a broken wall.]

KADE
No hero stuff. No panic stuff.
We slide past, we don't get noticed.

[Behind the wall, tethered mounts stay low and quiet; even Renna's Cub is tucked under her coat, ears flat.]

[Korr stares at the boat. Her face is stone, but her eyes are hate.]

KORR
That's my old patrol designation.

SURESH
Then you know the blind spots.

KORR
Yeah.
(quiet)
I trained them.

[Searchlight sweeps closer—closer—]

TWIST
(whispers)
If we get caught I'm blaming the tall guy.

GRIT
That's everyone.

TWIST
Exactly. Great system.

STEALTH TRIGGER (Optional): Coastal Slip — if failed → battle

BATTLE TRIGGER (If Detected): Dominion Shore Marines + Searchlight Drone

[Either way, they escape into darker water tunnels beneath a collapsed pier.]

SCENE 059 — "THE SEA'S WITNESS" — Under-Pier Tunnels — Minutes Later

[Water drips. Old warning signs. The sound of the sea above—low, heavy breathing.]

A shape waits ahead: MIRA DEEPCALLER. Not hostile. Not friendly. Just… present.

[At the tunnel mouth behind them, a Drake and two Striders settle into watch posture, tracking sound down the pier.]

RENNA
Oh good. Our ocean witch is back.

MIRA
I am not a witch.

RENNA
That's exactly what a witch says.

[Mira's gaze lands on the Abyss Pearl in Nix's pack. Then on Suresh.]

MIRA
The Pearl doesn't belong to the Interface.
It belongs to the one who heals.

SURESH
…Me?

MIRA
You carry blood that isn't yours.
And guilt that is.

Suresh doesn't flinch—she's too tired to.

SURESH
If you're here to judge me, get in line.

Mira steps aside, revealing a half-submerged stairwell spiraling down.

MIRA
Marinus's Sanctum is below.
Seat Tide correctly… and the coast will stop drowning itself.

KADE
And if we don't?

MIRA
Then the sea keeps collecting names.

Beat.

NIX-7
Mira… why are you helping?

Mira's eyes soften—barely.

MIRA
Because I was made to be a lock.
And I'm tired of guarding a cage.

She turns away.

MIRA
Come.
Let the water see what you choose.

SCENE 060 — "LEVIATHAN'S SANCTUM" — Prime Pedestal Chamber — Deep Night

[Ancient architecture under the coast. Coral like stained glass. A huge circular pedestal ringed by tidal channels. The air tastes like salt and old prayers.]

SOVA
This isn't Dominion.
This is Foundation architecture.

VEX
A holy place… that the world forgot.

CALLUM
That's most holy places, honestly.

[Nix approaches the pedestal. It hums in response—gentle, but immense.]

NIX-7
Prime Pedestal confirmed.
Tide Foundation.

She looks to Suresh.

NIX-7
Doctor… you must hold the Abyss Pearl.

SURESH
Why me?

MIRA
Because Tide doesn't cleanse machines.
It cleanses people.

Suresh's throat tightens. She hates being seen.

SURESH
Fine.
But if this is some symbolic "wash away your sins" thing, I'm warning you—
my sins are stubborn.

RENNA
Same. Mine come with a warranty.

SCENE 061 — "SEATING: TIDE" — Marinus's Sanctum — Continuous

SYSTEM PROMPT (CUTSCENE UI): SEAT RELIC? (Abyss Pearl Required)

[Suresh steps forward, holding the Abyss Pearl. It pulses in her hands like a second heart.]

Nix places her palm on the pedestal. Her glow-lines flare—then settle into a deep ocean rhythm.

TERMINAL (V.O.)
—PRIME CALIBRATION: AVAILABLE—
—BEGIN SEATING—

[Water lifts off the floor in thin spirals, orbiting Nix like moons.]

ASHKA
Movement in the tunnels.

KORR
They tracked us.

TWIST
Of course they did. We're very trackable. We have vibes.

BATTLE TRIGGER: Pedestal Defense — Protect Nix (3 Turns)
Enemies: Dominion Shore Marines + "Tide Dampener" Drones + Abyss Guardians

[Mid-battle, Renna's pet Cub leaps at a Dampener Drone, distracting it with CLAW STRIKE while Twist flanks.]

RENNA
That's my boy! Good distraction!

[Renna snaps two fingers; the Cub pivots to GUARD, body-checking a marine that breaks for Nix.]

SYSTEM POPUP: PET COMMAND — CUB (Guard Stance)

[Callum's Drake pet unleashes ELEMENTAL BURST (fire), scorching a cluster of marines.]

CALLUM
Weaponized pets. I approve.

[Callum whistles a descending note; the Drake banks and tags two enemies in ember-mark.]

ASHKA
Tagged. Taking them.

SYSTEM POPUP: Pet abilities active — Combat support engaged
SYSTEM POPUP: PET SYNERGY — MARK + BURST (Crit rate up)

During combat, Suresh staggers—visions hitting her in flashes:

operating tables

Dominion insignias

a child's small hand grasping hers off-screen, implied

a voice: "Keep them alive long enough to learn."

Suresh chokes on it.

SURESH
(whisper, furious)
No.

The Pearl pulses harder.

MIRA
Let it show you.
Then let it go.

SCENE 062 — "LEVIATHAN AWAKENS" — Marinus's Sanctum — After Defense

[The last enemy falls. The pedestal flares blue-white.]

TERMINAL (V.O.)
—PRIME CALIBRATION: ACCEPTED—
—TIDE STABILIZATION: INITIATING—

The chamber fills with rising water—yet no one drowns. It holds them like a hand.

Suresh clutches the Pearl as a massive silhouette forms in the water: LEVIATHAN—serpentine, crown-finned, ancient.

Everyone freezes.

RENNA
…Okay. That's the biggest thing I've ever seen that isn't trying to tax me.

Marinus's presence is not violent. It's truthful.

LEVIATHAN (V.O.)
(heard inside the chest, not the ears)
HEALER.
WITNESS.
CHOOSE.

Suresh is shaking. Not fear—relief so sharp it hurts.

SURESH
I can't undo what I did.

LEVIATHAN (V.O.)
NO.
BUT YOU CAN STOP DOING IT.

Suresh breathes in, ragged.

SURESH
…Then bind to me.

The Pearl dissolves into water-light and sinks into her chest like a brand.

SYSTEM POPUP: SUMMON UNLOCKED — LEVIATHAN (Owner: Dr. Marin Suresh)

Callum's eyes widen—reverent, hungry for power but respectful.

CALLUM
So that's what "earned" looks like.

Suresh glares at him through tears she refuses to let fall.

SURESH
Say one poetic thing and I'm prescribing you silence.

Marinus coils once, and the flood above them releases—like the sea unclenching.

SCENE 063 — "WASHED OUT" — Western Coastline — Pre-Dawn

[They emerge. The coastline has shifted—waterline receding slightly. Rubble settles. The air feels less panicked.]

Offshore, the Dominion patrol boat wobbles as currents change. Its searchlight flickers.

KORR
Tide stabilization just rewrote local currents.
They're going to think it's a storm surge.

SOVA
Good. Let them blame weather.
Weather doesn't testify.

MIRA
(quiet, to Nix)
Five seats remain.

Mira steps backward into shadowy surf—and is simply… gone.

RENNA
She does that on purpose.

TWIST
Respect.

Kade looks north—toward land that now ripples strangely, like gravity is breathing.

NIX-7
Mass is calling next.
The northern wastes are… heavy.

GRIT
Then we go where the world hurts.

DUNGEON 6 SETUP — OBSIDIAN QUARRY (MASS)

SCENE 064 — "GRAVITY COUNTRY" — Northern Wastes — Day

[The world gets weird fast: pebbles float, then slam down. Dust hangs mid-air. Footsteps feel like walking through syrup.]

PETRA
I feel like the planet's trying to sit on me.

SOVA
Mass Foundation instability.
Gravity isn't consistent here—it's… emotional.

RENNA
That's my least favorite kind of emotion.

[Korr slows, scanning distant industrial silhouettes.]

KORR
Dominion quarry.
They mine obsidian and "artifact stone" for Foundry projects.

SURESH
Foundry projects meaning "things that scream."

KORR
…Yeah.

Nix pauses, head tilting.

NIX-7
I detect a relic-frequency.
Core Weight.

Kade's grip tightens.

KADE
Then we take it before Marr does.

SCENE 065 — "THE QUARRY" — Obsidian Quarry Entrance — Late Day

[Massive pit mine. Cranes. Conveyor belts. Dominion banners flapping like threats.]

Workers in ragged gear haul stone under guard. Shock-collars. Rifles. No ambiguity what this is.

Suresh's face goes cold.

SURESH
They're using prisoners.

VEX
Then we free them.
And we cleanse the captors.

TWIST
"Cleanse" is such a polite word for murder.

VEX
I'm practicing restraint.

Kade looks at Korr.

KADE
You knew.

Korr doesn't flinch—she deserves the hit.

KORR
I signed orders that fed this machine.
(beat)
I'm not signing anything anymore.

OBJECTIVE (HUD): Free the Quarry Workers (0/3 Barracks Keys)

KADE
We do this clean: open cages, kill guards, no one left behind.

RENNA
Finally, a plan I can emotionally support.

SCENE 066 — "MAGISTRATE KORVAN" — Obsidian Quarry Overseer Platform — Night

[Mid-dungeon platform above the pit. A Dominion official in polished armor: MAGISTRATE KORVAN. He holds a remote trigger like a rosary.]

KORVAN
Rebels.
Defectors.
A medic with blood on her hands.

His gaze lands on Korr—pure disgust.

KORVAN
And the Marshal's embarrassment.

Korr steps forward, voice quiet and lethal.

KORR
Say my name.

Korvan smiles.

KORVAN
Nadia Korr.
You were built to obey. Even your "freedom" is a phase.

Korr's jaw twitches—reflex—but she doesn't break. She breathes through it.

SOVA
(under her breath)
That's not tether. That's conditioning.
He's trying to push the trigger you used to be.

KADE
Not happening.

Korvan raises the remote.

KORVAN
One button. Three hundred collars.
You move, they die.

Suresh steps forward—eyes like scalpels.

SURESH
You want leverage?
Try me.

Korvan laughs.

KORVAN
You'll hesitate. Healers always do.

Suresh smiles—dry, horrible.

SURESH
You don't know what I hesitate over.

Renna's fingers dance on a wrist-rig.

RENNA
Hey, Magistrate?
Your remote has a frequency.

Korvan's smile falters—one millimeter.

RENNA
And I'm about to bully it.

BATTLE TRIGGER: Mini-Boss — Magistrate Korvan + Dominion Gravity Troopers

[During battle, Korvan tries to trigger collars—Renna jams it. Vex shields prisoners. Ashka snipes the relay on the far tower.]

After defeat, Korvan crawls backward, reaching for a backup detonator.

Korr steps on his hand—hard.

KORR
No more collars.

KORVAN
(choking laugh)
You think you're saving them?
The quarry runs deeper than you.
The Colossus will crush you anyway.

Kade raises his gunblade.

KADE
Then we'll break the quarry.

Korvan spits blood.

KORVAN
Marr will remake the world.
And you'll beg to be part of it.

KADE
No.

[Kade ends it. Dominion guards panic and scatter. The workers' collars deactivate with a soft click.]

WORKER (hoarse)
…We're free?

SURESH
Yeah.
Now move. Before Dominion reinforcements remember how legs work.

A massive shape is dormant in the center: THE COLOSSUS—a titan construct of obsidian plates, rusted Dominion braces, and ancient glyphs that predate the Dominion by centuries.

Its eyes open—two dim cores like dying stars.

THE COLOSSUS
(voice like tectonic plates grinding)
MASS… UNSEATED…
ANCHOR… REQUIRED…

NIX-7
You are a guardian.

THE COLOSSUS

CALLUM
So… normal for us.

Nix approaches. The glyphs on its chest flicker, begging to be understood.

NIX-7
It is not Dominion-made.
Dominion chained it here.

Korr stares at the restraints—Dominion bolts hammered into ancient joints.

KORR
That's… my engineering corps signature.

KORR
We enslaved it.

SURESH
Everything the Dominion touches becomes a tool.

TWIST
So do we smash it, or…?

NIX-7

Good.
