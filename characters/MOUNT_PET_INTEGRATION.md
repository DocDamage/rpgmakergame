# Mount & Pet System Integration Summary

This document tracks all narrative integration points for the Mount and Pet systems in Chroma's Edge.

---

## System Unlock

### Part 2 - Scene 017: "TAMING UNLOCKS" (Crystal Caverns - After Boss)
**Location:** After defeating Commander Ellis Varn (Dungeon 3 boss)

**What Happens:**
- Light Foundation stabilizes, making creatures recognize safety
- Party encounters a **Luminous Strider** drawn to Nix's stable resonance
- **BEAST AFFINITY** unlocked as passive ability for all characters
- **TAMING** ability added to party skillset
- Tutorial explains taming mechanics (use Taming Stone on creatures below 30% HP)
- First mount tamed: **Luminous Strider** (Common Land Mount, 1.5× speed, 60s stamina)
- Establishes that creatures were hostile due to Foundation instability, not inherent nature

**Narrative Purpose:**
- Explains why world creatures can now be tamed (Foundation stabilization)
- Provides mechanical unlock that makes sense in-world
- Introduces both mount (travel) and pet (combat) concepts

- Demonstrates overworld secret detection mechanic
- Shows pet gathering/exploration utility

**Dialogue Highlights:**

- Twist: "Zero monster attacks. I could get used to this"

## Mount/Pet Restrictions

### Part 8 - Scene 094: "THE SPIRE OPENS" (Chroma Tower Gate)
**Context:** Party attempting to enter Chroma Tower with mounts

**Restrictions Introduced:**
- Mounts refuse to approach Tower (Tower rejects external resonances)
- **Mounts auto-stabled** (cannot be summoned within Tower floors)
- **Pets cannot accompany party** in Tower (Tower authority forbids external entities)
- Party must climb all 100 floors on foot

**Narrative Purpose:**
- Explains why Tower gameplay has no mounts/pets (dungeon restriction)
- Shows Tower as unique location with special rules
- Adds tension - party loses travel/combat advantages for Tower ascent

**Dialogue:**
- Grit: "Mine either. Tower's… rejecting them."
- Nix: "The Tower does not permit external resonances."

## System Mechanics Referenced

### Taming
- **Unlock Condition:** After 3rd dungeon boss (Level 25), Light stabilization
- **Method:** Use Taming Stone on creatures below 30% HP
- **Success Rate:** Based on creature rarity, HP %, and LUK stat
- **Limit:** Cannot tame duplicates

### Mounts
- **Categories:** Land, Aquatic, Aerial, Special Terrain
- **Benefits:** 1.5× to 3× movement speed, 0% encounter rate
- **Tiers:** Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary
- **Evolution:** Through riding hours, quests, or feeding items
- **Storage:** Unlimited stable capacity, instant summon (except dungeons/towns)

### Pets
- **Active Limit:** 2 pets simultaneously
- **Combat:** Auto-attacks, buffs, elemental bursts (scales with owner)
- **Overworld:** Secret detection, treasure alerts, gathering bonuses
- **AI Modes:** Aggressive, Defensive, Balanced, Manual
- **Evolution:** Through battle experience, bond hours, or quests

## Summary

The mount and pet systems have been integrated into the narrative at key points:

1. **Unlock:** Natural story moment (Dungeon 3 boss, Light stabilization)
2. **Tutorial:** First taming (Luminous Strider) establishes mechanics
3. **Travel:** Multiple scenes show mounts reducing encounter rates, enabling fast travel
4. **Exploration:** Pet secret detection demonstrated (Cub finds buried treasure)
5. **Combat:** Pets provide tactical support in battle (Cub distraction, Drake burst)
6. **Restrictions:** Tower entrance explains why system disabled in endgame dungeon
7. **Terrain:** Special mounts for extreme environments (Lava Striders for heat)

The system feels integrated into the world's Foundation-based lore, provides meaningful gameplay benefits, and respects narrative restrictions (Tower climb without external help).
