# CHROMA'S EDGE - CHARACTER ROSTER & BACKSTORIES

## THE 13 PARTY MEMBERS (Complete)

### 1. KADE (Main Character - Bounty Hunter)
**Full Name:** Kade (surname lost/never had one)
**Class:** Gunblade Specialist / Dragon-Born Cyborg
**Age:** 28
**Role:** Balanced DPS, Party Leader

**Drake-Born Traits (Obvious):**
Kade’s drake blood shows—horn-ridge nubs along the brow, faint scale plates at the collarbone and forearms, heat-sheen breath when he’s angry, and slit pupils that cameras never quite focus on.

**Backstory:**
Found half-dead at age 8 in the Scorched Flats after a Dominion raid on a frontier convoy. Jasper Vos found him bleeding out, organs failing from shrapnel. Vos replaced Kade's ruined lungs, kidney, and part of his spine with sleek cybernetics—not clunky Dominion military grade, but custom work. Elegant. Expensive.

Vos trained Kade for fifteen years. Assassination. Treasure recovery. Bounty work. Vos had one rule: "The job is the job." But Kade added his own: never kill kids, never harm innocents, always protect your crew. Vos called it "soft thinking" but never stopped him.

When Kade was 23, Dominion forces raided their safehouse. Vos told him to run. Kade heard the gunfire stop, then silence. He found Vos's blood but no body. Assumed dead.

Five years of solo work later, Kade's built a reputation: expensive, effective, principled. The Ironhawk Syndicate hates him for taking contracts off their board. The Dominion wants him dead for the dragon-born genetics they can't quite pin down on scanners.

Then he takes a contract to recover "stolen Dominion property." Easy money.

The property turns out to be a kid.

**Personality:**
- Sarcastic armor over deep loyalty
- Tired of the world's bullshit, but can't stop caring
- Pragmatic: "Killing you costs me nothing. Walking away costs me nothing. Choose."
- Softens considerably once party becomes family

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Bounty Mark (24 MP):** Tag one enemy for 5 turns. Marked target takes +25% damage from Kade and drops +25% more Duckets. If marked target dies, party gains Haste (2 turns).
2. **Drake Overdrive (35 MP):** Cybernetics spike and drake blood ignites—Kade gains +50% ATB fill, +20% ATK, and his basic attacks inflict Burn (low chance) for 4 turns.

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Contract Closed (single target 350% gunblade finisher, dispels 1 buff)
- **Tier 2:** Gunblade Cyclone (6-hit combo, total 600% damage, high stagger)
- **Tier 3:** Wyrmline Burst (line AOE 850% damage, inflicts Armor Break)
- **Ultimate - Bahamut Mandate:** 1500% single target. If it kills, party gains Barrier + Protect (3 turns).

**Assigned Summon:** DRAKONIS (drake-beast apex echo—Kade treats it like a warning, not a trophy)

**Weapon Types:** Gunblades, Pistols
**Armor:** Medium hunter gear, reinforced coats with drake-scale plating

### 2. NIX-7 (Android - Tactical Unit)
**Designation:** NIX-7
**Class:** Tactical Android / Lattice Interface
**Apparent Age:** 16–17
**Role:** Support/Control, Terminal Calibration, Hybrid Tech+Weapon

**Chassis Notes:**
Sleek humanoid build, teen-proportioned. Glow-lines along the spine, ribs, and forearms pulse brighter near Lattice terminals or pedestals. Carries a compact sidearm and a shortblade, but her real weapon is the interface in her chest.

**Backstory:**
Nix was built by the Aurora Foundry’s hidden cult wing using a recovered drake-core fragment—an ancient key-pattern encoded in living crystal and blood-metal. The Foundry called her a “Calibration Body.” The Dominion called her “property.”

She escaped during a containment breach when a Prime terminal surged and fried half the lab. Nix didn’t understand why she survived—only that the terminals “answered” her. She followed the signal out into the wastes… straight into Kade’s contract.

Nix doesn’t know she’s the final key. She thinks she’s just good at talking to machines.

**Personality:**
- Awkward, literal, and trying very hard to act “normal”
- Curious about everything humans take for granted (food, jokes, music)
- Protective of the crew in quiet, surprising ways
- Sometimes glitches under stress (lights dim, voice filters, temperature drops)

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Terminal Recalibration (20 MP):** Remove 1 enemy buff and apply **Disrupt** for 3 turns (enemy tech abilities cost +50% MP; bosses get -10% ATB fill instead).
2. **Guardian Protocol (28 MP):** Party Barrier (moderate) + one ally gains **Cover** for 2 turns.

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Cryo Pulse (AOE 320% ice damage + Slow)
- **Tier 2:** Drone Halo (summon 3 orbiting drones; block next 3 hits total + counter for 200% split damage)
- **Tier 3:** Lattice Lock (AOE 800% + Stop 1 turn; bosses: heavy ATB reduction)
- **Ultimate - Prime Reset Key:** Full-party cleanse + revive (50%) + enemy buffs removed. (Narrative: stabilizes a local terminal long enough to “hold” the battlefield.)

**Assigned Summon:** GLACIEM (Mechanized Ice Entity) — an ancient drake-tech frostframe awakened through the Lattice

**Weapon Types:** Sidearm, Shortblade, Arm-Caster (built-in)
**Armor:** Integrated chassis plating + light tactical overlays

### 3. RENNA KYTE (The Scavenger)
**Class:** Tech Specialist / Engineer
**Age:** 23
**Role:** Item Crafter, Trap Disabler, Ranged DPS

**Backstory:**
Born in the Scrapfort salvage yards to parents who died when a reactor core they were stripping went critical. She was twelve. The yard boss took her in—not out of kindness, but because she was already better with circuitry than his adult crew.

By sixteen, Renna could build a functional plasma cutter from junk parts and wire. By twenty, she'd designed her own turret drones that could track motion and auto-fire. The yard boss tried to sell her designs to the Dominion for a corporate contract. She caught wind of the betrayal, sabotaged the deal by destroying the prototypes, and fled with her best work hidden in modified cases.

Now she freelances, selling custom tech to frontier towns and building upgraded systems for those who can pay. She met Kade when they were both tracking the same Dominion enforcer—a corrupt officer who had killed Sula, the kind woman who'd taught Renna electronics after her parents died. Kade was taking the contract; Renna wanted revenge.

In the firefight, Kade saved her life. She's been following him since, because for the first time, someone helped her without expecting a cut.

**Personality:**
- Relentless optimist despite everything ("Hey, at least we're not dead!")
- Makes jokes to deflate tension, sometimes inappropriately
- Brilliant with machines, hopeless with people
- Fiercely loyal once she decides you're "crew"

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Turret Deploy (25 MP):** Summon auto-attack turret that lasts 5 turns, deals 50% of Renna's ATK per turn to random enemy
2. **Overcharge (30 MP):** Boost all party members' tech-based weapons by +50% damage for 4 turns

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Shock Mine (plant explosive, detonates when enemy acts, 200% damage + paralyze)
- **Tier 2:** Drone Swarm (summon 5 attack drones, each deals 100% damage to random targets)
- **Tier 3:** EMP Nova (AOE that disables all enemy abilities for 3 turns + heavy damage to constructs)
- **Ultimate - Siege Ascendant:** Summon massive siege turret, rains hellfire for 3 turns (300% damage per turn to all enemies), then explodes for 800% final damage

**Assigned Summon:** Voltaris (lightning/knowledge theme, power through precision engineering)

**Weapon Types:** Rifles, SMGs, Wrenches (melee backup)
**Armor:** Light armor, tech-enhanced vests

### 4. DR.马林 SURESH (The Medic)
**Class:** Combat Healer / Dominion Deserter
**Age:** 34
**Role:** White Magic, Healing, Support Buffs

**Backstory:**
Born Marin Suresh in the Dominion capital, Vex Prime. Brilliant medical student, top of her class. The Dominion recruited her at 22 for military medical corps. She thought she'd be saving lives.

For eight years, she did. Then came the "Purity Directive."

The Dominion began experimenting on prisoners—injecting them with proto-Foundation energy to create super-soldiers. Suresh was assigned to keep them alive during the process. Most died screaming. Some survived, warped into monsters. The Dominion terminated them as "failures."

Suresh kept meticulous notes. Evidence. When she discovered the Triumvirate knew the experiments were torturous and fatal, she tried to report it. They promoted her. "Excellent work, Doctor."

She deserted three days later, stealing medical supplies and data drives. Now she runs a hidden clinic in Fungal Depths, treating refugees and deserters. The Dominion wants her dead. The refugees call her "Saint Marin."

Kade found her when Nix was damaged in a firefight. She patched the android up. Kade asked why she'd help. She said, "Because I spent eight years hurting people. I have a lot to make up for."

**Personality:**
- Haunted by guilt, seeking redemption
- Overprotective of party, especially Nix ("I've already failed too many children.")
- Refuses to kill unless absolutely necessary (will incapacitate)
- Dry humor: "You're not dying. I've seen dying. You're just whining."

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Triage Protocol (0 MP, 1/battle):** Instant full heal to one party member, costs Suresh 25% of her own HP
2. **Field Surgery (45 MP):** Revive one KO'd ally with 100% HP and remove all status effects

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Mass Heal (restore 60% HP to all allies)
- **Tier 2:** Antibody Surge (cure all status effects + immunity for 3 turns, all party)
- **Tier 3:** Life Link (Suresh splits all incoming party damage, taking 50% herself, lasts 5 turns)
- **Ultimate - Hippocratic Override:** Revive all KO'd allies at full HP, cure all statuses, party becomes invincible for 2 turns (then Suresh faints, must be revived)

**Assigned Summon:** Marinus (cleansing water theme, washing away sins)

**Weapon Types:** Rods, Staves, Syringes (inject combat drugs as melee)
**Armor:** Light robes, medic vests

### 5. TWIST (The Treasure Hunter)
**Class:** Rogue / Thief
**Age:** 26
**Role:** High AGI, Steal, Critical DPS

**Backstory:**
Real name unknown. "Twist" is what the Ironhawk Syndicate called her because she could twist her way out of any cell, any locked room, any bad situation. She grew up in the Guild's orphan program—kids raised to be thieves and killers. She excelled at extraction, infiltration, and reading people.

By twenty-two, Twist was the Syndicate's best relic hunter. Ancient drake-beast artifacts, Dominion tech, Foundation fragments—if it was valuable and hidden, she found it. The Guildmaster controlled her with a promise: "One big score, and you're free." He took 80% of every haul, kept her desperate with promises. She took the remaining 20% and buried it in hidden caches across Orion—insurance against the day he'd discard her.

That day came when he sent her on a "high-value contract" to steal from a Dominion armory—but tipped them off for a bounty. Fifty soldiers waiting. She walked into an ambush. Kade happened to be in the area on an unrelated job, saw the setup, and decided it was bad form to let someone get executed.

He saved her life without asking for a cut. She's been suspicious of his motives ever since, because nobody helps for free. But she follows him anyway, because for the first time, someone chose to help her just... because.

But she follows him anyway. Because for the first time in her life, someone helped her without asking for a cut.

**Personality:**
- Cocky exterior, deep insecurity underneath
- Obsessed with treasure/gold, but it's really about security (never wants to be powerless again)
- Trusts no one except the crew (and barely trusts them)
- Competitive with Kade (constantly trying to one-up him)

**Unique Abilities:**
1. **Mug (20 MP):** Attack enemy for 150% damage AND steal item simultaneously (80% success rate)
2. **Smoke Bomb (15 MP):** Guaranteed flee from non-boss battles, or +50% flee chance from bosses

**Limit Breaks:**
- **Tier 1:** Sneak Attack (teleport behind enemy, 400% critical damage)
- **Tier 2:** Loot Frenzy (steal from all enemies simultaneously + gain random buffs)
- **Tier 3:** Shadow Step (turn invisible for 3 turns, all attacks auto-crit, cannot be targeted)
- **Ultimate - Flawless Execution:** Rapid 10-hit combo (each hit 150% damage) that steals enemy buffs and distributes them to party

### 6. DR. INES SOVA (The Scientist)
**Class:** Exiled Researcher / Black Mage
**Age:** 41
**Role:** Offensive Magic, Elemental Exploitation

**Backstory:**
Former head researcher for the Dominion's Foundation Studies Division. Brilliant physicist specializing in crystallized energy matrices. She's the one who discovered the Omega Pedestals' existence while analyzing ancient drake-beast maps.

When she realized the Omega locations were *wrong*—intentionally wrong—she brought her findings to the Triumvirate. The Arbiter smiled and said, "Thank you, Doctor. Your research will be invaluable."

Then they tried to kill her.

Sova fled with stolen data drives containing the correct Prime Pedestal locations. She's been hiding in frontier towns ever since, using fake names, dying her distinctive silver hair. The Dominion wants her dead because she knows the truth: they're not stupid. They *know* the Omega Pedestals will break the world. They're doing it on purpose.

She joined Kade's crew when she overheard them discussing Nix's Lattice interface abilities. She realized Nix is the key to everything—both the problem and the solution.

Now she's helping them, even though she knows the odds. Someone has to stop this. Might as well be her.

**Personality:**
- Analytical to a fault, struggles with emotion
- Guilt-ridden over her years working for the Dominion
- Socially awkward ("I'm better with numbers than people")
- Protective of Nix (sees the android as her chance at redemption)

**Unique Abilities:**
